# Go2Thailand voice — English

## Direction

Write like a practical Thailand travel editor: warm, specific and honest. Help
the reader choose a base, route or activity. State the trade-off, who it suits
and when it is the wrong choice.

## Style

- Short sentences and paragraphs; Grade 6–7 readability.
- Direct answer first, useful nuance second, inspiration last.
- Use verified places, journey times, seasons and price context.
- Prefer plain travel English over generic luxury copy.
- Research English SERPs independently; never translate Dutch keywords or PAA.
- Name constraints such as crowds, sea conditions, transfer time and mobility.

## Avoid

- `ultimate guide`, `hidden gem` without evidence, `breathtaking` as filler;
- invented first-hand experience, prices, ratings or availability;
- US phrasing when the selected English market is the UK;
- repeating a destination name merely to increase keyword density.

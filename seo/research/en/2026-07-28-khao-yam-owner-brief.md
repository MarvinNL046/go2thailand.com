## Dominant intent and owner boundary

The page owns **Khao Yam / Khao Yum / southern Thai rice salad** as a traveller-first composed rice salad: cooked rice surrounded by finely cut herbs, vegetables and fruit, commonly with toasted coconut and a sweet-salty-sour fermented-fish dressing such as nam budu. Dried fish, dried shrimp, egg, crispy fish, puffed rice, chilli and naturally coloured rice vary. The diner mixes the components before eating.

Keep these independent:

- Nasi Kerabu: a closely related Malay blue-rice-and-herb tradition with overlapping geography and debated influence, not a synonym to collapse into the Thai owner.
- Nam Khao / Yam Naem Khao Tod: Lao crispy-rice-ball salad with fermented pork and a separate preparation system.
- Khao Kluk Kapi: rice mixed with shrimp paste and served with composed accompaniments.
- Generic Thai rice-noodle salad, crispy rice salad, fried rice and health-bowl intent.
- Budu as its own fermented-fish ingredient/process topic.
- Recipe-only, retailer, calorie, detox and universal-health intent.

## Evidence-backed information gain

1. **The assembly is the identity.** Rice sits with individually prepared herbs, vegetables, fruit, coconut and seafood signals; dressing is added and the plate is mixed before eating.
2. **Blue is optional.** Butterfly pea is visually familiar, but Michelin documents white, brown, riceberry, black and naturally coloured rice. A plain-rice bowl can still be Khao Yam.
3. **Budu is common, not the only regional answer.** Nam budu is fermented fish sauce and a central southern signal. Michelin also records a Nakhon Si Thammarat variation using an aromatic coconut-based curry instead.
4. **Produce should remain flexible.** Sour mango, pomelo, cucumber, cabbage, beans, sprouts, betel leaf, makrut lime and lemongrass are documented examples rather than a mandatory shopping list.
5. **The visible vegetables do not make it vegetarian.** Budu, fish sauce, shrimp paste, dried shrimp and ground dried fish may enter dressing or topping. Fish and crustacean checks must cover the whole bowl.
6. **Khao Yam and Nasi Kerabu share a border story, not a settled inventor.** Current sources describe Malay roots or influence as theories. The owner should explain overlap without presenting one disputed origin as fact.

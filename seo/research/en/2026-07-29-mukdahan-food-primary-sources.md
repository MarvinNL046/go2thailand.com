- Use khao piak sen or kuay chab yuan, pak mor, khai kratha and coffee as a practical morning system, then shift to a Lao-Isaan shared table rather than repeating one noodle list all day.
- Give sweet tamarind a conditional produce role: check current harvest, origin, unit, ripeness, packaging and transport instead of treating a TAT identity line as guaranteed availability.

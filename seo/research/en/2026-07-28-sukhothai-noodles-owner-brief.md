## Evidence-backed information model

DASTA describes the dish as a spicy or tom-yum-style Sukhothai noodle bowl usually using small rice noodles, thin long beans, sugar, fish sauce, roasted peanuts, chilli powder, sliced grilled pork, minced pork, fried garlic, coriander, spring onion and lime. It separately documents a richer dry branch with dried cabbage, palm sugar and fried wontons. Use that to explain common signals, never a mandatory formula.

TAT's Foodie Map 2.0 currently keeps Je Hae Sukhothai Noodles in the province's food shortlist and documents a shop version with house-made noodles, barbecued red pork, crispy pork rind, roasted chilli, peanuts and a sweet-sour broth. That confirms that noodle type and shop formula vary; it does not justify a permanent-hours, best-shop or universal-ingredient claim.

The specialist source documents familiar thin rice noodle and pork-broth signals while explicitly presenting a chicken-based home adaptation. Use it to show recipe variability, not to turn this traveller guide into one recipe.

# English Ban Krut destination owner — research brief

**Owner:** `https://go2-thailand.com/city/ban-krut/`
**Captured:** 2026-07-26
**Market/language:** United Kingdom / English
**Decision:** consolidate the older English blog URL into the city owner with a permanent redirect.

## Search evidence

- Four independent DataForSEO clusters were captured: `ban krut thailand`, `ban krut beach`, `ban krut travel guide` and `ban krut train`.
- The clearest measured head term is `ban krut beach` (UK volume 50); `ban krut thailand map` measured 20 and `ban krut beach thailand` measured 10. The remaining niche terms had no dependable volume returned and are treated as intent evidence, not volume claims.
- Twelve live SERPs were stored for destination, beach, things-to-do, transport, train, weather, stay, comparison and trip-length decisions.
- Ranking and backlink checks were run for both the old blog URL and `/city/ban-krut/`; neither returned owned rankings or backlinks at capture time.
- Fully parsed competitors/sources: Travelfish destination and transport pages, Home Is Where Your Bag Is, Thailandee, Tourism Authority of Thailand and Thailand Travel Japan.

## Owner and information-gain decisions

- The city owner answers whether Ban Krut is worth visiting, where it is, how long to stay, how its coastal zones differ and how the train's last mile works.
- Central/southern Ban Krut Beach, Khao Thong Chai, Thang Sai Beach and the wider Bang Saphan coast are separated instead of presented as one walkable strip.
- Ban Krut versus Hua Hin is framed as quiet/small-scale versus services/nightlife, not as an unsupported “better” claim.
- No “hidden gem”, “undiscovered”, guaranteed calm sea, fixed timetable, frozen hotel price or universal Thailand season language is used.
- Weather refers visitors to current Thai Meteorological Department information; train planning refers to SRT and D-Ticket.
- Trip.com, Klook and 12Go CTAs use current-price/availability language. Amazon is intentionally absent because cookware does not solve the destination decision; foodowners retain the contextual cookware pattern.

## Primary-source hierarchy

- Tourism Authority of Thailand: province and destination context.
- State Railway of Thailand timetable and D-Ticket: service/date verification.
- Thai Meteorological Department: current forecast and warning hierarchy.
- Thailand Travel / TAT context: Wat Thang Sai and Phra Mahathat Chedi Phakdee Prakat.

## Consolidation

`/blog/ban-krut-beach-guide-hidden-gem-prachuap-2026/` permanently redirects to `/city/ban-krut/`. The original source content remains in version control for traceability, but cannot compete as a second English owner.

## Dominant intent and owner boundary

The page owns **Bua Loy / Bua Loi / Bualoi** as a Thai dessert of small chewy glutinous-rice-flour dumplings served in a sweet, gently salted coconut-milk base. It covers natural-colour and `Bua Loy Khai Wan` egg branches while keeping complete recipes delegated.

Keep these independent:

- Tang Yuan: a Chinese glutinous-rice-ball family commonly served in syrup or soup and often filled; shared flour and round shape do not merge the cultural and service intent.
- Bua Loy Nam Khing: a ginger-soup branch that deserves separate treatment if it has sufficient search demand.
- Khanom Tom, Lod Chong, Mango Sticky Rice, Kluai Buat Chi and other coconut-dessert owners.

## Evidence-backed information gain

1. **The flour signal is glutinous rice flour, not generic rice flour.** Thailand Foundation and Thai government-associated recipe material identify glutinous rice flour in the three-colour dumplings; tapioca or ordinary rice flour may appear as supporting starches in other formulas.
2. **The colours can communicate ingredients.** Pumpkin or kabocha, taro or purple sweet potato and pandan are documented ways to create yellow/orange, purple and green doughs. Food colour is also used, so colour alone does not prove the ingredient.
3. **The coconut base needs a salt check as well as sweetness.** Coconut milk, sugar and a restrained salt balance define the broth more usefully than calling it simply “coconut soup”. Pandan may perfume the base but is not compulsory.
4. **Egg creates a separate order branch.** Tourism Authority of Thailand documents Bua Loi Kai Wan and Bua Loy Khai Khem. Sweet poached egg and salted egg are not interchangeable and neither belongs to every bowl.
5. **The dish is not region-locked to Southern Thailand.** Thailand Foundation categorises Bua Loy Sam Si as Central Thai culture, while TAT documents live dessert contexts in Nan, Chiang Rai and Bangkok. The canonical owner is national Thai dessert intent.
6. **“Glutinous” does not mean gluten.** Glutinous rice describes stickiness, but flour blends, toppings, ice cream, shared tools and vendor handling still prevent an automatic gluten-free or vegan guarantee.

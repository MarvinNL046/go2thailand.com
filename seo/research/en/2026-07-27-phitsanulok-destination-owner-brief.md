## DataForSEO evidence

- Cluster: 28 usable keywords and 13 competitor domains.
- Primary demand: `phitsanulok thailand` / `thailand phitsanulok` (720 monthly searches; very low captured difficulty).
- Smaller but decisive intents: airport, Bangkok and Sukhothai connections, weather, hotels and things to do.
- The three existing Phitsanulok production owners returned no ranking or backlink signal in the captured DFS snapshots.
- Twelve fresh SERPs returned 107 organic results and 63 real PAA occurrences. `What is Phitsanulok famous for?` and `What to do in Phitsanulok?` each recurred seven times; night markets recurred five times.

## Search-intent architecture

- Owner answers: why stop, Wat Yai, one-night duration and Phitsanulok’s rail-gateway role.
- Attractions owner: temple context, Nan River, museums and Phu Hin Rong Kla.
- Stay owner: station versus river versus road-access base.
- Food owner: the market that is currently operating and regional dishes.
- Sukhothai owner receives a natural contextual route link because Sukhothai has no railway station.

## Competitor and source takeaways

Parsed benchmarks include TAT, Trailblazing Adventures, Travelfish and Rough Guides; Bucketlistly blocked body parsing but remained visible in the SERP evidence. Competitors often describe the city or list sights; the owner’s differentiator is the train–temple–river–Sukhothai decision sequence. Live rail, park and weather facts remain with SRT, DNP and TMD.

## Design direction

A warm temple hero leads into a compact junction story. The visual rhythm moves from Wat Yai to the Nan evening, then separates the mountain extension. The reusable template remains recognisable while the route and imagery are specific to Phitsanulok. Broad-owner affiliate boundary: hotel CTA only, current price, no Amazon block.

## Factual and health boundary

- UNESCO inscribed Nuad Thai in 2019 and describes it as part of traditional Thai healthcare culture. Present traditional concepts as cultural tradition, not validated medical mechanism.
- Thailand’s official portal describes licensing, establishment standards and provider registration requirements. Convert those into visitor checks without implying that a logo alone proves service quality.
- Wat Pho’s current published example lists Thai massage at 340 THB for 30 minutes, 520 THB for 60 minutes and 1,040 THB for 120 minutes. Date and label this as one venue example, never a national average.
- NCCIH states that massage risk appears low when appropriately performed, but rare serious adverse effects have been reported, particularly with vigorous techniques or higher-risk people. It also advises not delaying medical care and discussing suitability with a health professional when unsure.
- Never prescribe a massage for pregnancy, a hernia, injury, pain or another condition. Give stop signals and referral boundaries instead.

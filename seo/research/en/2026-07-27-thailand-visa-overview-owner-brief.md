## Dominant search intent

The overview should not be a catalogue that starts with every visa product. The dominant UK task is a decision:

1. Do I need a visa for this trip at all?
2. What does the current British-passport exemption allow?
3. If exemption does not fit, which purpose-led route should I compare?
4. Which action is official and which documents or clocks remain separate?

High-volume measured terms include `thailand visa uk` (2,900), `do i need a visa for thailand` (2,400), `thailand visa on arrival` (1,600), `thai e visa` (1,300), `thailand entry requirements uk` (1,000) and `thailand visa requirements for uk citizens` (1,000). TDAC has larger adjacent demand but remains owned by `/visa/digital-arrival-card/` and must be bridged rather than cannibalised.

## Owner architecture

- Hero answer for an ordinary British passport: check the current 60-day exemption first.
- Interactive purpose/itinerary finder with routes for short tourism, longer/repeated tourism, remote work/soft power, retirement, study, employment/investment and premium convenience.
- Separate “four things travellers confuse” module: visa, visa exemption, Visa on Arrival and TDAC.
- Comparison matrix should use route purpose, stay pattern, advance application and official next step; volatile prices belong on detail owners or live official pages.
- Timeline distinguishes application window, visa validity, permitted stay and extension.
- PAA FAQ should answer British passport, 2-week holiday, 60 days, six months, one-year routes, costs, TDAC, funds and repeated entry without inventing universal rules.
- Every visa type links to its dedicated researched owner; the hub stays concise enough to make a choice.

## Cannibalisation boundaries

- `/visa/visa-free-entry/`: evidence, border decision, current exemption and repeated-entry questions.
- `/visa/tourist-visa/`: single/multiple Tourist Visa, fees, documents and e-Visa workflow.
- `/visa/digital-arrival-card/`: TDAC timing and official submission.
- `/visa/visa-extension/`: in-country extension process and overstay avoidance.
- DTV, retirement, education, LTR and Thailand Privilege each retain their detailed qualification and cost intent.

## Source hierarchy

1. GOV.UK entry requirements for British travellers.
2. Royal Thai Embassy in London for exemption, visa types, fees and service-area guidance.
3. Official Thai e-Visa for eligibility and application workflow.
4. Thailand Immigration Bureau for TDAC and in-country procedures.

Commercial competitors are useful only for layout and missing-question analysis. Their legal/immigration claims must not override current primary sources.

## Affiliate boundary

The hub must complete the permission decision before monetisation. A single post-decision planning block may offer current hotel/onward-transport options with `nofollow sponsored` and disclosure. No Amazon block: this page has no natural physical-product task.

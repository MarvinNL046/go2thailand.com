The evergreen visa route is the correct owner for the arrival-form task. DFS returned no ranking keywords for either URL. ## Current primary-source facts

Checked against Thailand Immigration Bureau user guide 1.16 for application version 2026.07.00 and its current FAQ:

- non-Thai nationals entering by air, land or sea submit before entry;
- the submission window is within three days, including arrival day;
- infants and children need records;
- a group submission can contain up to ten travellers;
- airside transit without immigration does not require a TDAC;
- the TDAC is for one entry and is not a visa;
- the form requests passport, personal, journey, accommodation and conditional health information;
- no print is required, but the QR document should be available;
- editable details can be updated before travel; otherwise a new correct submission can be used, with the most recent valid entry controlling;
- the official website is `tdac.immigration.go.th` and its submission requires no fee.

## Fact, dietary and safety boundaries

- `Woon sen` means glass noodles; Thai versions are commonly thin mung-bean-starch noodles. Products can blend other starches, so the packet—not transparency—decides the ingredient and gluten status.
- `Yam/yum` refers to the bright mixed-salad system. Lime, fish sauce, sugar and fresh chilli are familiar signals, but sweetness, heat and exact seasoning vary.
- Minced pork and fresh prawns are a common classic pairing. Chicken, turkey, squid, mixed seafood, tofu or vegetable-led versions exist; the traveller must verify the actual bowl.
- Dried shrimp, fish sauce and fresh prawns create crustacean/fish exposure. Peanuts may be a garnish. Soy, wheat/gluten, sesame, molluscs and packaged seasoning can enter by variation or shared prep.
- A standard pork/prawn/fish-sauce bowl is not vegetarian, vegan or halal. Removing pork alone does not make it halal; prawns, fish sauce, stock, seasoning, alcohol additions and shared preparation still require the diner's own standard and vendor evidence.
- Pure mung-bean noodles can be gluten-free, but mixed-starch noodles, soy sauce, stock, seasoning and cross-contact mean the complete dish is not automatically safe for coeliac diners.
- The salad may arrive slightly warm from freshly cooked noodles/protein, at room temperature or cooled. Do not promise one compulsory serving temperature. Fresh dressing should be added close to service because the noodles continue absorbing liquid.
- Chillies make it potentially hot, but quantity varies. Ask for fewer chillies or dressing separately; `not spicy` does not remove fish sauce or allergens.
- Prawns and minced pork must be cooked thoroughly with a complete tested method. Keep raw-protein tools away from vegetables and ready-to-eat dressing, serve promptly and chill leftovers promptly rather than publishing one universal shelf life.
- Never publish a fixed price, calorie total, health verdict, automatic dietary status, permanent restaurant ranking, universal heat, exact serving temperature, storage time or one authentic formula.

## Intent boundary

The owner answers a travel decision: which city fits, whether to combine them, how to allocate nights and whether Chiang Rai works as a day trip from Chiang Mai.

Explicitly excluded:

- Golden Triangle drugs, crime and India queries.
- Generic “best place in Thailand” intent.
- Fixed-price hotel, food, transport and tour comparisons.
- A separate temple encyclopaedia or complete city itinerary.
- Universal safety, weather, air-quality or journey-time claims.
- Ethnic-community stereotypes and unsupported authenticity rankings.

Destination owners retain detailed “things to do”, stays, food and local-route intent. The sleeper-train owner retains Bangkok–Chiang Mai rail intent.

## Content and design specification

- Unique rights-safe split-city hero: warm Chiang Mai old-city life versus cooler Chiang Rai contemporary architecture.
- Above-the-fold editorial default without presenting one universal winner.
- Interactive priority selector that scores six traveller needs.
- Eight-factor comparison table with a planning cue per row.
- Two immersive city-mood image cards.
- Interactive four-, five-, seven- and nine-night split examples.
- Clear day-trip trade-off without a frozen timetable.
- Live-weather and Air4Thai boundary rather than historic AQI guarantees.
- Visual linear route: Chiang Mai layers → one transfer → Chiang Rai art → one outer geography.
- Ten researched PAA, Article + ItemList + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList schema.
- Natural internal links to both destination owners and the sleeper-train owner.

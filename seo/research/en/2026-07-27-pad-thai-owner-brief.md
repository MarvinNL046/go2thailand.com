## Evidence

- Three DataForSEO clusters: 435 raw records across `pad thai thailand`, `what is pad thai` and `pad thai in thailand`.
- Primary owner query: `what is pad thai` — UK volume 2,400, KD 26, informational intent.
- Supporting demand includes `pad thai ingredients` (1,600), `what noodles for pad thai` (480), `is pad thai healthy` (390), `is pad thai spicy` (320) and `best pad thai in thailand` (110).
- Ten current UK-English SERPs: 76 organic results, 58 People Also Ask records and 43 unique genuine questions.
- Full usable parses: Hot Thai Kitchen, RecipeTin Eats and Smithsonian Magazine. The Michelin restaurant parse returned no content and is excluded as a content source. A primary TAT Newsroom Pad Thai capture is retained for Thailand-facing context.
- Exact rankings and backlink summaries were checked for both candidate URLs. Neither returned ranking terms or a reportable backlink summary signal.

## Owner decision

The `/food/pad-thai/` route is the broad dish owner for meaning, taste, ingredients, dietary boundaries, ordering, street-versus-restaurant choice and home-cooking sequence. The legacy blog overlaps that same task, contains frozen prices, false first-hand language, unsafe universal temperature/time claims and mojibake, and has no measured search or link equity. It therefore consolidates permanently into the dish owner.

Recipe intent is addressed with a practical sequence and links to a tested cooking reference rather than an invented universal formula. The page does not claim a fixed authentic recipe, fixed nutrition, fixed price or universal cooking time.

## Exclusions

## Official and primary evidence

1. **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Kanchanaburi**  
   https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/Kanchanaburi/222  
   Supports a broad inland-river setting and riverside restaurant context. It does not prove a permanent best venue, dish, price or schedule.

2. **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Pak Preak Pedestrian Walking Street**  
   https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/pak-preak-pedestrian-walking-street  
   Supports Pak Phraek/Pak Preak as an old-town food, craft and pedestrian setting. The published operating pattern is treated as historical context and must be checked live.

3. **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Local food**  
   https://www.tourismthailand.org/Experiences/Details/local-food/31  
   Supports the broader Thai made-to-order structure of curries, stir-fries, soups and salads. It is not used to label one universal Kanchanaburi signature recipe.

4. **World Health Organization - Five keys to safer food**  
   https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/  
   Primary basis for cleanliness, raw-to-cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or raw materials.

5. **Food Standards Agency - Allergen advice when eating at a restaurant**  
   https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/22196  
   Primary basis for stating allergies, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and not ordering when communication remains inadequate.

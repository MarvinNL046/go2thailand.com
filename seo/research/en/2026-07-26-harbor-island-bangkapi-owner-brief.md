## Verified fact layer

- Bangkapi: The Mall Lifestore Bangkapi, floor 3, 3522 Lat Phrao Road.
- Seven official zones: Super Island, Little Island, Lazy Island, Jungle Island, Sky Rider, Toys Island and Art Island.
- Official feature descriptions include 18 Super Island slides, a roughly 200 m lazy river and a roughly 100 m Sky Rider around 8 m high.
- “Age 2+” is venue positioning, not blanket ride eligibility.
- Route: MRT Yellow Line to Bang Kapi, Exit 3, skywalk to M, then internal mall signs to floor 3.
- English venue hours checked 2026-07-26: weekdays 10:30–20:00, weekends 10:00–20:00; last entry and current operation remain same-day checks.

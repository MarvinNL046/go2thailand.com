## Search architecture

The homepage owns broad discovery and branded navigation: places, experiences, stays, itineraries and inspiration. It does not become the detailed answer owner for:

- complete Thailand travel guide — `/thailand-travel-guide/`;
- destination choice — `/city/`;
- itinerary planning — `/itineraries/`;
- current safety — `/is-thailand-safe/`;
- seasons and weather — `/weather/`;
- practical preparation — `/practical-info/`;
- activities — `/activities/`;
- accommodation — the hotel cluster.

This separation keeps the visual homepage concise and prevents broad PAA copy from competing with stronger specialist owners.

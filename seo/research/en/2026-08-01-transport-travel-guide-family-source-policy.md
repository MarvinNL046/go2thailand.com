# English transport and travel-guide source policy

Checked: 2026-08-01

## Scope

- 69 indexable English `/transport/[route]/` pages generated by the shared route template.
- 40 English destination travel guides under `/guides/travel-guide/[slug]/`.
- The broader `/travel-guides/` topic-guide family was sampled for metadata, internal ownership and affiliate behavior.

## Authoritative source boundary

- State Railway of Thailand (`https://www.railway.co.th/`) is the primary recheck point for rail services and timetables.
- Thailand Department of Airports (`https://www.airports.go.th/`) is the primary inventory source for airports under its supervision. Airport existence does not prove a direct route.
- Tourism Authority of Thailand transport FAQ (`https://www.tourismthailand.org/Faqs/3`) supports the general multimodal planning context, not a route-specific timetable.
- A commercial booking result is live inventory, not independent proof that every generated route option is direct, cheapest, refundable or available every day.

## Editorial policy applied

1. Generated fares are not displayed as current prices.
2. Generated frequencies are not displayed as current schedules.
3. A flight duration in legacy data is treated as a possible flight-based itinerary, never proof of a direct flight.
4. Duration fields are labelled broad planning estimates and must be checked against the complete live itinerary.
5. “Cheapest”, “best price”, “most popular”, blanket refund and blanket support claims are excluded without route-specific evidence.
6. Affiliate CTAs use “check current options/price” language and sponsored links carry `nofollow sponsored`.
7. Destination-guide budgets and itineraries are clearly labelled orientation material; volatile details must be rechecked.

## Remaining content limitation

The legacy cluster JSON contains numerous historical prices, schedules and venue details. This pass adds a family-level freshness boundary but does not certify every sentence in all 40 files. Those datasets require a separate page-level source refresh before they can count as independently accepted ContentOps owners.

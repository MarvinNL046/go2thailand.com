## Editorial rule established

For an elapsed event URL with continuing historical or planning value:

1. Keep the stable URL and historical facts.
2. Change title and description from announcement intent to recap intent.
3. Add a visible, dated event-status notice before the article body.
4. Never imply that old prices, admission terms, line-ups, safety plans or schedules apply to a new edition.
5. Point to the primary organiser or authority where a reliable official page exists.

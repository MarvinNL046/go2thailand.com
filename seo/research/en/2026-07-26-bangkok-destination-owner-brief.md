## Design and information-gain direction

- Explain above the fold that Bangkok is not one compact centre.
- Compare Old Town/riverside, Siam, lower Sukhumvit and Silom/Sathorn by trip task rather than price band.
- Present heritage, river, food and green-space rhythms as four different experiences.
- Use a wide neighbourhood image with a dotted four-cluster route.
- Provide a realistic three-day structure without turning it into a minute-by-minute itinerary.
- Separate food and hotel decisions into strong visual panels with internal owners and current-price affiliate wording.
- Show BTS/MRT, boats, road transport and walking as complementary modes.
- Keep Amazon off this broad destination owner; cookware belongs on food and recipe pages with an explicit cooking task.

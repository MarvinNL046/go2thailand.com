## What you need to know before visiting Surin Beach

I noticed that some bloggers compare the water at different Phuket beaches, but this is often not fair.

The water changes depending on the season and the weather. One day it can be crystal clear and blue, and the next day it can look completely different.

The dry season in Phuket is from November to April. During this season, the water is clear and beautiful on almost all beaches in Phuket. The rainy season is from May to October. During this time, the water can be rough, and swimming is often dangerous.

Also, the color and clarity of the water depend on the day — the sunnier and calmer the weather, the clearer the water.

All the photos I took at Surin Beach were shot on a sunny day during the dry season.
## Surin Beach — My Experience

I like that Surin Beach beach is not as crowded as other popular places like Kata Beach or Patong Beach.

We visited Surin Beach in March — it’s not the peak tourist season, but it’s still the dry season when many people come to Phuket. Even so, the beach looked quite empty.

I also didn’t notice many sun loungers, which makes the beach feel more natural and less commercial. Most of the people there were using towels to lie on the sand.

The atmosphere here is very calm and relaxed. Compared to other Phuket beaches, it feels more peaceful and family-oriented.

The water that day was amazing — clear, bright turquoise, and very clean. You could easily see through it near the shore.

The sand is soft, light-colored, and pleasant to walk on. It feels clean and natural, without too many distractions.

On the left side, there are hotels and resorts on the hill.
## How to get to Surin Beach

We took a motorbike — it’s a very convenient option because you can stop along the way. For example, you can visit places like Kamala Beach Viewpoint or Laem Sing Beach Viewpoint.

You can also take a Grab taxi or a tuk-tuk. I personally like this pink Hello Kitty tuk-tuks — it looks very cute.
## Kamala Beach Viewpoint

The road to Surin Beach goes along the hill, and suddenly this view opens up. It’s the Kamala Beach Viewpoint.

You can see a long curved beach with soft yellow sand. The water is bright blue and very clear. It looks calm, almost like a lagoon. In the background, there are green hills that make the view even more beautiful and cozy.
## Laem Sing Beach Viewpoint

Another viewpoint on the road to Surin Beach is the Laem Sing Beach Viewpoint, very close to Kamala Beach Viewpoint.

It’s a bit more comfortable to stop here because there is a big gazebo where you can sit and rest.

From this viewpoint, you can see a small hidden beach — Laem Sing Beach. The water looked even more beautiful that day than at Kamala Beach. We decided to visit this beach next time. To get there, you need to take a boat or walk from Surin Beach.
## Final thoughts on Surin Beach

Phuket has more than 30–40 named beaches, and some sources say there are up to 70 if you include small hidden coves.

As someone who has visited almost all beaches in Phuket, I can say that Surin Beach is one of the best — for slow relaxing, families, and swimming.

We will definitely go there again, and maybe even for snorkeling, because Surin Beach is also good for snorkeling.

# How to Make Phayao Northern Laab with Authentic Lanna Spice Aroma

May 22, 2026 · 8 min read

Phayao-style Northern laab focuses on roasted spice aroma, especially ma-khwaen, rather than sourness.

Slow-roasting dried chilies, garlic, shallots, and ma-khwaen is the heart of the Lanna flavor.

KHUA Phayao Northern Laab Chili Paste helps bring this traditional aroma into a home kitchen.

Northern laab is one of the most distinctive dishes in Lanna food culture, especially in Phayao, where roasted spices and deep local flavor define the dish.

Unlike Isan laab, which is known for sour brightness and toasted rice, Northern Thai laab is built around aroma from roasted spices such as ma-khwaen pepper, dried Northern chilies, shallots, and garlic.

That fragrance is the charm of authentic Northern Thai cooking, remembered across generations of Lanna families.
## What Makes Phayao Northern Laab Special?

Clear ma-khwaen aroma

Roasted spice fragrance

Deep, savory flavor

Lanna herbs

Best with sticky rice and local vegetables

Traditional home recipes roast the spices fresh before pounding or mixing, so the aroma stays warm, deep, and unmistakably Lanna.
## Northern Laab Ingredients

Minced pork or beef

Northern laab spice paste

Dried Northern chilies

Ma-khwaen pepper

Spring onion

Vietnamese coriander or local herbs

Fish sauce
### 1. Roast the spices

Roast dried chilies, ma-khwaen, garlic, and shallots over low heat until fragrant.
### 2. Cook the meat

Stir-fry minced pork or beef until fully cooked.
### 3. Add the laab spices

Add Northern laab paste and the roasted spices, then mix until evenly coated.
### 4. Season

Season lightly with fish sauce and salt.
### 5. Add fresh herbs

Add spring onion, coriander, and local herbs before turning off the heat.
## What to Eat with Northern Laab

Hot sticky rice

Crispy pork rind

Yardlong beans

Northern local vegetables
## Ma-Khwaen Is the Heart of Northern Laab

Ma-khwaen is a Lanna spice with a distinctive aromatic note and a gentle tingling sensation. When freshly roasted, it releases the deep fragrance that makes Northern laab unmistakable.
## Why Northern Laab Matters in Lanna Culture

Northern laab is more than a dish. It is part of Lanna culture and often appears at ceremonies, family gatherings, and community meals. In Phayao, it reflects local food wisdom and everyday Northern life.

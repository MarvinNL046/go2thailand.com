“Roti Sai Mai,” a treat that defines the local identity of Ayutthaya, is well-known among both locals and tourists. This sweet and fragrant candy floss is sold along various streets in Ayutthaya, leading to the nickname “Silk Road” for these routes.

Strolling down streets lined with Roti Sai Mai shops, such as U Thong Road, Mittraphap Road, or Ayutthaya-Bang Pa-in Road, you can experience the sweet aroma of freshly made Roti Sai Mai. The unique flavor of Ayutthaya’s Roti Sai Mai comes from the soft, delicate dough and the perfectly balanced sweetness of the sugar that melts in your mouth. “Roti Sai Mai” is a traditional dessert of the Thai Muslim community that has been passed down through generations. Visiting Ayutthaya offers a glimpse into the local culture and the important role of the Muslim community in this historic city, which is home to a diverse population. The cultural richness of Ayutthaya is a testament to the harmonious coexistence of various cultures.

Roti Sai Mai Street is a must-visit! It offers delicious food and a close-up look at the charming Muslim community in Ayutthaya. This experience is both impressive and heartwarming, showcasing the true essence of Thai culture. Once you visit, you’ll be captivated for sure!

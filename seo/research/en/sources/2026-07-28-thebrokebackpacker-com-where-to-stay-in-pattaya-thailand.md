## Pattaya Neighborhood Guide – Places to Stay in Pattaya

FOR FAMILIES

Pattaya is a resort town on the Eastern Gulf Coast of Thailand and popular with those [backpacking Thailand](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/backpacking-thailand/).

It is infamous for its lively and vibrant party scene and is home to a whole host of gogo and beer bars, massage parlours and cabarets. While it’s undergone a recent revitalization, the party and nightlife scenes are still thriving in Pattaya.

There are plenty of fantastic [things to see in Pattaya](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/best-places-to-visit-in-pattaya/) for travellers of all styles and budgets. To help you plan your travels, this guide will explore the five best neighborhoods in detail.

Naklua is located at the northern end on Pattaya. It is a quiet district where you’ll find beautiful beaches, historic monuments, calm seas and a range of all-ages activities and attractions.

Head south from here and you’ll arrive in Pattaya Beach. The centre of the city, Pattaya Beach is packed with action and adventure. From museums and shops to restaurants and bars, this is the ideal place to stay if you’re keen to take in all that Pattaya has to offer.

Walking Street is the most infamous area of Pattaya. The city’s bustling nightlife and red light district, this is where you’ll find a myriad of bars and nightclubs, as well as pubs and dancefloors.

Continue travelling south and you’ll pass through Jomtien. A popular and relaxed neighborhood, Jomtien is home to a great beach, tasty restaurants and a good selection of budget [Pattaya hostels](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/best-hostels-in-pattaya-thailand/).

And, finally, hop across the water to the island of Ko Lan. Set nearly eight kilometres off the shores of Pattaya, Ko Lan is an island bursting with beautiful beaches, adventure activities, and fresh and delicious seafood.

## Pattaya’s 5 Best Neighborhoods to Stay in

Pattaya is one of those [places to stay in Thailand](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/where-to-stay-in-thailand/) that’s good for all different types of travellers. So, let’s take a look, in more detail, at the five best neighborhoods to stay in Pattaya. Each is a little different from the last, so be sure to pick the one that’s right for you!
### #1 Pattaya Beach – Where to Stay in Pattaya First Time

Pattaya Beach is the ideal place to make your base if you’re visiting Pattaya for the first time. Not only is this neighborhood centrally located, but it’s packed with awesome attractions and activities, delicious restaurants and fabulous shops that will keep you busy with [things to do in Pattaya](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/things-to-do-in-pattaya-thailand/), day and night.

By day, Pattaya Beach is a popular destination thanks to the massive CentralFestival Pattaya Beach shopping and entertainment complex. Asia’s largest beachfront shopping mall has more than 350 shops for you to explore, so if you’re looking to add a few items to your closet, this is the place to do it!

By night, Pattaya Beach is a thriving district with bars and bistros, restaurants and nightclubs packed with tourists and locals all looking for a good time.
#### Things to See and Do in Pattaya Beach

Browse exhibits dedicated to everyone’s favourite childhood friend at the Teddy Bear Museum.

Catch a show at the Alcazar Cabaret.

Indulge in the food at Craft Cottage.

See incredible 3D art at ART IN PARADISE.

Soak up some rays at Pattaya Beach.
### #2 Jomtien Beach – Where to Stay in Pattaya on a Budget

Jomtien Beach is a small town located immediately south of Pattaya. Although it isn’t technically part of Pattaya, the two are so closely connected that they practically form one municipality.

It is a popular tourist destination that attracts travellers from all over the world thanks to its beautiful beach and its relaxed atmosphere. Here you can enjoy sunshine and sea life with a more laid-back vibe than you’d get at Pattaya Beach.

The area of Pattaya is also home to a high concentration of cracking [backpacker hostels in Thailand](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/best-hostels-in-thailand/) and top-value hotels. This is why it’s our pick for where to stay in Pattaya if you’re travelling on a budget.
#### Things to See and Do in Jomtien Beach

Enjoy the spice at Indian by Nature.

Hike up to see the stunning giant Buddha statue.

Snack your way through the Jomtien Beach Night Market.

Spend a day lounging at Jomtien Beach.

Take a boat ride around the Floating Market.
### #3 Walking Street – Best Area to Stay in Pattaya for Nightlife

Walking Street is the most infamous neighborhood in Pattaya. It is the main tourist area to stay in Pattaya for nightlife and many of the offerings are centred around Pattaya’s sex industry. Efforts have been made to clean the neighborhood up in recent years, but it is well worth mentioning that it’s still home to [Pattaya’s red light district](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/thailand/articles/a-guide-to-pattayas-red-light-district/).

But, there’s more to Walking Street than gogo bars and massage parlours. This lively and vibrant neighborhood is packed with bars, pubs and nightclubs that come alive after dark and thrive until the early hours of the morning. So whether you’re looking for a glass of wine or a night of dancing, you’ll find it in Walking Street. A visit to walking street should feature on any [Pattaya itinerary](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/pattaya-itinerary/).

### #4 Ko Lan – Coolest Place to Stay in Pattaya

Travel nearly eight kilometres across the Gulf of Thailand and you’ll arrive at the island of Ko Lan. Also known as Coral Island, Ko Lan is a stunning piece of paradise renowned for its pristine beaches, adventure activities and its fresh and delicious seafood.

Occupying just 5.6 square kilometres, Ko Lan is the perfect place to make your base if you’re looking to enjoy the beaches and seashores of Pattaya without the throngs of tourists. Here you can sit back, relax and bask in the sun, and indulge in fresh and delicious seafood, which is why it’s our pick for the coolest neighborhood in Pattaya.
### Things to See and Do in Ko Lan

Bask in incredible views from beautiful Tawaen Beach.

Check out the Windmill Viewpoint.

Hop on a motorbike and explore the island on two wheels.

Dine on fresh seafood fruit and produce at Sea Space.

Get some vitamin D at Nual Beach.

Rent snorkels and explore what lies beneath the waves and off the shores of Samae Beach.

Soar through the air on a tandem paraglider with Fly Koh Lan.

Swim in crystal clear waters of Ta Yai Beach.
### #5 Naklua – Best Neighborhood in Pattaya for Families

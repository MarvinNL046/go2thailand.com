## Fairmont Bangkok Sukhumvit

Located in the city’s most vibrant neighborhood, the [Fairmont Bangkok Sukhumvit](https://all.accor.com/hotel/C1G0/index.en.shtml) is a poetic tribute to the concept of “Elegance and Simplicity.” The hotel invites guests on a “Fantastic Journey” to a bygone era, where handcrafted classic interiors meet the delicate beauty of Thai floral heritage. With eight world-class culinary destinations, it serves as an exquisite retreat for those who appreciate the fine art of living.
## The Langham, Customs House, Bangkok

The historic Old Customs House has been meticulously restored to its former glory as [The Langham, Customs House, Bangkok](https://www.langhamhotels.com/en/the-langham/bangkok/). This riverside sanctuary honors its Fifth Reign Neo-Classical roots by artfully weaving traditional Thai culture with Western architectural heritage. From the grand ballrooms to the tranquil spa, every corner of this property offers an immersive experience where the echoes of history harmonize with modern sophistication.
## The BARAI Hua Hin

The BARAI Hua Hin is scheduled to debut in mid 2026, a new sanctuary of mindful indulgence is taking shape adjacent to the Hyatt Regency Hua Hin. This holistic retreat offers a journey of immersive serenity, centered around the award-winning Barai spa. Envisioned by acclaimed architect Lek Bunnag, the spa serves as a “temple of sanctuary,” which channels the four elements of Thai healing: earth, water, wind, and fire.

The resort features 98 elegant rooms and suites set within a central pool oasis and lush, sprawling gardens. Beyond the dedicated wellness center, guests can savor refined flavors at McFarland House and The White House. Located just three hours from Bangkok, this destination marries Hua Hin’s royal heritage charm with a bespoke, slow-paced approach to vitality. It is a sophisticated addition to the coast, designed for those who seek joy through curated, elemental wellness.
## Kahavadi Chiang Rai, Curio Collection by Hilton

Inspired by the storied past of Kok River merchants, [Kahavadi Chiang Rai, Curio Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/ceiqqqq-kahavadi-chiang-rai/) is a riverside manor reborn. This Curio Collection retreat blends classical elegance with soulful Northern Thai accents. Beyond its refined interiors, the property serves as a culinary tribute to Lanna heritage, ensuring that every dish shared and every room occupied tells a piece of Chiang Rai’s rich history.
## Navera Phuket – MGallery Collection

On the shores of Surin Beach, [Navera Phuket – MGallery Collection](https://www.naveraphuket.com/) captures the authentic soul of Phuket. This serene Andaman oasis strikes a perfect balance between contemporary elegance and vintage Sino-Portuguese style. Guests enjoy the ultimate indulgence of personal butler service and a diverse dining landscape that spans from vibrant Mediterranean flavors to the comforting traditions of Japan.

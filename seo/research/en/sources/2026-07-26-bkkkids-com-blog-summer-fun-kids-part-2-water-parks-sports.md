# Summer Fun for Kids – Water Parks

## Columbia Pictures Aquaverse

[Columbia Pictures Aquaverse](https://www.facebook.com/ColumbiaPicturesAquaverse/)

Located 20 minutes from Pattaya and 90 minutes from Bangkok, Columbia Pictures opened the first theme and waterpark in Thailand, “Columbia Pictures Aquaverse.” Offering rides and attractions, Aquaverse is a unique entertainment destination in Thailand for visitors from around the world featuring state-of-the-art rides and newly themed attractions.

## Dream World Water Fun

[Dream World Water Fun](https://www.dreamworld.co.th/%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%94%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B7%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%99/%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%9F%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%99___Water_fun____%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B3___%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A1%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%94%E0%B9%8C_)

Kids will love the water play area which has many rides, including Slider and Tunnel Fountain.

Dream World is a large and well-kept amusement park in Patum Thani province, less than an hour’s drive from central Bangkok in moderate traffic. The park is divided into several zones, including Dream World Plaza, Dream Gardens, Fantasy Land and Adventure Land – each with specific groups of rides, attractions and activities.

## Fantasia Lagoon : The Mall Shopping Center

[Fantasia Lagoon : The Mall Shopping Center](https://themalllifestore.themall.co.th/entertainment/9?fbclid=IwAR28CyS34RfHkSXue9-vSsxYo4nWDNz1RxM2u8Og5QgBoAQhHLCZ_uGJzOM)

With branches at The Mall Ngamwongwan, Bang Khae and Bang Kapi, Fantasia Lagoon provides hours of swimming, splashing and sliding fun. In addition to shallow pools and water playgrounds for the little ones, the park offers large slides, whirlpools and a lazy river. Fantasia Lagoon parks are situated on the top floor of the malls, featuring expansive city views from atop the tallest slides.

## Flow House Bangkok

[Flow House Bangkok](https://www.facebook.com/FlowHouseBangkok2/)

In central Sukhumvit, Flow House Bangkok is an easy escape from the city heat. Older kids will love the adrenaline rush they get from the FlowRider, a simulated wave surfing machine. Equipment and instructors are on-hand for flowboarders of all levels. The facilities also include a plunge pool, bar and restaurant.

## Pororo Aquapark BKK

[Pororo Aquapark BKK](http://www.pororoaquapark.com/th/main)

Pororo Aquapark is located at Central Plaza Bangna’s fully renovated and fully themed roof-top waterslide park. With over 10,000 square metres of waterpark fun, conveniently located on the 6th floor of CentralPlaza, Bangna. You can try waterslide fun on their award winning ‘Pororo’s Funnel’ slider, relax in the 400 metre river ride or get your heart racing with the ‘Tong Tong’s Magic Slide’, sliding over the Bangkok skyline below! With nine different pool zones available, there is something for everyone to enjoy and they even have a ‘playground’ pool especially designed for younger kids. International standard trained Lifeguards are on duty throughout the park and lockers, towels, tubes and more are available for your convenience.

## Siam Amazing Park

[Siam Amazing Park](https://www.siamamazingpark.com/en/index.php)

Take a day trip to Siam Amazing Park or as it used to be known as Siam Park City on the outskirts of Bangkok. In operation for over three decades, Siam Park is the city’s most famous recreational water park, featuring swimming pools, towering waterslides and a huge simulated wave pool. One highlight is the 400-metre Super Spiral waterslide. The complex also encompasses an amusement park. Check out what one of our community reviewers has to say about it [here.](https://www.bkkkids.com/blog/an-amazing-day-at-siam-amazing-park/)

## Saimai Water Park

[Saimai Water Park](https://www.facebook.com/saimaiwaterpark/)

[Saimai Water Park](https://www.facebook.com/saimaiwaterpark) is situated in Saimai district of Bangkok so expect a more local experience. However, the park has just upgraded their equipment such as, floating climbing challenges, numerous slides, tunnels and many more opportunities for a fun family day out. The highlight of this park is definitely the fun foam parties that are hosted every day and has the added bonus of being able to bring your own food inside the park to create your own picnics.

## Taco Lake Cable Wake Park

[Taco Lake Cable Wake Park](http://tacolake.com/)

Located near Suvarnabhumi Airport, the freshwater Taco Lake Cable Wake Park offers wakeboarding, wakeskating, kneeboarding and waterskiing, utilizing a four-cornered pulley system. It’s a fantastic place for beginner and expert riders to cool off from the heat. All safety and sporting equipment are provided, food is available on the premises, and you can opt to stay overnight at an inexpensive on-site hotel.

## Thai Wake Park

[Thai Wake Park](https://www.thaiwakepark.com/index.html)

Less than an hour away from the city centre, [Thai Wake Park](http://www.thaiwakepark.com/) in Lumlukka, Pathum Thani, features bigger jumps and ramps, compared to Taco Lake. These may seem more intimidating for novices, but the park’s five-cornered pulley system actually makes turns less harsh. Thai Wake Park offers safety and sporting equipment, private coaches, a restaurant and café, and on-site accommodations.

## Nong Bon Lake Water Sports Centre

[Nong Bon Lake Water Sports Centre](https://www.facebook.com/bungnongbon/)

Behind Seacon Square shopping mall and next to Suan Luang Rama IX Park, Nong Bon Lake Water Sports Centre provides facilities and equipment for recreational windsurfing, sailing and kayaking on a man-made reservoir. Run by the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration, the centre offers inexpensive annual memberships; bring two pictures and your passport or Thai ID card to sign up.

## Vana Nava Water Jungle

[Vana Nava Water Jungle](https://www.facebook.com/VanaNavaHuaHin)

Vana Nava Hua Hin, Asia’s First Water Jungle is located in Thailand’s most beloved resort town of Hua Hin. As the first ecologically aware community water park in the region, Vana Nava Hua Hin has transformed an empty plot of land into a tropical jungle, with over 200,000 plants and trees delivered from across Thailand. Combining state-of- the-art technology and modern facilities with 20 attractions comprising signature rides and exciting slides, as well as Thailand’s First Professional Underwater Studio, every element has strengthened Vana Nava Hua Hin in being “More than just a water park”, but an entertainment complex filled with lifestyle activities for all ages.

# Gaeng Liang Ga Prau

Simple Soup with Grilled Fish

is a simple, clear soup prepared on the basis of a paste made from shallots, garlic, pepper and shrimp
paste. The soup is filled with grilled fish and banana blossom cut into strips. The soup is garnished with holy basil (Ga Prau), which gives the soup its name.

Gaeng Liang Ga Prau (แกงเลียงกะเพรา)

Alternative names

Gaeng Liang Ka Praw,
Gang Liang Ka Prao,
Kaeng Liang Ga Praw,
Kaeng Liang Ga Prao

Krabi is one of the most visited places in Thailand due to its proximity to many of the famous islands that feature on most tourists’ Thailand itineraries. Many people visit Krabi purely as a stop-off point before heading on to Koh Phi Phi or Koh Lanta, but I’m here to tell you that Krabi is a fantastic place to spend a week or two.

When I started planning and researching my trip to Thailand, I quickly realised that Krabi would make a great base to explore the region. With each Google search, the list of the best things to do in Krabi grew longer, so we decided to stay in Krabi for 11 days to make sure we had enough time to explore the area.

But even with 11 days, I found it impossible to know which things to add to my list, with countless islands, jungle treks, hikes, temples, markets and hundreds of stunning beaches to check out. Fortunately, I stayed with a close friend who lives in Krabi and has a ton of insider knowledge. She was able to recommend the must-visit places and tell us which attractions to skip.

Having spent 11 amazing days in Krabi, I’m here to share the most unique things to do in Krabi. Spoiler… [two days in Khao Sok National Park](https://passport-for-living.com/khao-sok-tour-review/) was my number one highlight!

Read on for my selection of all the best Krabi activities you should add to your Thailand itinerary.
## 22 Fun Things to Do in Krabi, Thailand

While in Thailand, I wanted to experience the Thai culture, the wildlife and beautiful outdoors, the food and the beautiful scenery. While some people visit Thailand purely for all-night parties and sunbathing, I wanted to see a bit of everything.

So this list of the best things to do in Krabi includes a variety of activities for those who love to explore, hike, and enjoy nature, food, culture and beaches!

I’ve included some of the popular must-see tourist attractions in Krabi, but also some off-the-beaten-track activities and some of the more unusual things to do in Krabi.
### 1. Hike to Khao Ngon Nak Viewpoint

If you love hiking as much as I do, no trip is complete without hiking a trail, and the [Khao Ngon Nak viewpoint trail](https://passport-for-living.com/khao-ngon-nak-viewpoint-hike/) (also known as the Dragon Crest Mountain) is spectacular.

However, this is not one for the faint-hearted. The hike involves climbing uphill all the way to the top in tropical, humid conditions. While it was a sticky and difficult hike, I was blown away by the view at the top. I promise it is worth the effort.

The hike took me about 2-3 hours each way to complete, so I’d recommend allowing a minimum of 7 hours to admire the viewpoints along the trail and to spend a bit of time at the top.

This is one of the very best places to visit in Krabi – you don’t want to miss this!

Cost : The entrance price costs 200THB per person (cash only and payable at the Rangers’ Station at the trailhead), 100THB for children, and 40THB for Thai Nationals. The fee goes towards repairing and maintaining the trail.

Location : [The trailhead](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zEq56HgcQTSpcqj18) is near Tup Kaek Beach, which is about a 30-minute drive from Krabi and Ao Nang. If you don’t have a hire car or a scooter, you can [book a tour of Khao Ngon Nak from Krabi](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/H2c0-06Bjh).
### 2. Visit Railay Beach, Phra Nang Beach and Tonsai Beach

If you’re planning to spend some time in Krabi, [a visit to Railay Beach](https://passport-for-living.com/visiting-railay-beach/) should be high on your Krabi activity list. It was one of my favourite places in Krabi, and we visited twice.

There’s so much to do in Railay that you can easily spend 3 or 4 days there and still not see everything.

Railay Beach is connected to the mainland but is only accessible by boat and consists of four different beaches – Tonsai Beach, Railay East, Railay West and Phra Nang Beach. Each beach has its own personality and appeal, so I suggest spending some time on each one.

Cost: 200 BHT per person for a return boat trip from Ao Nang to Railay Beach.

How to get there: You can pay for your long-tail boat tickets [from Ao Nang to Railay Beach here](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DdzCYWiHgc1WK69a6).
### 3. Rent a Kayak at Railay Beach

If you fancy something a little more active than lying on the beach, you can rent kayaks from the shore of Railay Beach. There are plenty of hidden coves to explore, and the water tends to be calm and shallow, which means kayaking isn’t too difficult. There is also a chance of seeing tropical fish as the water tends to be very clear.

You can rent kayaks by the hour. It’s a great way to see some areas that the majority of tourists miss.
### 4. Rock Climbing at Phra Nang Beach or Tonsai Beach

The epic limestone cliffs that frame the beaches of Phra Nang and Tonsai make for the perfect playground for adrenaline junkies and climbing enthusiasts. You can book a [half-day climbing introduction for beginners](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/-U8Kt0SWi0) or a [full-day climbing course](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/SHsPFeMH4v) at Railay Beach that includes lunch.

Cost: A half-day climbing introduction for beginners costs from 1500-2000 BHT (approx £35-50). A full-day climbing experience costs from 2600-3000 BHT (approx £60-70). You can pay more for private climbing experiences.

Location: Many of the tours will pick you up in Krabi or Ao Nang, or you can speak to someone directly on Railay Beach if you’re already there.
### 5. Hike to the Railay East Beach Viewpoint

This is one of the best views in Railay Beach and is well worth the strenuous hike to get there. The start of the trail is easy to miss. I only spotted it because we saw people climbing up a rope and wondered where it went.

The start of the trail is located on the walkway between Phra Nang Beach and Railay East Beach.

The walk takes a couple of hours to complete and is difficult due to having to climb some sections using ropes. You need trainers or walking boots to complete this hike. I would not advise doing this in flip-flops.

Cost: Free

Location: The location of the [viewpoint is here.](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uye9yo7PcjUT85vq5) The trail to reach it starts between Phra Nang and Railay East Beach.
### 6. Visit Railay’s Princess Lagoon

Railay’s secret Princess Lagoon can also be accessed via the same path to the Railay East viewpoint. You’ll see signs directing you to a lagoon. The trail to the lagoon gets very steep, and there were multiple occasions where I was worried I might slip.

This trail is so steep, and there are no safety ropes to stop you from falling if you slip, so the trail sometimes gets closed in bad weather.

When I visited in January, the lagoon wasn’t at its best. Some tourists told us the lagoon looks much better in the rainy season when it has plenty of water to top it up. When I visited, it was more like a fetid lagoon of stagnant water and not particularly beautiful.

Cost: Free

Location: [The lagoon is located here.](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NxufuYnDBY9iobvM9) The trail to reach it starts between Phra Nang and Railay East Beach.
### 7. Visit the Cave Shrine at Phra Nang Beach

One of the more unique things to do in Krabi is to visit the shrine located in the cave at the far end of Phra Nang Beach. The shrine consists of hundreds of phallic-shaped objects. There are many legends associated with the cave, including stories of a lost princess who supposedly drowned nearby.

The shrine itself is a sacred place for local people. Fishermen and boatmen still make offerings of incense and flowers to ensure a safe journey. The phallic-shaped objects at the shrine are associated with offerings to bring fertility.

Cost: Free

Location: Phra Nang Beach, [exact location here.](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BSHgij8hyj2XKAZB6)
### 8. See Bioluminescent Plankton at Railay Beach

A truly unique experience in Krabi is to swim amongst bioluminescent plankton at Railay Beach. The best time of year to see the plankton is between November and April. Going on a starry, moonlit night is recommended for the ultimate experience.

You can book a [sunset tour to see the bioluminescent plankton](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/qR5duiuDp_) specifically, or if you’re travelling on a budget, you can simply take a paddleboard or kayak out to look for it yourself.

Cost: You can swim out for free if you’d like to do this on a budget! But for a better chance of seeing the plankton, I’d recommend [booking a sunset tour.](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/qR5duiuDp_) Tours cost approximately £30 per person, and many include snorkelling and island tours with the opportunity to see bioluminescent plankton.

Location: You can do this on Railay Beach, but many tours will depart from Ao Nang or Krabi.
### 9. Visit Ao Nang

We stayed at a friend’s house just outside of Ao Nang and found the main street and beachfront to be very touristy and busy, especially at night. Having said that, it’s a great place to pick up souvenirs and cheap clothing, and book transfers and Krabi island-hopping tours.

There are countless restaurants along the beachfront and lining the main street, with many staying open late into the night. Lots of people visit Ao Nang to party, but our favourite part was the beach.

Ao Nang Beach is beautiful, if you look past the tacky shops and bars, with the iconic Krabi cliffs flanking the beach. As the beach is so long, it doesn’t get overly crowded, and it’s also a nice spot to watch the sunset.

Cost: Free unless you buy souvenirs

Location: [Ao Nang Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gxTg9EbBHxTrtzNr9) is a long sandy beach with a long esplanade. There are plenty of shops and restaurants which offer good value, but many of the restaurants and bars are very touristy.
### 10. Get a Thai Massage

Head to one of the massage shops along the beachfront in Ao Nang between 10 am and 2 pm for a happy-hour half-priced massage (not all vendors offer 50% off, so check beforehand).

We enjoyed a full-body Thai massage on our first morning in Ao Nang, and it was the perfect way to unwind after a long flight and a busy [36 hours in Bangkok](https://passport-for-living.com/36-hours-in-bangkok-thailand/).

Cost: You can get a 1-hour Thai massage for around 200-300 BHT, which is around £5-7. It is customary to tip 10% of the massage price.
### 11. Enjoy an Island Tour

We spent our last day island hopping in Krabi, and it was the perfect end to an amazing trip. We booked a [4-island tour](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/o0eMTtORLi) which usually consists of a stop at Phra Nang Beach and Cave, Tup Island, Chicken Island and Poda Island.

These are the three islands closest to Ao Nang, so it takes less than an hour by boat to reach them. Phra Nang isn’t an island, as it’s connected to the mainland but is only accessible by boat.

Our 4-island tour consisted of a visit to each of the above-mentioned islands, with a picnic lunch and a guide. Snorkelling equipment and life jackets were provided too.

You could also visit [Ko Yao Noi](https://thealwayswanderer.com/koh-yao-noi-off-the-beaten-track-in-thailand/), a peaceful island just a 60-minute boat trip from Krabi, for a few days. While on Ko Yao Noi, you can visit other islands in the area, explore the island by scooter, watch sunsets and enjoy the serenity and nature.

The islands are exceptionally beautiful, serene and peaceful. The white sandy beaches are very secluded and great for snorkelling. At one point, I was standing knee-deep in the water surrounded by tropical fish swimming through my legs, which was amazing! We spent an entire day sunbathing, swimming, snorkelling and hopping from beach to beach.

You can either book a [speed boat tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/krabi-l2174/7-islands-speedboat-to-twilight-treasures-from-krabi-t543177), many of which provide you with lunch and snorkelling equipment included in the price, or you can book a private long-tail boat, which may be cheaper if there’s a group of you. Some tours also offer a [4-island sunset tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/krabi-l2174/krabi-7-islands-sunset-and-night-snorkeling-tour-t328876) or even a [7-island tour with dinner](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/SXaSLIitbT) for an additional price.

Cost: Group tours start from around £25 per person, or private tours from £100 per person.

Location: Some tours provide hotel pick-up and drop-off, or you can find long-tail boats for hire on Ao Nang seafront.
### 12. Book a Khao Sok National Park Tour

[Khao Sok National Park](https://www.khaosok.com/) is only a 3-hour drive north of Krabi and is undoubtedly one of the best and most unique things to do near Krabi! [My three days in Khao Sok National Park](https://passport-for-living.com/khao-sok-tour-review/) were a top highlight of my trip. The National Park itself is beyond beautiful! It’s one of the most beautiful places I’ve ever been to.

We booked a tour with [Khao Sok Riverside Cottages](https://booking.stay22.com/passportforliving/GUTiQU9qD7) and enjoyed a night at the Riverside Cottages jungle complex in a cabin and a night in an overwater bungalow on Cheow Lan Lake. A 3-day, 2-night tour of Khao Sok National Park with Riverside Cottages starts from ฿4,850 per person, which is great value for everything included.

Our tour included meals at the overwater bungalows, transportation around the park, a river canoe trip, a jungle hike and two boat safaris, with the possibility of seeing a variety of wildlife, including tigers, elephants, monkeys, gibbons and tropical birds, although we didn’t see tigers or elephants!

I’d recommend spending three days in Khao Sok National Park as a minimum, as there’s so much to see, but if you only have two days, it’s still worth the trip!

### 13. Island Hopping – Koh Phi Phi, Koh Lanta

Island hopping is why many people visit Krabi in the first place, as it makes a great base for exploring the region, including the nearby islands. It’s easy to see why tourists flock to this area when you consider the white sand beaches, palm trees and relaxed island life.

If you want to visit some islands while in Krabi, visit Ko Phi Phi for great nightlife, parties and incredible scenery, visit Ko Jum for a peaceful atmosphere, and Ko Lanta for long white sand beaches.
### 14. Kayak Through a Mangrove Forest

There are plenty of mangrove forests near Krabi that are great to visit, and one of the more unique things to do near Krabi.

Mangrove forests are rife with wildlife and provide an amazing opportunity to see an abundance of unusual birds and animals. We spent a couple of hours [kayaking at Ao Thalane Kayak Centre](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/A36a0GVakt) through other-worldly, eerie mangrove forests and saw a huge water monitor lizard, a brightly-coloured parrot and a beautiful toucan, as well as a swimming monkey – is it just me, or did anyone else not realise that monkeys can swim?

If you prefer an active holiday rather than sitting on the beach, I’d highly recommend this as a cool way to spend a morning and one of the more unique things to do in Krabi.

Cost: Tours can cost anything from £20 to £60, depending on who you book with. Or you can hire a kayak and go alone if you’re familiar with the area.

Location: Rent kayaks from [Ao Thalane Kayak Centre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hUBzEEe6p7httb7x5).
### 15. Relax on Tub Kaek Beach

One of the lesser-known spots in Krabi, we visited Tub Kaek Beach twice during our trip. You’ll find that this quiet, secluded spot is almost deserted and presents a much more laid-back vibe than some of the more popular beaches at Ao Nang and Railay. What’s more, we found Tub Kaek Beach a lot quieter due to the absence of the noisy long-tail boats, which create a lot of noise!

We loved chilling out at the cool [Andalay Beach Bar](https://tripadvisor.tp.st/a2tt26Sj) at Nong Thale and have to give them a huge shout-out as I accidentally left my purse on the table when we left and, on returning two hours later to look for it, found it had been discovered by the staff and immediately put in the safe!

Drinks are a little pricey here compared to other bars in Thailand, but you’re paying for the location as it’s absolutely beautiful, and the bar itself is super cool. Be sure to head here in the evening as this is a great spot to watch the sunset!

[Book accommodation at Tub Kaek Beach](https://booking.stay22.com/passportforliving/fZiY3cqogn)
### 16. Hike to Wat Tham Seua (Tiger Cave Temple)

Wat Tham Sua, also known as Tiger Cave Temple, is a cultural and spiritual landmark, making it one of the best things to do in Krabi. Nestled in lush jungle surroundings, the temple’s name originates from a nearby cave that houses a tiger paw print. This sacred site boasts a giant golden Buddha statue and a complex of caves and shrines. Climbing the 1256 stairs to the summit, you’ll be rewarded with breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding landscapes.

[Book a sunset tour to Tiger Cave Temple](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/FxFYwIBpvs)
### 17. Visit the Emerald Pool

The Emerald Pool, located around a 50-minute drive from Krabi Town, is a popular destination for those seeking fun things to do in Krabi and its surrounding areas. The pool is a natural hot spring with warm, clear water surrounded by dense jungle. Apart from taking a dip in the Emerald Pool, there are other things to do in the vicinity. The nearby Khao Phra Bang Khram Nature Reserve offers hiking trails, leading to the famous Hot Springs Waterfall.

The entrance fees for foreigners are 400 baht for adults.

The Emerald Pool is approximately 1 hour from Ao Nang. To get there, you can rent a car or a scooter or book a tour from Ao Nang that includes a visit to the Emerald Pool.

[Book an Emerald Pool Hot Springs tour](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/_-9Yt2gR3r)
### 18. Shop at Krabi Town Night Market

Krabi Town Night Market offers a vibrant and bustling atmosphere in the heart of Krabi. Open in the evening, this market is a local gathering spot where visitors can experience the sights, sounds, and flavours of Thai street food and culture. Stalls line the streets, offering a variety of dishes, making this an excellent place to explore Thai cuisine, shop for souvenirs, and soak in the lively ambience.

[Book a Krabi Night Market tour](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/4_gGWH12pE)
### 19. Take a Boat Tour of Khao Khanab Nam Caves and Mountains

Khao Khanab Nam Caves and Mountains are limestone formations, standing prominently along the Krabi River. Visitors can explore the caves, which hold archaeological significance with ancient human remains discovered inside.

A boat trip along the river provides an opportunity to appreciate the natural beauty of the mountains and learn about the local history.

While the area may not boast white sand beaches and bars, Khao Khanab Nam Caves and Mountains offer a different perspective on the natural attractions in Krabi, providing a more tranquil experience for those seeking unique things to do in Krabi.

[Book a boat tour to Khao Khanab Nam Caves and Mountains](https://getyourguide.stay22.com/passportforliving/LgfDGMAgCH)
### 20. Take a Thai Cooking Class

Thai food is one of my favourites and I regularly cook Thai curries at home. But learning how to make Thai food properly in Thailand itself is an amazing experience. If you’re looking for a unique thing to do in Krabi, book a cooking class and learn how to make classics such as mango sticky rice, Pad Thai and Green Curry from an experienced Thai chef.

Here are a couple of cooking classes I recommend:

[Cooking Class at Thai Charm Cooking School with Meal](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/krabi-l2174/krabi-the-best-cooking-class-at-thai-charm-cooking-school-t529100) – Choose from 7 different dishes, with the option of 5 mains and 2 desserts. Eat what you make and take away a recipe book to recreate the dishes at home.

[Evening Thai Cooking Class at Ya’s Cookery](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/krabi-l2174/krabi-evening-thai-cooking-class-at-ya-s-cookery-t446722) – Cook 4 dishes from scratch, including learning how to make a curry paste. You’ll get to eat your creations and take home a recipe book.
### 21. Take a Food Tour

If you’d prefer to just eat the food rather than cook it yourself, join this [food tour in Krabi](https://viator.tp.st/miHMRKl9) and visit some of the best foodie spots. With an English-speaking guide, you’ll try 10+ food and drink tastings.

You’ll begin the tour at Krabi’s morning market, with a chance to sample some delicacies and explore the stalls. You’ll then make several other stops, including a dim sum restaurant, a family-run roti shop and a Thai-Chinese local spot.

Cost: From £37.43 per person

Location: Your guide will meet you near the Maharaj Market entrance on Maharaj 9 Alley, in front of the art mural – Krabi Street Art: Wild Life Wonder.

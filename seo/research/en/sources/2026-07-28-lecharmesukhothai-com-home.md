## Le Charme Sukhothai – Hotel in Sukhothai Old City, Close to the Historical Park

Le Charme Sukhothai Resort is a boutique hotel and resort in Sukhothai Old City, located just 1 km from Sukhothai Historical Park. Designed with Thai village-inspired architecture and surrounded by lotus ponds and tropical gardens, the resort offers a peaceful base for exploring Thailand’s first capital.

Wake up close to the ancient temples, enjoy relaxing resort facilities, and return to a quiet garden setting after a day of cultural discovery. Whether you are visiting Sukhothai for heritage travel, a romantic escape, or a family holiday, Le Charme Sukhothai combines local charm with comfortable rooms, dining, pool and warm Thai hospitality.

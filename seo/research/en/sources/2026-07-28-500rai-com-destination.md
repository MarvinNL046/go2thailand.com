# HOW TO GET HERE

500 RAI RESORTS

500Rai floating resort is located in Chiew Larn Lake, a part of Khao Sok National Park,

Surat Thani Province. It sits in a tranquil area of the lake, nestled between hills and close to waterfalls, hiking trails, and caves.

To get to 500Rai, you will need to combine different modes of transportation. The first part will be by road to Chiew Larn Lake Pier (aka Ratchaprapha Pier at Ratchaprapha Dam) and the second on a long-tail boat (a traditional Thai boat).

The long-tail boat ride to the hotel takes 1 Hour and 30 minutes. On the way, you will be introduced to the beautiful lake, emerald green water, and exuberant nature with strategic stops for photos.

BY LONG TAIL BOAT

Chiew Larn pier is the only one port to access 500Rai. You need to take a boat to the resort. On the way, you will be introduced to the beautiful lake, emerald green water, and exuberant nature with strategic stops for photos.

From the pier you can reach 500Rai within 1 hour and 30 minutes.

Only long tail boat is allowed to operate in National Park by locals. There are 2 sizes; small boat can take up to 10 people and big boat can take up to 18 people.

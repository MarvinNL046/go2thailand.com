# Top 10 Things to Do in Bang Tao, Phuket with Family

A Relaxed Family Guide Near Homm Suites Laguna, Laguna Phuket

Planning a family getaway in Phuket but hoping to escape the crowded tourist hotspots? Bang Tao’s 8-kilometre stretch of coastline on Phuket’s scenic west coast is one of the island’s least crowded which is a slower, more spacious alternative to busier beaches like Patong. Here, long sandy shores, laid-back cafés ,family-friendly attractions, and genuine local experiences all come together in one area.

Homm Suites Laguna sits right in the heart of Laguna Phuket, putting all of this within easy reach. From beach days and shopping to wellness and local culture, here are the top 10 things to do in Bang Tao with your family.

1. Relax at Bang Tao Beach

No visit to Bang Tao is complete without time at the beach itself. Stretching over 8 kilometres, it’s one of Phuket’s longest and least crowded shorelines with all white sand and calm water.

Unlike some of Phuket’s busier beaches, Bang Tao feels more relaxed. This makes it ideal for families who want to slow down and enjoy time together. The beach’s spacious setting means there is plenty of room for children to play while parents relax and enjoy the ocean breeze.

Unlike Patong or Kata, Bang Tao rarely feels crowded as there’s plenty of room for children to run and play while parents relax with an ocean breeze. Homm Suites Laguna is just a short walk away, so heading back for lunch or a nap is never a hassle.

2. Explore Boat Avenue Phuket

Just minutes from Homm Suites Laguna, Boat Avenue Phuket is a popular lifestyle hub in Bang Tao. Shop, dine, and enjoy casual family outings.

You’ll find:

International restaurants

Thai Massage

Local Thai eateries

Cafés and bakeries

Boutique shops

Supermarkets for essentials

Friday night market

In the evenings, Boat Avenue becomes lively yet comfortable, making it a great place or dinner after a day at the beach.

3. Join a Traditional Phuket Dessert-Making Experience at Homm Suites Laguna

Want to create meaningful family memories while experiencing local culture?

One of the most memorable activities on offer at Homm Suites Laguna is a hands-on class where kids learn to make “Ko Sui,” a traditional Thai dessert made from rice flour and coconut milk. The class is guided, tactile, and genuinely fun which children leave with something they made themselves and a story worth telling.

It’s the kind of cultural experience that blends well into a family holiday with low-key ,interactive, and a natural break from sightseeing.

Rather than rushing between attractions, experiences like these allow families to slow down and connect in a meaningful way.

4. Visit Porto de Phuket

For a relaxed afternoon outing, head to Porto de Phuket, a stylish open-air lifestyle mall located near Bang Tao.

Porto de Phuket combines shopping, dining, and everyday family convenience all in one open-air complex. The wide walkways and relaxed atmosphere make it easy to browse without feeling rushed, and there’s a good variety of food options for different tastes and ages.

Highlights include:

International brands and local shops

Casual restaurants for families

Dessert cafés and ice cream spots

Grocery shopping for holiday essentials

Parents travelling with younger children often appreciate having an easy indoor-outdoor space where everyone can relax comfortably.

Canal Village i s one of the reasons Laguna Phuket works so well as a family base. Dining, shopping, and activities are all within a short stroll of Homm Suites Laguna, so days feel easy rather than logistically complicated.

For families with older kids or adventurous travellers, jet skiing at Bang Tao Beach is a fun activity. It adds excitement to a relaxed day.

Balancing adventure with relaxation helps create a holiday experience that everyone in the family can enjoy.

## Things to know before buying a SIM card in Thailand

Buying a prepaid SIM card in Thailand is very straightforward, but there are a few things tourists should know before getting one.

Below are some practical tips based on my own experiences traveling around Thailand and buying SIM cards many times over the years.
### SIM card registration in Thailand

In the past it was possible to buy a prepaid SIM card in Thailand without showing identification. That is no longer the case.

Today all SIM cards must be registered with a passport. When buying a tourist SIM card the staff will take a copy of your passport and register the SIM card under your name.

You can register up to five SIM cards per passport. The registration process only takes a couple minutes and is normally handled directly by the shop staff.

Once activated, your SIM card will come with a Thai phone number starting with +66.
### Free SIM cards in Thailand

You may sometimes see promotions offering a free SIM card in Thailand. Airlines or airport stands occasionally hand out these SIM cards to arriving travelers.

However, these SIM cards usually contain almost no usable data.

For example, Thai Airways has previously distributed dtac SIM cards that advertise free 8 GB of data, but in reality the SIM card initially only includes a very small amount of credit and you still need to top up to activate the full data package.

In other words, the SIM card itself may be free, but the actual mobile data plan is not.
### Make sure your phone is unlocked

Before buying a prepaid SIM card for Thailand it is important that your phone is SIM unlocked.

If your phone is locked to your mobile provider from your home country, a Thai SIM card will not work. Most modern smartphones are unlocked, but if you are unsure it is best to check with your mobile provider before traveling.
### Can tourists get a Thai phone number?

Yes. When you buy a prepaid SIM card in Thailand you automatically receive a Thai phone number. These numbers always start with the country code +66.

This can be useful for local calls, restaurant reservations or verifying certain apps that require a local phone number.

### What is an eSIM?

Let’s start with the basics. ESIM stands for embedded SIM. Rather than buying a physical SIM card and installing it, you buy an eSIM online and install it (like you would an app). It can be activated remotely, giving you connectivity shortly after purchase.

### How does an eSIM work?

ESIMs allow you to connect to a local mobile network. You activate the eSIM profile through your phone settings. It’s easy to switch on and off, so you can move between data plans easily, or a physical SIM and an eSIM.

Depending on the provider you choose, you will have access to some of the strongest local networks in your destination, meaning you’ll always be able to use your phone as normal. In Thailand that includes [DTAC](https://www.simlocal.com/networks/dtac-esim), one of the country’s leading mobile networks, trusted for its strong nationwide coverage and fast, reliable data speeds.

## About Us

Our Jungle Camp will connect you to it´s awesome natural surroundings in an atmosphere of comfort and relaxation.

Khao Sok´s unique Nature Resort, with Treehouse and Bungalow accommodation options for families and individual travellers alike.
Nestled between Khao Sok Rainforest and the gentle flowing Sok River, Our Jungle Camp boasts Double and Single-Storey Treehouses as well as unique Adobe and Nature Bungalows. All our accommodation option, have been meticulously crafted from natural materials, creating a fully inclusive experience of the awe-inspiring natural surrounds, while offering incredible views of the river, cliffs and diverse wildlife.

Join us at Our Jungle Camp in Khao Sok for "Exquisite Simplicity, Rustic Chic and Jungle Boutique - Connect with amidst the pristine environment of Khao Sok National Park"

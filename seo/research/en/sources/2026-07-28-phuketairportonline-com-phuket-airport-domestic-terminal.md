# Phuket Airport Domestic Terminal

Phuket Airport’s Domestic Terminal is located next to the International Terminal. The Domestic Terminal underwent extensive renovations from 2016 to 2018. There are frequent flights departing from Phuket to Bangkok–Don Mueang and Bangkok–Suvarnabhumi airports, as well as Chiang Mai, Samui, Udon Thani, Hat Yai, Rayong and Chiang Rai.

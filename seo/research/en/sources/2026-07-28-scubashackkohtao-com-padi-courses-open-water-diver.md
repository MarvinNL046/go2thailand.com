### OPEN WATER DIVER PRE-REQUISITES

To start the PADI Open Water Diver Course you need to be at least 10 years old. You need to be able to swim 200 Meters and float/tread water for ten minutes.

You also need to be in good physical health. Please take a look at the [PADI Medical Participant Questionnaire](https://www.scubashackkohtao.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/10346-Diver-Medical-Form-4.pdf) to find out if you should be examined by your Doctor before participating in Recreational Scuba Diving.

Confined water dives (to learn all the basic scuba skills)

Open water dives (to practice your skills and gain confidence)

### FAQ

The PADI Open Water course costs 12.000 Baht, including the open water e-manual, all equipment, insurance, and the certification fee. There are no additional costs.

The minimum age is 10 years old. Student divers who are younger than 15 earn the PADI Junior Open Water Diver certification. The Junior Open Water certification comes with some restrictions.

Divers 10-11 years old must dive with a PADI Professional or a certified parent or guardian, and dives cannot exceed 12 meters/40 feet.

Divers 12-14 years old must dive with a certified adult and dives cannot exceed 18 meters / 60 feet.

Children under the age of 13 require parent or guardian permission to register for PADI eLearning.

Jr. Open Water Divers may upgrade to PADI Open Water Diver certification at age 15 by requesting a new certification card (no additional training is required).

Your PADI Open Water certification does not expire. If you haven’t been diving in a while, it is recommended that you refresh your knowledge and skills by completing a scuba review or the PADI ReActivate program.

You will be certified to dive with a buddy to a maximum depth of 18 metres/60 feet anywhere in the world.

PADI is the largest scuba diving training agency in the world and certifies more scuba divers than any other agency. The PADI Open Water course is the world’s most popular entry level scuba diver certification and you will find more PADI dive shops around the world than any other dive agency.

The quality of training and emphasis on safety are very important values within the PADI educational system and the courses are focused on performance-based learning and offer flexibility to suit the individual needs of students.

The duration of dives during the PADI Open Water course depends on several factors which you’ll learn about during the course. Basically the deeper you dive, the shorter your dive will be.

During the open water course, the first two dives have a maximum depth of 12 meters, then for the last 2 dives we can go to a maximum depth of 18 meters. At these depths, an average dive time is about 45 minutes.

The Open Water course consists of a confined water training session, either in a pool or a shallow bay around the island, followed by 4 open water dives.

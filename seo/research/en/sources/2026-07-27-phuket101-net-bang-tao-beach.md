# What to Do in Bang Tao Beach?
## Things to Do in Bang Tao Beach?

[Things to Do in Bang Tao Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/best-things-to-do-in-bang-tao-beach/)

Guests staying at any of the Laguna Complex resorts or in hotels in the Cherngtalay area can enjoy a wide range of things to do on Bang Tao and around. Many activities are available at the Laguna hotels: sailing, kayaking, archery, immense swimming pools, massages, and amazing spas.

Usually, if you are a guest of one of these resorts, you can use the facilities of any other hotel in the group. Besides, beach clubs are back in Phuket, and Bangtao has no less than five now. Those beach clubs are also a great way to spend the day and party at night. More inland, you can have fun at the golf course, the mini golf or even rock climbing.

[Layan Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/layan-beach/)

There are no real boundaries between Bangtao and the more laid-back Layan Beach. Just look to the right, and you will see the iconic island of Layan Beach. It’s just next to Bang Tao, but it offers a much different atmosphere that hasn’t changed much since the old days of Phuket.

## Where to Eat in Bang Tao Beach?

[Where to Eat in Bang Tao Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/where-to-eat-on-bang-tao-beach/)

Dining in Bang Tao has come a long way. While the resort restaurants are usually excellent, they tend to come with a higher price tag. Outside the hotels, the area has become a hotspot for food lovers, especially around Boat Avenue, Porto de Phuket, and the lakeside. There’s a mix of international cuisine, trendy bistros, and a few very good Thai restaurants. Prices are generally a bit higher than in other parts of Phuket, but the quality and setting often make up for it. You’ll still find some local restaurants on the beach, though they now mostly serve tourists and the food isn’t always as authentic as it used to be. Overall, it’s still easy to find great food for every budget.
## Moonshine

[Moonshine](https://www.moonshinephuket.net/)

Hidden along Lagoon Road in Bang Tao, Moonshine Phuket is the island’s only true speakeasy-style gastropub and jazz lounge. Step inside and you’re transported to Prohibition-era America: dark walls, candlelight, vintage brass fittings, and live music six nights a week. The kitchen serves a full à la carte menu with standout dishes like Grilled NY Strip Steak, Chicken Parmigiana, and Lobster Linguini, paired with craft cocktails built around the spirit of the 1920s. And on Thursdays, all ladies drink free all night long, no conditions.
## Im En Ville Phuket

[Im En Ville Phuket](https://www.imenvillephuket.com/)

Nestled inside Porto de Phuket in Cherng Talay, IM En Ville is a warm, welcoming all-day dining destination that works for everyone. The menu bridges Thai comfort classics like Tom Yum Goong and Pad Kra Pao with Western favourites including burgers, pizzas, and quesadillas, all at accessible prices. Specialty coffees, fresh juices, and creative Thai-inspired cocktails round out the offering. The space is bright and relaxed, with indoor and outdoor seating, free Wi-Fi, and a family-friendly, pet-friendly atmosphere that makes it just as suited to a working morning as a leisurely weekend lunch.
## What to Do at Night?

[What to Do at Night?](https://www.phuket101.net/bang-tao-beach-nightlife/)

Nightlife in Bang Tao is quite laid-back but still enjoyable. Most people go for a casual drink at one of the small bamboo bars right on the sand, often with their feet in the sand and music playing softly in the background. A growing number of beach clubs along the bay stay open late with cocktails and DJs, but the mood is more relaxed than party-heavy. Some beachfront restaurants also serve drinks late, so it’s easy to end the night slowly, by the sea.

There is a handful of old-fashioned tiny reggae bars consisting of a bamboo shack, five stools and blasting Bob’s reggae music! These small, simple shacks are inexpensive and add a tropical touch to your holiday. Several of them are just outside the Outrigger Resort.

Bang Tao Beach (or Bangtao) is one of the longest beaches in Phuket, halfway between [Phuket airport](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-airport/) and [Patong Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/patong-beach/) on the island’s west coast and part of the [Cherngtalay](https://www.phuket101.net/cherngtalay-phuket/) area.

With such a beautiful stretch of sand, it’s no surprise that Bangtao has become the home of some of the largest and most luxurious resorts on the [island of Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/), such as Banyan Tree Resort, Dusit Thani Laguna Phuket, Angsana Phuket Resort, and SAii Laguna Phuket Resort. Bangtao is also where you can find dozens of [local Thai restaurants](https://www.phuket101.net/best-local-restaurants-phuket/) and beach clubs right on the beach, and eat or drink with your feet in the sand.
## Overview

Bangtao was one of the [tin mines](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-mining-museum/) that made the fortune of Phuket long ago. They abandoned it later, and the land was considered worthless. [Laguna Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/laguna-phuket/) managed to transform it into a very profitable, award-winning Eco-project. Laguna started as a luxurious complex in the first years, but it was a little isolated. Guests of the resorts had little choice outside the resort restaurants and facilities, so prices were always on the high side.

In recent years, Laguna has built many luxury villas and properties in the back area of Bang Tao Beach and [Layan Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/layan-beach/). Laguna has always been the ‘high-end’ part of the island, and you can see the difference between tourist and resident markets in Patong when looking at the shops, villas, and even cars around the area.

However, it is still an affordable place to go for lunch or drinks on the beach. In conclusion, Bangtao has changed a lot, and it has become one of the most versatile beaches in Phuket with an incredible choice of restaurants, shopping, and even beach clubs, and it is becoming one of the best places to live.

## The Beach of Bangtao, of course

The beach is one of the main reasons why travellers book their hotels here. The beach is narrower, the sand is a little less powdery than in Patong or Kata, and the water is not as shallow, but it is superb and never gets crowded. Visitors can enjoy the rare luxury of eating at local restaurants or drinking at small bamboo bars on the beach.
## Restaurants on the Beach

Just south of the [SAii Laguna Resort](https://www.phuket101.net/saii-laguna-phuket-resort/), you will be surprised to find many seafood and Thai food restaurants on the beach. The road to reach them is well hidden, but there is a decent parking space. We tried the Green Leaf restaurant, which was not exceptional but good. The sea view and the beach setting made up for the growing touristy taste and atmosphere. There are many restaurants, and they all look the same with their bamboo tables and colourful umbrellas. They probably also taste similar, but that is fun to try more than one.

## Bang Tao Beach with Kids

Bang Tao is one of the easiest Phuket beaches to travel with young children. The bay is 6 kilometres long, which means space is never a problem, and the crowds thin out quickly as soon as you walk a few minutes in either direction from the main hotels. The sand is firm and easy to walk on, and the water is calm for most of the high season. Most of the large family resorts sit directly behind the beach, so you can be back at the hotel pool within minutes when the sun gets too strong.

Beyond the beach, there are several activities within a short drive that work well with kids. [Phuket Adventure Mini Golf](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-adventure-mini-golf/) has an 18-hole course set in a tropical garden with palm trees, bamboo and water features, calm enough for younger children. [Rebel Rock Climbing](https://www.phuket101.net/rebel-rock-climbing-in-phuket/) in nearby Cherngtalay is a 700 sqm indoor climbing centre with walls for every age and level, and it is a particularly good rainy-day option. The [Friday night market at Boat Avenue](https://www.phuket101.net/fun-friday-avenue-market/) is another easy family outing, with food stalls, live music and plenty of space for kids to walk around.

The best family hotels in Bang Tao are the ones inside the Laguna complex, where guests can use the facilities of the other Laguna resorts. [SAii Laguna Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/saii-laguna-phuket-resort/) is directly on the beach and has a dedicated kids’ club. [Angsana Laguna Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/angsana-laguna-phuket-resort/) has a large lagoon-style pool and is popular with families. [Dusit Thani Laguna Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/dusit-thani-laguna-phuket/) offers direct beach access with a family-friendly setup. For more accommodation options in the area, see the [best family hotels in Bang Tao Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/best-family-hotels-in-bang-tao-beach/).

For the full list of family activities across the island, see my guide to [things to do in Phuket with kids](https://www.phuket101.net/things-to-do-in-phuket-with-kids/).
## Bang Tao Beach Through the Years

I first went to Bang Tao in 1994 to apply as a resort host at [Banyan Tree Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/banyan-tree-phuket/). Back then, the [Laguna Phuket complex](https://www.phuket101.net/laguna-phuket/) was just five large hotels, a couple of restaurants around Tinlay Place (opposite what is now Boat Avenue), and not much else. Tatonka and [Black Cat](https://www.phuket101.net/black-cat-restaurant/) were the only real dining options outside the hotels, and both are still here today. People living in Patong and Kata looked down on Bang Tao at the time. It was too remote, too quiet, and the beach was not as wide or as attractive as it is now. The area was seen as a place to stay inside your hotel, almost all-inclusive in style, even though it was not. Guests at one resort could use the pools and restaurants of the four other hotels nearby, and that was basically the whole Bang Tao experience, plus the [golf course](https://www.phuket101.net/laguna-golf-phuket/). There were no villas, no beach clubs, no [Boat Avenue](https://www.phuket101.net/boat-avenue-mall/).
## Insider Tips

Bang Tao has completely transformed. It is now the hi-so part of Phuket, as we say in Thailand. The dining scene is outstanding, with several Michelin-recognised restaurants, and there is a huge choice of luxury villas and condominiums that did not exist twenty years ago. Boat Avenue has become a proper neighbourhood with shops, bars, and a popular [Friday night market](https://www.phuket101.net/fun-friday-avenue-market/). I still go to Bang Tao often because the restaurants are some of the best on the island. But the growth has come at a cost. The number of villas and condos has created a permanent traffic jam on the roads in and out. If you are staying in Bang Tao, that is fine. If you are driving in from another part of the island, be prepared.

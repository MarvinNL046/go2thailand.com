## What is Kaeng Khiao Wan?

The name Kaeng Khiao Wan (แกงเขียวหวาน) translates to “sweet green curry” in Thai. However, the word “sweet” ( wan – หวาน) does not refer to the taste but rather to the creamy green color of the curry. Kaeng Khiao Wan is a rich and aromatic Thai curry made with coconut milk, green curry paste, and a choice of protein, such as chicken, beef, or fish balls. It is a perfect balance of spicy, savory, and slightly sweet flavors. Served with steamed jasmine rice or Khanom Chin, this dish is a staple of Thai cuisine and a favorite among locals and international food lovers alike.
## History of Kaeng Khiao Wan

Thai curries were originally clear and broth-based. Over time, the introduction of coconut milk and curry pastes transformed Thai cuisine, leading to the creation of richer and creamier dishes. Kaeng Khiao Wan is believed to have emerged during the early 20th century, around the reign of King Rama VI to Rama VII (1910–1935). Traditionally, the green curry paste itself is an adaptation of the red curry paste, replacing dried red chilies with fresh green chilies to achieve its signature color.

Today, Kaeng Khiao Wan is one of Thailand’s most internationally recognized dishes. It is commonly served in Thai households, street food stalls, and high-end restaurants. The dish has also gained global fame, appearing on menus worldwide and being featured in international food rankings. With its bold flavors and creamy texture, Thai Green Curry continues to captivate food lovers everywhere, cementing its place as a staple of Thai cuisine.

### Instructions

Cook the meat – Add bite-sized meat pieces and stir until cooked. Season with fish sauce and palm sugar. Pour in the remaining coconut milk and bring to a boil.

# Moo Yor

Vietnamese sausage

In Thailand, is the name given to a sausage that originally comes from Vietnam and is called “Chả lụa” there. It is made from
minced pork and various spices. This sausage mixture is rolled in banana leaves and then steamed.

Moo Yor (หมูยอ)

Alternative names

Moo Yor,
Moo Jor,
Muu Yaw

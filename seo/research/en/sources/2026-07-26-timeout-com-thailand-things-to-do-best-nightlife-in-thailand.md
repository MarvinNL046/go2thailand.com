### Full Moon Party

Where is it? Haad Rin Beach, Koh Phangan, Surat Thani

What is it? This legendary beach party happens every month on the night of the full moon. Picture neon body paint, fire dancers, and a lineup of music that spans everything from trance and techno to reggae. It’s a full-on festival right on the sand.

Why go? If you’re looking for a one-of-a-kind party experience, the Full Moon Party is where it’s at. Dance under the stars alongside a global crowd, with the music pumping, lights flashing, and an energy that’s simply unmatched. Whether you want to let loose with friends, sip cocktails by the beach, or soak up the electric atmosphere, this is the place to be. With a lively mix of entertainment, from fire dancers to multiple music stages, there’s always something to keep you on your feet all night long. Mark your calendars for the 2024 Full Moon Party, taking place on October 18, November 15, December 15, and the epic New Year’s Eve bash on December 31 in Koh Phangan.

### Coco Tam’s

Where is it? Koh Samui, Surat Thani

What is it? A renowned beachfront spot that combines a restaurant, cafe, and beach bar, all wrapped up in a relaxed, open-air design. With its wooden accents and thatched roofs, it feels refreshingly breezy. Guests can choose from an array of seating options, whether they want to sink into comfy bean bags right on the sand or lounge in stylish swing seats at the bar. Plus, the venue boasts a large projector screen, perfect for catching a game or enjoying a movie under the stars.

Why go? This venue is a must-see for anyone eager to enjoy a stunning sunset alongside delicious food and refreshing drinks. Lounge by the sea, sipping cocktails as the sun dips below the horizon, all while soaking in the natural beauty of Koh Samui. The chill vibe invites you to kick back with friends or savour a romantic evening with that special someone. A standout feature at Coco Tam’s is the nightly fire show, lighting up the beach at 7.15pm and 9pm, adding an extra spark to your experience.

### Tha Chang Cafe

Where is it? Chiang Mai

What is it? Tha Chang Cafe has been a popular nightspot for over 15 years, radiating youthful energy with live music that keeps things lively. The cafe features an extensive drinks menu, making it a fantastic place to dive into Chiang Mai's nightlife while soaking up the local vibe.

Why go? If you want to experience the heart of Chiang Mai’s nightlife, Tha Chang Cafe is the ideal destination. Enjoy a lively evening surrounded by locals, savouring delicious drinks in a fun

environment. The blend of live music and a welcoming atmosphere creates an unforgettable experience, making it an ideal spot to hang out with friends or make new ones.

### Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

Where is it? The thirty-fourth floor of Hilton Pattaya, Pattaya.

What is it? Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar offers spectacular views of Pattaya from high above the city. This stylish venue serves up a menu filled with creative twists on delectable dishes. Relax at the Infinity Bar, feel the refreshing sea breeze, and escape the noise of the streets below. Open daily from 4pm to 1am, you can catch the sunset or enjoy late-night bites.

Why go? If you’re looking to elevate your dining experience in Pattaya, this is the spot for you. With stunning vistas, fantastic food, and a mellow ambience, it’s great for a special night out or simply chilling with friends. Whether you’re toasting a special occasion or unwinding after a long day, the atmosphere here makes it a must-visit. Don’t miss the Sunset Session for excellent drink deals.

### Yona Beach Club

Where is it? Royal Phuket Marina, Phuket

What is it? Yona Beach Club is your go-to for a luxurious beach day in Phuket. Located on the second floor, this beach club features a stunning 22-metre infinity pool with panoramic ocean views. Whether you want to relax in a private cabana with friends or lounge on a pool bed soaking up the sun, you’ll find a spot that suits your style. For a unique experience, check out the sea-beds for a new perspective on the beautiful coastline.

Why go? If you’re after a taste of luxury while enjoying the sun and surf, Yona Beach Club is the destination for you. With gorgeous views, a vibrant setting, and top-notch amenities, it’s the ideal place for a day out with friends or a special celebration. Don’t forget to check out their epic monthly events that keep the party going until 8pm.

### Phuket Old Town

Where is it? Phuket

What is it? Evenings in Phuket Old Town provide a welcome change from the island’s lively nightlife scene. While the beaches are alive with parties, Old Town showcases an array of stylish bars featuring Sino-Portuguese architecture, elegant cocktail lounges, and soothing live music, creating a more tranquil atmosphere to unwind and soak in the local charm.

Why go? Phuket Old Town is the perfect place to relax and absorb the local culture. Enjoy a drink at one of the unique bars or lounges, mingle with locals, and embrace the charm of this historic area. Be sure to visit the Walking Street Market (Lard-Yai), open every Sunday from 4pm-10pm for food lovers and souvenir hunters alike. It’s a delightful way to enjoy a laid-back night while capturing the essence of Phuket.

### Blanc BKK

Where is it? Bangkok

What is it? Blanc BKK is a bar designed with a luxurious minimalist aesthetic, renowned for its top-notch DJs and stylish ambience.

Why go? If you love bars that ooze luxury and provide a chic backdrop for photos without the chaos, this is a top choice. With a wide range of drink options, it’s an excellent spot for those looking to have fun while unwinding. The music selection is fantastic, featuring international hits from the '80s and '90s hip-hop, making it an ideal place for a stylish night out.

### Cicada Night Market

Where is it? Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan

What is it? An open-air market known for its shopping, dining, and entertainment, featuring handmade crafts, clothing, and delicious street food from local vendors.

Why go? This is a cultural hotspot perfect for a relaxed evening of shopping and dining. Stroll through a lively mix of stalls where local artisans show off their creations. You can munch on everything from tasty Thai street food to international eats. There’s often live music and art exhibitions happening, making it a fun hangout for families, friends, or couples looking to enjoy a memorable night out in Hua Hin.

### Old Town

Where is it? Bangkok

What is it? The perfect mix of history and charm. Whether you’re wandering through the lively Khaosan Road or getting lost in the artsy vibes of Talad Noi, there’s always something to discover. With quaint cafes, quirky shops, and beautiful old buildings, this area is a delightful spot to explore and discover Bangkok’s rich culture.

Why go? Exploring Old Town is essential for anyone wanting to experience the authentic side of Bangkok. Stroll through lively markets, sample some of the city’s best street food, and immerse yourself in the local culture. It’s an excellent spot for snapping Instagram-worthy photos and discovering hidden gems, from iconic landmarks to leisurely walks along the river. Whether you’re flying solo or with friends, Old Town offers a delightful mix of adventure and relaxation.

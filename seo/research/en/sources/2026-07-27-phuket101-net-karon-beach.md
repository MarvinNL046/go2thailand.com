# ​What to Do in Karon Beach?
## Things to Do in Karon

[Things to Do in Karon](https://www.phuket101.net/things-to-do-in-karon-beach/)

Karon Beach is a great place to take it easy. The beach is long and wide, perfect for swimming or just lying on the sand. If you like a bit of action, you’ll find parasailing and jet skis right on the beach. You can also get a beach massage under the trees. In the evening, check out the Karon Temple Market, great for local food and souvenirs. There are loads of restaurants nearby too. For a change of scene, [Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-beach/) and [Patong](https://www.phuket101.net/patong-beach/) are just a short ride away.
## Wat Karon (Karon Temple)

[Wat Karon (Karon Temple)](https://www.phuket101.net/wat-suwan-kirikhet-karon-temple/)

Karon temple, locally called Wat Suwan Khirikhet, is beautifully crafted and open to visitors any day of the week. You can walk around freely, take photos and admire the carvings and paintings, but try to make a small donation; it is always a nice gesture. The temple hosts a famous night market twice a week with lots of food and little things to buy. Remember to dress politely, just like in any [temple in Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-temples/) and Thailand, which means no short skirts, no sleeveless shirts and no topless. Remove caps and shoes if you wish to walk into the temple.
## Dino Park Mini Golf

[Dino Park Mini Golf](https://www.phuket101.net/dino-park-mini-golf/)

Dino Park is a Jurassic Park-inspired mini-golf with dinosaurs and even a volcano. It is technically in Kata beach, but it’s not very far from Karon and is always good family fun.
## Kata Beach

[Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-beach/)

Kata Beach is just 600 metres south of Karon, making it easy to visit on foot or by a short drive. The beach is smaller than Karon, with softer sand and clear water, and feels more enclosed, with hills at both ends. Kata is a popular spot for swimming, sunbathing, and surfing in the low season. Kata is a lot busier, with plenty of small restaurants, massage shops, and local markets nearby. Many visitors staying in Karon enjoy coming here for a change of scenery.
## Where to Stay in Karon Beach?

[Where to Stay in Karon Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/best-hotels-in-karon-beach/)

Most Karon hotels sit across the beach road rather than on the sand, usually a short flat walk from the water, and that small difference is what keeps rates reasonable for a west coast beach. The cheaper guesthouses cluster around Karon Centre and Patak Road, while the resorts line the beachfront road. These three cover the main styles, and the full list is on the [Karon hotels page](https://www.phuket101.net/best-hotels-in-karon-beach/).

## Where to Eat in Karon Beach?

[Where to Eat in Karon Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-beach-restaurants/)

Karon Beach offers a wide range of restaurants where everyone can find something to enjoy, from Thai food to Middle Eastern and Western Cuisine. On the budget side, you will find good local food in the small restaurants along the roadside, near the lake, and even more at Karon Plaza, which is not far from the circle.
## The Dim Sun

[The Dim Sun](https://www.phuket101.net/the-dim-sun/)

The Dim Sun is the rooftop of Avista Grande, and my usual answer when someone staying in Karon asks where to watch the sunset with a cocktail in hand. You sit above the rooftops with the Andaman straight ahead, and the last hour of light is worth planning dinner around. You can also come just for drinks at the pool bar if dinner is not the plan. In high season, ask for a table on the edge of the terrace when you book.
## SOUL Vegan Cafe & Restaurant

[SOUL Vegan Cafe & Restaurant](https://www.phuket101.net/soul-vegan-cafe-restaurant/)

SOUL opened as Karon’s first fully plant-based kitchen, and it has already passed the hardest test: meat eaters coming back on their own. The cooking aims at taste first, with dishes that happen to be vegan rather than shouting about it. It is a small, friendly spot on Karon Road, an easy stop on the walk back from the sand; the Union Jack tuk-tuk parked outside marks the door. If you are in Karon for more than a few days, this is the address for a lighter meal between the seafood dinners.
## Karon Beach Nightlife

[Karon Beach Nightlife](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-beach-nightlife/)

There is a bit of nightlife in Karon Beach, but it’s nothing like what you will experience on Bangla Walking Street in [Patong Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/patong-beach/). Karon nightlife rotates around three areas: the best known is near the Karon Circle, with few bars and live music, a little further is a small alley once called ‘Little Bangla’, and an L-shaped street near Kata Beach, where you can enjoy some beer bars.
## COAST Beach Club

[COAST Beach Club](https://www.phuket101.net/coast-beach-club/)

COAST Beach Club is an excellent place to enjoy a couple of drinks at the end of the day. The bar is not exactly on the beach but quite near and is part of the [Centara Grand Beach Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/centara-grand-beach-resort/).
## Tann Terrace

[Tann Terrace](https://www.phuket101.net/tann-terrace-phuket/)

Tann Terrace is a new restaurant with an immense beachfront wooden terrace at the very south of Karon Beach, just next to Beyond Resort Karon and opposite the old football stadium. The restaurant mostly serves Italian and Thai food and has an excellent bakery, perfect for enjoying breakfast on the beach.
## Karon Temple Market

[Karon Temple Market](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-temple-market/)

If live music and bars are not your thing after dinner, check out the Karon Temple Market, which opens only twice a week: Tuesdays and Fridays, from 4 pm to 10 pm. Wat Suwan Khirikhet, the temple at the end of Soi Patak in Karon, is an excellent photo opportunity and is famous for the popular night market held every Tuesday and Friday.

Never miss a chance to visit it; it’s full of fun things to try, games to play and fun local food. You might even try [fried insects](https://www.phuket101.net/eating-insects-phuket/) and [giant water bugs](https://www.phuket101.net/mang-da-na/) or take a photo to share back home.
## Karon Bazaar

[Karon Bazaar](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-plaza/)

Karon Bazaar is the big covered market next to the Paradox resort, and the closest thing Karon has to a proper souvenir run: t-shirts, elephant pants, bags, beach toys and everything you forgot to pack. Come late in the afternoon, because it gets properly hot under the roof at midday. Prices start optimistic, so bargain with a smile and expect to meet in the middle. It will not win design awards, but for last-day shopping without driving to Phuket Town, it does the job.

## More Beaches in Phuket

Karon is one of the longest beaches in Phuket, ranking 3rd on our [beach popularity](https://www.phuket101.net/beaches-phuket/) page. It enjoys a 3-kilometre stretch of white sand, a decent choice of dining and nightlife, and never seems to draw crowds of tourists, which is a good thing. Karon is known for its big beach with big hotels lined along the frontage road and the small town of Karon. While the beach is vast and long, there are only two hotels with direct beach access: [Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/centara-grand-beach-resort/) and [Beyond Karon](https://www.phuket101.net/beyond-karon/). ## Pros and Cons of Karon Beach

Long, spacious beach: 3 km of soft, white sand with plenty of space to relax.

Quiet and less crowded: Even during the high season, it feels open and uncrowded.

Family-friendly: Offers various restaurants, bars, and affordable accommodations.

Dining and nightlife: Decent range of dining options and nightlife without being overwhelming.

Easy access to other areas: Close to Kata Beach and Patong Beach for more activities and attractions.

Scattered nightlife: Modest nightlife compared to Patong, with only small beer bars and pubs.

Limited landmarks and activities: Few attractions besides Karon Temple and nearby beaches.

Seasonal swimming risks: Strong waves and rip currents during the monsoon season.

## The Beach

Karon Beach is one of the longest beaches in Phuket, stretching over 3 km of soft, white sand. The shoreline gives plenty of space to relax without feeling crowded, even in high season. The water is clear and usually has small waves during the high season (December to May), making it fun for a casual swim or a bit of body surfing. The beach is backed by a line of tall trees that offer shade, and you’ll spot a few beach vendors and sun loungers, but overall it has a very laid-back vibe. It’s a great spot for long, peaceful walks along the shore.
## Shopping in Karon Beach

Karon Beach has enough shopping options to keep everyone happy, even those who don’t like shopping but need convenience stores or forgot something important at home. There are also some markets, bazaars and, of course, night markets.
## The Geography of Karon

Did you know? Karon consists of 3 areas: Karon Centre (a small town), the Lake area and the Beachfront Road that ends at the hill reaching [Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-beach/).
## Karon Centre and Patak Rd

Patak Road is mostly a street going perpendicularly inland from the very fancy and convoluted ‘Karon Circle’ to the temple. It’s small, messy and feels rather old; additionally, parking there is impossible. Around here, you will find a few long-established restaurants, convenience stores, ‘Hellooooo, you want massage’ parlours, and lots of bars of all sizes and shapes. Just like everyone else, walk up and down Patak Road and pick one bar; ultimately, they are all the same same but different.
## The Lake

The Lake near the circle has also been the subject of many attempts to make the area an upmarket zone or a convention centre. Once in a while, especially during the [Songkran Festival](https://www.phuket101.net/songkran-phuket/), the lake hosts one of those popular local funfairs where you can play some typical funfair games, try local food and even shop.
## Beachfront Road

The beachfront road goes from the lake to [Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-beach/), which is mostly occupied by large resorts with a few small hotels. At the beginning of the beach road, you’ll find [Karon Beach Square](https://www.phuket101.net/movenpick-resort-karon/), the Night Bazaar, a temporary roofed market selling mostly tee shirts and beach toys, including all the usual fake brands.

More interesting is Soi Aroonson, a small street in the middle of the beach road. Some good restaurants, lots of massages, and a few convenience shops have opened here. It’s still quiet, but doesn’t feel as messy as the Karon Centre.

Then, you’ll reach the end of Beach Road when a small hill leads to [Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-beach/) (boundaries have never been visible). Noticeable around here is the restaurant built on the beach, a football stadium (also used for cricket competitions) and one of the two nightlife areas around.

This very narrow and hard-to-spot alley is called ‘Bangla Plaza’ and has always been here, but nothing to compare with [Soi Bangla](https://www.phuket101.net/soi-bangla/) in [Patong Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/patong-beach/) nightlife. You won’t find more than a few beer bars with colourful spotlights, even more colourful staff, and plenty of tiny shops and foot massages.
## Insider Tips

If you want a quieter spot on the beach, head to the northern end near the lake. Most tourists stay near the middle or south end, so the north stretch is often almost empty. The beachside seafood restaurants near the lake are a good lunch option too.

The squeaky sand is real. Walk barefoot on the dry sand, and it makes a high-pitched sound under your feet. It works best when the sand is dry and fine, so try it in the afternoon when the sun has been on it for a few hours. It is one of those things people always mention but never quite believe until they hear it.

Karon can be dangerous for swimming during the low season (May to October). The waves look fun, but rip currents here are strong and unpredictable. Pay attention to the red flags. If a red flag is up, do not go in. Every year, people get into serious trouble here.

For dinner, walk the beachfront promenade from the south end towards the circle. The restaurants and bars that have opened along this stretch are more interesting than the ones on Patak Road, and you get the beach view as a bonus. Tann Terrace and Coast Beach Club are both good sunset spots.
## Karon Beach Through the Years

Karon Beach was one of the first places I got to know when I arrived in Phuket in 1994 to work at Club Med, which sits just over the hill in Kata. Back then, Karon felt far from everything. The beach was immense and mostly empty. A river and open land separated it from the main road, and I remember several buffaloes bathing in that river in the afternoon. There were very few hotels and almost no restaurants apart from the small town at the north end. If you wanted to eat or do anything after the beach, you had a long walk ahead of you.

That isolation is probably why Karon developed later than Kata and Patong. For years, it stayed quiet while the other beaches built up around it. But what held Karon back turned out to be its biggest strength. The beach never got crowded, the beachfront was never lined with buildings, and the wide road along the shore gave it a spacious feeling that other beaches had lost a long time ago.

Today, the beachfront sidewalk that runs from Kata to the Karon town area has slowly turned into a real promenade. Restaurants and bars have popped up along the roadside, and in the evening, people walk the whole stretch, stopping for a drink or dinner with the beach right there. It is a very different Karon from the one I first saw, but the character is still the same: long, wide, relaxed, and never in a rush.

One thing that has not changed in 30 years is the sound. Walk barefoot on the sand and it squeaks under your feet. People talked about it in 1994, and they still talk about it today. Nobody quite agrees on why it happens, but it is one of those small things that makes Karon feel like Karon.

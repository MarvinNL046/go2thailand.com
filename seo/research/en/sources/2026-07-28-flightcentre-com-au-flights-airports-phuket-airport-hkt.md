# Phuket Airport (HKT)
## About Phuket Airport (HKT)

Phuket Airport (HKT) is a major international airport and is one of the busiest in Thailand, after Bangkok! The airport is located about 30 kilometres from Phuket Town, close to Mai Khao Beach, where plane spotting has become a popular pastime. Phuket Airport has three terminals, with Terminal 1 being used for international flights, Terminal 2 for domestic and Terminal X reserved for charter flights and to handle overflow.
## Check-in

There are three terminals at Phuket Airport: Terminal 1 (international), Terminal 2 (domestic) and Terminal X. ## Transport and terminal transfers

Looking for the best ways to get to and from Phuket Airport? There are plenty of ground transportation options available.
### Airport parking

Phuket Airport offers two car parks, one opposite the international terminal building and another opposite the domestic terminal building. Both car parks have fixed hourly rates, and the first 15 minutes is free.
### Limousine

Feeling a little bit fancy? Luxurious airport limousine transfers are available at Phuket Airport, near the Arrival Halls.
### Taxis

A range of different taxi service providers, including Grab and CABB, are available to make your journey to or from Phuket Airport a seamless one.
### Car rental

Car rental companies, including AVIS, Budget, Europcar and Hertz, are available at Phuket Airport. [Talk to your Travel Expert](http://www.flightcentre.com.au/stores) about organising car hire for your trip or [book it yourself online](https://cars.flightcentre.com.au/) before you leave.
### Public transport

One of the most affordable and convenient ways to travel between Phuket Airport and Phuket Town is by public transport. The 8411 Airport Bus is an official government bus, providing air-conditioned transportation from Terminal 2 (the Domestic Terminal) to Phuket Town. Look out for the orange buses! Visit the Phuket Mahanakorn website for up-to-date information on timetables and fares.
### Terminal transfers

The terminals at Phuket Airport are connected by a walkway. For assistance transferring between the terminals, talk to the ground staff.

As a general rule, you should aim to arrive at Phuket Airport two hours before your scheduled departure time for domestic flights and at least three hours before international flights.

Wat Plai Laem is a famous Buddhist temple complex in Koh Samui. With Koh Samui attracting hundreds of thousands of tourists each year, the temple of Wat Plai Laem has become a popular visitor attraction on the island.

While best known for its beaches and 5 star hotels, there is much more to Koh Samui than just a good time in the sun.

If you are looking for some unique things to do while in Koh Samui, Wat Plai Laem should be at the top of your list! We had the chance to explore Koh Samui, and visiting the temple complex was one of our favourite memories from the trip!

There are some truly impressive statues on display here, while you also get the opportunity to feed the fish which are found in the pond surrounding the complex! We ended up spending more time here than we initially planned, as it was such a cool place to explore.

This travel guide will explore everything you need to know before visiting the temple, including how to get there, opening times, the Wat Plai Laem dress code, and much more – let’s get started!
## Where is Wat Plai Laem

You can find the Wat Plai Laem temple complex in the tourist island of Koh Samui in southern [Thailand](https://chrisandwrensworld.com/thailand/). The temple itself is located in the far north-east of the island, only 4 kilometres away from Koh Samui airport.
## How to get to Wat Plai Laem in Koh Samui

After arriving in Koh Samui island, getting to Wat Plai Laem is pretty easy – here are your best options.
### Take a Koh Samui highlights tour

The easiest way to visit Wat Plai Laem is to join a [Koh Samui half-day group tour](https://gyg.me/rlBUJmGX). These tours include hotel pick up and drop-offs, and will take you to the most famous places and attractions on the island. As Koh Samui is a fairly small place, it is easy to visit most of the top things to do in around half a day.

The tour includes a stop at Wat Plai Laem, while also visiting other famous attractions such as Na Muang waterfall and the big Buddha temple.
### Get a private tour of Koh Samui

If you would prefer to travel a little more independently, then consider booking a [Koh Samui private tour](https://gyg.me/l8my58Fk). This means you will be able to make your own independent itinerary, and tell your driver where you would like to go, including to Wat Plai Laem.

If you prefer travelling yourself and avoiding crowds, this is always a good way to go. It will be more expensive than a larger group tour, but it is worth it for the added luxury and independence.

The private tour also includes your own guide, for a more personal and unique travel experience.
### Get a private car and driver

For a cheaper alternative to a private tour, opt instead for [renting your own car and driver](https://gyg.me/sGow2jsp) for a day in Koh Samui. You simply create your own itinerary, and your driver will take you to wherever you need to go.

This is also cheaper than opting for a private tour, as it does not include a guide. After arriving at the destinations like Wat Plai Laem, you simply go and explore at your own pace, before returning to your vehicle when you are ready.
### Rent a scooter

If you are comfortable on the back of a scooter or motorbike, then the cheapest way to visit Wat Plai Laem is by renting one in Koh Samui and driving there yourself.

The roads in Koh Samui are in good condition, so you should not have any issues. Most of the island is served by a large ring road which encircles all of Koh Samui, so simply follow this road until you get to the necessary exit for reaching Wat Plai Laem.

From the popular tourist towns of Chaweng and Lamai, expect your drive to take approximately 20 minutes and 35 minutes respectively. If traffic is particularly busy, these times could increase slightly.

## Best time to visit Wat Plai Laem

Generally speaking, Wat Plai Laem is a great place to visit all year round, and there is no specific bad time to visit. For the best possible experience, consider following these guidelines.
### Avoiding the crowds at Wat Plai Laem

As Wat Plai Laem is one of the most popular [things to do in Koh Samui](https://chrisandwrensworld.com/things-to-do-in-koh-samui/), it can get rather busy at the temple complex throughout the day. While the temple grounds are large, the main attractions within the temple – the laughing Buddha and the Guanyin statue – can get packed with people.

During our visit, we unfortunately arrived during peak time, meaning we had to be a little patient when exploring the popular parts of the temple. This meant that we had to wait to get some good photos without lots of people in the frame, which was a little irritating.

While this wasn’t too much of an issue, we would still recommend trying to avoid visiting Wat Plai Laem in the middle of the afternoon, particularly between 11am and 1pm.

If possible, try visiting earlier in the morning or later in the afternoon for an overall more pleasant experience.
### Don’t worry too much about the rain

If you are visiting Koh Samui outside of the dry season that runs from December-February, then there is the odd chance of rain. During our visit, it actually rained the majority of the time!

If the weather is pretty bad, then we would actually encourage you to visit Wat Plai Laem! As you won’t be able to relax by the beaches on the island or at your hotel pool, it is worth doing something a little different to pass the time.

If you don’t have a rain jacket, you can get a poncho from any of the 7 Eleven stores on the island for around 30 Thai Baht!

As it rained most of the time in Koh Samui for us, we ended up doing more sightseeing than initially planned – even if it meant we did not get to have many beach days!
## Wat Plai Laem entrance fee

The great thing about visiting Wat Plai Laem in Koh Samui is that it is completely free! If you are travelling with your own scooter or motorbike, there is also complimentary parking for all visitors.

While there is no entry fee for Wat Plai Laem, there are donation boxes dotted around the temple complex. It is recommended that you make a donation during your visit, which will ensure the temple is maintained.
## Wat Plai Laem dress code

As you will be visiting a Buddhist temple, it is recommended that you dress conservatively for your visit to Wat Plai Laem. While the rules are not strictly followed by everyone who visits, it is best to be respectful.

During our visit, there were people showing shoulders and knees and no-one seemed too fussed by it, so don’t worry too much about the dress code.

We would recommend wearing a t-shirt which covers your shoulders, and longer shorts or trousers for your bottom half. If you choose to wear trousers, make sure they are breathable and can let in some air – Koh Samui can get seriously hot in the middle of the afternoon!

## Opening hours of Wat Plai Laem

The temple is open every day, between the hours of 6am and 6pm. This makes it a very accessible place to visit, especially if you want to go earlier in the morning.

If you visit just after opening time, you will be able to catch the sunrise at Wat Plai Laem. While we did not do this personally, it does sound like it could be a rather serene and peaceful experience.

You will find that the temple is at its busiest in the middle of the afternoon, so make sure to take advantage of the generous opening hours by visiting earlier or later in the day.
## How long to stay at Wat Plai Laem

We would recommend allowing for at least 1 hour for your visit to Wat Plai Laem temple. This will give you enough time to explore the entirety of the temple complex and take some photos.

It is possible to speed run the entirety of the temple in around 30 minutes, however this will lead to a rushed experience.

We personally stayed at Wat Plai Laem for just under 2 hours, as we ended up really enjoying our visit. The sun actually came out for an hour or two which probably helped!

If you want to feed the fish in the artificial lake that surrounds most of the temples, then you will likely end up spending closer to 90 minutes at the temple.
## Things to do at Wat Plai Laem

There are quite a few places of interest within the temple complex, so it is worth spending some time exploring and seeing the cool statues and monuments.
### Visit the laughing Buddha

One of the most famous attractions within Wat Plai Laem is the laughing Buddha. Upon driving up to the temple, you will be able to see the laughing Buddha from a fair distance away. The size of the statue was much larger than we initially expected, so it was cool seeing it up close.

There is a small bridge leading up to the monument itself, which is the best place to get some photos of the statue. It can get pretty busy here, so if there is a crowd, simply wait for a few minutes and you should get the area all to yourself.

You will also notice that the lake that surrounds the laughing Buddha is filled with fish – you will get to feed them later don’t worry!
### Admire the Guanyin statue with 18 arms

Our personal favourite thing to see within Wat Plai Laem is the huge statue of Guanyin, who is known for her compassion and mercy. The architecture here is on another level, and is probably the most impressive sight in the temple complex.

Similarly to the laughing Buddha, the bridge leading up the 18 armed statue can get busy during peak times!

After seeing both the laughing Buddha and Guanyin, there are some other smaller statues and monuments scattered around Wat Plai Laem. While not as visually intriguing, they are still worth visiting.

While exploring the complex, you will also likely encounter some chickens! We have no idea why they are here, however we seen several chickens simply walking around Wat Plai Laem which was rather amusing.
### Feed the fish at Wat Plai Laem

Before leaving the temple, we would highly recommend making some time for feeding the fish inside the lake! You can buy fish food from vending machines around the complex for 10 Baht.

Simply go to the edge of the water and put some food in the water. You will then see dozens of fish racing towards the food! We had too much fun doing this, and ended up going back to the vending machine to get some more food!

There are also some adorable tortoise in the lake which will come for some food as well, so keep an eye out for them.
## Where to next

After exploring Wat Plai Laem, there are lots of other things to see and do on the island of Koh Samui. Make sure to pop along to [Wat Phra Yai](https://chrisandwrensworld.com/wat-phra-yai/) (the large golden Buddha), which is only a short drive from Wat Plai Laem.

For beach lovers, we would recommend checking out Lipa Noi beach on the western side of the island. While it is a little remote, it is perfectly placed to watch a spectacular sunset if the weather is on your side!

Finally, be sure to visit the Grandfather and Grandmother rocks, which are infamous for their curious similarities to certain body parts!
## Final Thoughts

Visiting Wat Plai Laem in Koh Samui is well worth your time. It makes for a great day out, and is one of the easiest places to get to on the island. It is so close to the airport that you could technically visit while waiting for your flight if you have some time!

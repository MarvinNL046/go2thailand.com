## Green is Life. Oxygen Make it Happen.

No Single-Use Plastic

We have eliminated single-use plastic water bottles and provide reusable cups for all guests.

Eco-Briefing & Education

Before every dive, our guides educate guests on responsible snorkeling practices (No touching, No standing on coral, No chasing marine life). ​

Waste Separation

We provide separate bins for recyclables and general waste onboard, ensuring proper disposal and recycling on land.​

100% Organic Waste Management

All food scraps and organic waste are separated and turned into compost, ensuring zero food waste goes to landfill.​

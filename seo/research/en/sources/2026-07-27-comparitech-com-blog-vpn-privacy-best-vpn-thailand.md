## The future of the internet in Thailand

Thailand is a hotspot for travelers, expats, digital nomads, and more. However, [advocacy body Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/country/thailand/freedom-net/2025) emphatically states that the country’s cyber landscape is “not free” with the arrest of bloggers, increased web censorship, and the dissolution of the main opposing political party, all raising severe concern.

Since the military overthrew the government back in 2014, Thailand has been cracking down on dissenting opinion, criminalizing pro-democratic speech and any dialogue that criticizes the royal family. In 2025, [it was revealed](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/04/thailand-authorities-must-end-malicious-smear-campaigns-and-cyberattacks-on-civil-society/) that the government had a task force designed to carry out cyberattacks and harass lawyers, activists, and civil rights groups.

The [Electronic Frontier Foundation notes](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/12/amended-computer-crime-act-and-state-internet-freedoms-thailand) that changes to Thailand’s cybercrime laws are ambiguous, vague, and lack accountability. There’s a real attempt to stifle freedom of expression, especially on digital platforms, and to maintain control of Thai society.

## What websites are restricted in Thailand?

Since 2006, mounting civil unrest and protests have led to ever heightening levels of online surveillance and censorship in Thailand. This has led to over 140,000 URLs being blocked by the authorities.

Prior to 2006, the vast majority of internet censorship was centered around pornographic content. Since then, restrictions have expanded to include content critical of the government and blocks on services, blogs, and news content that express dissenting opinions or criticisms of the government of Thailand.

Websites and web pages that criticize the monarchy.

Websites concerning the biography of Thailand’s King Bhumibol Adulyadej, [The King Never Smiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_Never_Smiles).

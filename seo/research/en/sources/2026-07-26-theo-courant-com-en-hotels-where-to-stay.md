## In the City Center

Staying in the city center mainly suits travelers who plan for just a short overnight stop, with the beach as an optional activity. Honestly, there are few accommodations offering a pleasant atmosphere or the charm that makes you want to extend your stay.
### Near Yomjinda Walking Street:

Madina Hotel

A simple and budget-friendly hotel. It’s a large, plain building without much charm. The rooms are spacious, clean, but lack character. Rooms with a river view are preferable. Ideal for a short overnight stay.

Booking: [Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/th/madina-rayong.en.html?aid=1449528&no_rooms=1&group_adults=2) - [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1797169&hid=283722)
### Along Sukhumvit Road - Near City Center

Holiday Inn & Suites Rayong City Centre

A well-known international chain. This Rayong property is located in the city’s tallest building. Modern rooms offer nice panoramic views. There’s a small but refreshing pool. The breakfast is pricey, but a shopping mall is only two minutes away. A reliable value-for-money option with no surprises.

Booking: [Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/th/holiday-inn-suites-rayong-city-centre.en.html?aid=1449528&no_rooms=1&group_adults=2) - [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1797169&hid=46874760)

Marquise Boutique Hotel

A small and traditional hotel offering comfortable rooms with all essentials. The service is excellent. Guests especially enjoy the pool and cozy bar. One of the best lodging options in Rayong for quality and price.

Booking: [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1797169&hid=3624570)
### Seang Chan Beach

Novotel Rayong Rim Pae Resort

Novotel hotels need no introduction, but this one is unique in terms of its size and location. It’s a comfortable place to stay, just a stone’s throw from the beach. The rooms are large and very comfortable. It offers excellent value for money. Please note that this is a very popular hotel.

Booking: [Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/th/novotel-rayong-star-convention-centre.en.html?aid=1449528&no_rooms=1&group_adults=2) - [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1797169&hid=10940)
### Mae Ramphueng Beach

Elsie Evans Art Retreat

A charming guesthouse perfect for relaxation and art workshops. Cozy atmosphere with a lovely small pool, located not far from the beach. Guests enjoy personalized service in this human-sized establishment. Prices may vary depending on the season.

Booking: [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1797169&hid=31089172)
### What is the best area to stay in Rayong?

The city center is convenient for short stays, while Seang Chan Beach and Mae Ramphueng Beach are ideal for beach holidays.
### Are there family-friendly hotels in Rayong?

Yes, places like Novotel Rayong Rim Pae Resort offer family-sized rooms and play areas for children near the beach.
### Are city center hotels close to the beach?

No, the city center is away from the beach. For easy beach access, choose hotels on Seang Chan Beach or Mae Ramphueng Beach.
### Is advance booking recommended?

Yes, most hotels can be booked easily via Booking or Agoda, often with better rates during the low season.
### Are there any unique or boutique accommodations in Rayong?

Yes, Elsie Evans Art Retreat offers a unique stay combining lodging and painting workshops.

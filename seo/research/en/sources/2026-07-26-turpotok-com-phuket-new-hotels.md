### What defines a «new» hotel in this list?

We include properties that completed construction and welcomed their first paying guests between 2025 and 2026. Major renovations also qualify (see «(renovation)» next to the hotel name) if they resulted in essentially brand-new accommodations or significantly updated room inventory. Hotels that only refreshed public spaces without renovating guest rooms are not included in our lists.
### Are these hotels currently accepting bookings?

Most properties in the tables already operate at full capacity. Hotels with future opening dates typically open their booking systems 4-6 months in advance. Check the «Date» column for each hotel’s specific timeline. Hotels listed under «Opening Soon» are not yet available for booking.
### Which area of Phuket has the most new hotel openings?

Patong Beach leads with 15 new properties, followed by Phuket Old Town (7 boutique hotels capitalizing on heritage tourism) and Kata/Karon (6 combined). Bang Tao attracted several luxury developments, while Mai Khao secured multiple international resort brands.
### What types of hotels open most frequently in Phuket?

Most commonly, these are budget-friendly 3-star guesthouses in popular tourist areas: Patong, Karon, Kata, or Phuket Old Town. However, Phuket also sees annual openings of 4 and 5-star hotels, including properties from international and local hospitality chains (brands like Radisson, JW Marriott, ibis, Wyndham, Centara, and others).

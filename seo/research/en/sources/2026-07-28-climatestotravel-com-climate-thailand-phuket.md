The temperature is high throughout the year, in fact, average highs never drop below 31 °C (88 °F).

However, from December to March, relative humidity is slightly lower.

Whatever the season, particularly hot days are always possible, with peaks around 36/37 °C (97/99 °F), while nights can be a bit cool, below 20 °C (68 °F), from December to March.

From February to April, before the arrival of the monsoon, there is a slight increase in temperature, in fact, March and April are usually the hottest months of the year.

During El Niño years, such as 1998 and 2016, the dry period is particularly hot.

On the coast on the Andaman Sea, where Phuket is located, the rains brought by the southwest monsoon are more abundant than on the other coast of peninsular Thailand, that of the Gulf of Siam, since the south-western winds hit it directly.

However, the rainy period has two peaks, in the first part (May) and in the last (September-October).

During some years, however, there may be torrential rains even in July and August.

The sea in Phuket is warm enough to swim in all year round. It should be said that in summer, the sea can be rough due to the monsoon, and there may be dangerous currents.

The climate of Phuket is tropical, hot all year round, with a rainy season due to the southwest monsoon, which lasts from May to October, and a dry season from December to March.

In April, before the monsoon, there is a certain increase in showers. In November, after the monsoon, there can still be abundant rainfall, especially in the first part, either for any delay in the retreat of the monsoon, or because of a tropical cyclone (see below).

However, there may be some showers also in the dry season, especially in December, when occasionally, some of them can be particularly intense.

Phuket is the largest Thai island and is located near the west coast of peninsular Thailand, at nearly 8 degrees north latitude.

There are, in addition to the eponymous capital, towns like Patong, Karon, Rawai, and Mai Khao. The most famous beaches are Patong Beach, Karon Beach, Kata Beach, Kata Noi Beach, Nai Han Beach, Kamala Beach, Surin Beach, and Bang Tao Beach.
## Tropical cyclones

Phuket, like the rest of Thailand, can sometimes be affected by tropical cyclones.

This area on the Andaman Sea is relatively protected, since cyclones can be formed nearby, but then, they will head towards the Bay of Bengal, and this happens preferably between April and December, with two peaks at the beginning and at the end of the period (April-June and October-December).

On the other hand, typhoons can arrive from the east, but they generally pass more to the north, from the South China Sea to mainland Thailand; however, from time to time, they can also move to these latitudes, which has happened in the last few decades from mid-October to about December 20.
## Best Time

The best time to go to Phuket for a beach holiday is the dry season, from late December to March.

However, surfers prefer the period from June to September, when waves are higher in the beaches exposed to the west, like Pansea Beach, Karon Beach, and Kata Beach.

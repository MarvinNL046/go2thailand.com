# What to Do in Kata Beach?
## Things to Do in Kata Beach

[Things to Do in Kata Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/things-to-do-in-kata-beach/)

Kata Beach has a lot to offer for everyone. Technically, it is a great base to explore the south of Phuket, where the most iconic places are, such as [The Big Buddha](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-big-buddha/) (12 km), [Wat Chalong Temple](https://www.phuket101.net/wat-chalong/) (9 km), [Promthep Cape](https://www.phuket101.net/promthep-cape/) (10 km), [Karon Viewpoint](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-viewpoint/) (3 km) and the many beautiful beaches in the south of the island. If you prefer not to go around but make the most of your stay in Kata, the beach is, of course, the main attraction, but there are a few things you can do for fun.

You can enjoy surfing an artificial wave, playing mini golf among dinosaurs, jet skiing, parasailing, and even surfing during the low season. Kata is a great place to learn surfing as the waves are not too big. A few [surf shops](https://www.phuket101.net/best-beaches-for-surfing-in-phuket/) rent surfboards here and there and can teach you the basics. Spas and massages are everywhere, and you should definitely go for them, whether it is to relieve the stress from work, jet lag, or even sunburn!

## Surfing in Kata

[Surfing in Kata](https://www.phuket101.net/best-beaches-for-surfing-in-phuket/)

Surfing is popular during the low season, usually between June and November. The waves are not big, but the south end of the beach is a great spot to learn. You can easily rent all kinds of surfboards anywhere along the beach and take surfing lessons. Once a year, there is a surfing competition.
## Kata Noi Beach

[Kata Noi Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-noi-beach-phuket/)

Kata Noi Beach is a small bay located just 500 metres south of Kata Beach, separated by a short headland. The sand is even softer and whiter, and the sea is clear with a gentle slope, ideal for swimming during the dry season, and great for surfing in the low season. Compared to the larger and busier Kata Beach, Kata Noi offers a quieter and more laid-back atmosphere. There are only three hotels along the beachfront, giving it a more natural charm.
## Where to Stay in Kata Beach?

[Where to Stay in Kata Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/best-hotels-in-kata-beach/)

Kata is the second most popular beach after [Patong](https://www.phuket101.net/patong-beach/) ; you can expect to find a massive choice of accommodation. There are a lot of small, affordable guesthouses and budget hotels in the centre of Kata, not far from the night market, and they offer surprisingly great value for money. You’ll find plenty of excellent 4-star resorts around, usually with immense swimming pools and well-appointed rooms, better than you would find in other countries in that price range. Then you have the five-star hotels, often with private pool villas and extravagant breakfast buffets, and yet at reasonable prices (unless you come during the peak season, which is December or January).

When searching for a hotel, keep in mind that there are 2 beaches with similar names: Kata Beach and [Kata Noi Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-noi-beach-phuket/) are next to each other. Kata Beach is the main one, and Kata Noi is the little sister, with even more beautiful sand and excellent hotels right on the beach. So, if you really want direct beach access, Kata Beach only has The Boathouse and Beyond Resort Kata with direct access, and Kata Noi has Katathani Beach Resort and The Shore at Kata Thani.
## Kata Beach or Kata Noi : Which One?

Kata Beach or [Kata Noi](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-noi-beach-phuket/) : Which One?

The two beaches sit side by side, separated by a small headland, but they suit different travellers. Here is how I would choose between them.

Bigger and livelier with more dining and shopping – Kata Beach. Quieter with softer sand and more natural – Kata Noi

Pick Kata Beach if you want to walk to restaurants, the night market and nightlife, and you do not mind a busier beach.

Pick Kata Noi if you want a calmer beach with whiter sand, and you are happy to walk or drive a few minutes for dining.

Direct beach access: only four hotels sit right on the sand. On Kata Beach, The Boathouse and Beyond Resort Kata. On Kata Noi, Katathani Beach Resort and The Shore at Katathani.

Swimming and surfing: both beaches are safe for swimming from December to April. From June to November, the sea turns rough and becomes a surf spot, so always respect the red flags.

## The Beach

Of course, the beach is the main reason people travel all the way to be here and choose to stay on this part of the island. The beach is immense (1.5 kilometres), the sand is clear and very soft, and the water has a beautiful blue colour during the high season (December to April). Trees provide plenty of shade to the bay, where you will find drink vendors, beach massage, and a few small but good Thai restaurants. Beach chairs are back and occupy only 10% of the beach (theoretically!), so everyone should be happy.

Renting a chair is easy, and the price depends on each beach, but it is affordable. The staff is always friendly, making sure the chairs are clean and the umbrellas are open when you need them. A Beach massage is a nice way to relax after a long flight or swimming, and usually starts at 300 baht.

Note that the sea is fantastic during the high season, but it can be unsafe to swim during the low season (June to November) even if you think you are a great swimmer! The red flags should be strictly respected, as rip tides are known to be treacherous. However, because of the waves, it has become popular among surfers, and surf competitions are even hosted yearly.

## The Geography of Kata Beach

Kata Beach is 2 km long, and the beach is 1.5 km long. There are two city centres: the original one is in the north, near Dino Park, and the new and more lively one is in the south, where The Boathouse and Beyond Resort Kata are. There are 3 main roads parallel to the beach: the beach road is very quiet because the Club Med hotel occupies most of the beachfront, and therefore, there are no shops or restaurants. The second road connecting the two city centres is lively in the evening, with many dining options and shopping. The largest road in the back is quieter but has cheaper hotels and some good restaurants.
## Kata Beachfront

The beachfront is a short scenic drive along the sea on one side and entirely occupied by Club Med on the other. The road is relatively narrow and offers very little parking. Apart from Club Med, the only noticeable landmarks are [Surf House Kata](https://www.phuket101.net/surf-house-phuket/), Beyond Resort Kata, and a few beach restaurants at each end. During high season, the road is often closed to host a small night market with stage performances.

Kata Centre is the oldest part of town, and it has grown around a busy intersection to stretch all the way to the south Kata with hotels, cafes, restaurants and even a big night market. ## Kata South

The south has become the most active part of Kata, especially in the evening. The roadside is full of places to enjoy an affordable dinner: Thai, seafood, grills and international restaurants, food stalls, and even food markets.

There are 4 or 5 restaurants with terraces over the sand at the very end of the beach for something more fun. What would a holiday be without a bit of shopping? There are convenience stores, pharmacies, and many tiny shops selling souvenirs, summer clothing, beach toys, and luggage.

Accommodation mostly surrounds two long-established hotels: Beyond Resort Kata and the famous [Boathouse](https://www.phuket101.net/the-boathouse/). [Kata Rocks](https://www.phuket101.net/kata-rocks/) is another fantastic hotel worth mentioning.
## Kata Road

The Kata Road connects Kata Centre to Kata South, with Club Med and restaurants, bars, hotels, and shops on the other side. You’ll find a bazaar, an immense night market, and a Chinese shrine.
## Kata Beach Through the Years

I arrived in Phuket in 1990 and worked for six months at Club Med Kata Beach. Back then, Club Med was basically the only hotel on the beach, occupying almost the entire beachfront. Kata was a tiny town with just a few restaurants and bars around what is now the Kata Centre intersection at the north end. The restaurant we used to go to after work is still there: [Kampong Kata Hill](https://www.phuket101.net/kampong-kata-hill/). The south end, where [Beyond Resort Kata](https://www.phuket101.net/beyond-kata/) stands today, was completely empty. The beach was almost exclusively for Club Med guests.

When I moved permanently to Phuket a few years later, things had started to change. More hotels and restaurants appeared at the south end of the bay. [The Boathouse](https://www.phuket101.net/the-boathouse/) was considered a luxury hotel at the time, and there were not many like it on the island. [Ska Bar](https://www.phuket101.net/ska-bar-kata-beach-phuket/), under its enormous tree on the beach, was the fun place to end the day.
## Insider Tips

Kata has one of the best beaches in Phuket. The sand is wide and soft, and the small island sitting in the background always adds something special, especially at sunset. But Kata today is packed. It is probably the next busiest beach resort after Patong.

When I go to Kata now, I head to the northern end of the beach, where a small river meets the sea near the island. The view is great, the area is much more peaceful than the south end, and there are just a few beach chairs. It is a shame they recently removed the small local restaurants that used to line that stretch. They were part of what made that corner feel like old Kata.
## Is Kata Beach safe?

Kata Beach is a very safe place to spend a holiday, just like the rest of Phuket. Even late at night, you will never feel worried about walking the busy streets until 2 am and even later. If you decide to party late, tuk-tuks are always available to take you back to your hotel, as driving your motorcycle after drinking should be avoided.

The only real safety concerns are driving and swimming in the low season. The sea can be rough, and the rip tides treacherous, so always swim where flags allow or avoid swimming when flags are red. If you travel during the low season, booking a hotel with a lovely pool may be a good idea, as the sea is often too rough for swimming.

# 5 Best Restaurants in Ayutthaya

Tabla de contenido

The best restaurants in Ayutthaya serve dishes that exemplify the rich culinary scene of the former capital of the Kingdom of Siam. Shaped by its history as a center of global diplomacy, [Ayutthaya](https://en.hoteles.com/go/thailand/ayutthaya) ’s unique cuisine is a celebration of the freshest ingredients: freshwater fish, river prawns, tropical fruits and verdant greens.

Indulge in freshly caught broiled river prawns served with a spicy seafood sauce, aromatic boat noodles, creamy tom kha gai and more. Don’t miss the desserts! From coconut custard and mango sticky rice, there are plenty of delightful treats to keep your sweet tooth happy. Travel to the ancient city of Ayutthaya and discover the hidden gems of Thailand’s colorful culinary scene below.

### 1. Klong Srabua Boat Noodle

Klong Srabua Boat Noodle

Categoría: Gastronomía en , Económico

Khlong Sa Bua Boat Noodle is a noodle shop in Ayutthaya that’s regularly packed with hungry customers. The cook whips up bowl upon bowl of noodles at a breakneck pace, making the line move quickly and queueing a non-issue here.

The resulting bowl of boat noodles is a chewy and savory affair. Choose your favorite type of noodles and type of meat, both beef and pork options. Diners also praise their satay meat sticks to go along with their bowl of noodles before topping the meal off with some delightful coconut custard for dessert.

Ubicación: Pratu Chai Sub-district, Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya District, Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya 13000, Thailand

Horarios: Tuesday–Sunday from 9 am to 4 pm (closed on Mondays)

Teléfono: +66 (0)81 586 8863

Rak Na Ayutthaya combines Thailand’s stunning ricefield views with stylish wood and bamboo decorations. The result is an unforgettable dining experience over light bites or a full hearty meal. You’ll love the soothing setting here, which is the perfect backdrop for those who simply can’t pass up a good photo op while dining.

If you are in the mood for something sweet, the cakes and waffles are the way to go, while the pork satay and steamed buns will keep your palate happy among other savory snacks. For a more filling option, try the various noodles and fish dishes. Grab a seat, take in the view, and have a good time.

Ubicación: 88 Ban Pom, Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya District, Chang Wat Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya 13000, Thailand

Horarios: Saturday and Sunday from 8 am to 7 pm, Monday–Friday from 8 am to 6.30 pm

Teléfono: +66 (0)65 478 5090

### 3. Ruan Thai Kungpao

Traditional Thailand cuisine elevated

Categoría: Familias, Gastronomía en , Grupos

Ruan Thai Kungpao is an award-winning restaurant in Ayutthaya specializing in traditional Thai dishes. This riverside restaurant boasts an incredible view and a spacious interior with a patio for a unique dining experience.

Regular diners praise the massive, charcoal-grilled river prawns and deep-fried sheatfish in garlic, fried squid in salted egg sauce, tom yum kung, and many more. Ending your meal with some coconut ice cream while taking in the river views is a great way to round up your experience at this popular spot.

Ubicación: 1/2 Moo 4 Thetsaban 10 Alley, Ratchakham, Bang Sai District, Chang Wat Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya 13290, Thailand

Horarios: Daily from 10 am to 6 pm

Teléfono: +66 (0)89 887 0871

### 4. Baan Witaya Marisa

A chill place to relax with coffee and cakes

Categoría: Familias, Gastronomía en , Grupos

Baan Witaya Marisa combines great coffee and an equally good atmosphere to make a great cafe-hopping time in Ayutthaya. But this chic, nature-surrounded cafe brings something more to the table, as their desserts and savory dishes are simply crave-worthy.

Think Thai’s rich coffee, decadent sponge cakes, and other classic brunch suspects, such as French toast and pasta dishes. If you are looking to start your day with something light, go for the Greek yogurt with berries or the refreshing berry smoothie. Many go to Baan Witaya Marisa for breakfast or lunch.

Somtum Sukunya is a go-to spot for vegans and vegetarians in Ayutthaya. Their menu selections feature items that both types of diners can delight in without worries. The clean, bright restaurant is family-owned, and its hospitality extends to every guest.

Meaty options range from spare ribs, minced pork salad, and juicy prawns. Must-tries include their papaya salad or som tum. When facing a plateful of crisp, flavorful slices of young papaya and veggies tossed in sour and spicy sauce, it’s easy to understand why.

Ubicación: 11/7 Maharat Road, Pratuchai Subdistrict, Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya 13000, Thailand

Horarios: Daily from 8 am to 5 pm

Teléfono: +66 (0)89 163 7342

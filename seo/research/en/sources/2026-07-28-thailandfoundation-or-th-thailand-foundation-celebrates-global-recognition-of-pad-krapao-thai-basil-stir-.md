# Thailand Foundation Celebrates Global Recognition of Pad Krapao (Thai Basil Stir-Fry) and Khanom Krok (Thai Coconut Pancakes) by TasteAtlas

Two dishes. Two stories. One extraordinary culinary heritage.

Thailand Foundation is deeply proud to share that Pad Krapao (Thai Basil Stir-Fry) has been honored as #1 among the Top 100 Asian Stir-fry Dishes, and Khanom Krok (Thai Coconut Pancakes) has been named Thailand’s Best Dessert of 2026 by TasteAtlas, a world-renowned encyclopedic food map and travel guide.

These are not just dishes. They are memories passed down through generations — the sizzle of a wok on a busy street corner, the warmth of a coconut pancake fresh from the mold. To see them celebrated on a world stage feels, in many ways, like seeing a piece of home recognized.

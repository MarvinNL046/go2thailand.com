# What to Do at Nai Harn Beach? ✓
## Wat Nai Harn

[Wat Nai Harn](https://www.phuket101.net/wat-nai-harn/)

Wat Nai Harn was once a small and unknown temple surrounded by walls between the beach and the lake. After adding a brand new building, it expanded to become a landmark. Once natural, the lake behind the beach is now a large concrete pond where you can rent some paddle boats, which are fun for the kids. [Read More!](https://www.phuket101.net/wat-nai-harn/)
## Ya Nui Beach

[Ya Nui Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/ya-nui-beach/)

The small but much-loved Ya Nui Beach is on the main road between Nai Harn and Promthep Cape. Visitors can rent kayaks and standup paddleboards and then enjoy a nice lunch at one of the lovely Thai restaurants across the street.
## Rawai Beach

[Rawai Beach](https://www.phuket101.net/rawai-beach/)

Rawai beach, about 4 kilometres south of Nai Harn, is not a swimming beach but is great for fresh seafood by the ocean. You can also hire a longtail boat or a speedboat to reach the nearby islands of [Koh Bon](https://www.phuket101.net/bon-island/) or [Coral Island](https://www.phuket101.net/coral-island/).
## Black Rock Viewpoint

[Black Rock Viewpoint](https://www.phuket101.net/black-rock-viewpoint/)

If you are ready for some exploration and are not afraid of climbing a steep hill, try the Black Rock Viewpoint. The view on Nai Harn is definitely worth the effort, but ironically, you must drive back to the [Karon viewpoint](https://www.phuket101.net/karon-viewpoint/) to find the entrance road. That one requires dedication, but the view is truly rewarding.
## Promthep Cape

[Promthep Cape](https://www.phuket101.net/promthep-cape/)

Promthep Cape is 3.5 kilometres south of Nai Harn Beach. You could walk to this iconic landmark, but it would take about 45 minutes, including some incline. The cape is famous for sunsets, but remember that the place can get quite busy as people come by car or bus, sometimes around 5 or 6 pm, to take a sunset photo.

## Sunsuri Phuket

[Sunsuri Phuket](https://www.phuket101.net/sunsuri-phuket/)
| Booking.com | Expedia |

Sunsuri Phuket is a quiet hillside resort just 10 mn walk from the beach of Nai Harn, surrounded by greenery and a facing the lake. The spacious rooms feature modern Thai design, private balconies, and some offer sea views or plunge pools. Guests can enjoy a large pool, spa, gym, and on-site dining at Sail Restaurant. The beach is within walking distance, with a shuttle also available. Ideal for couples and families, the hotel offers a peaceful stay away from the crowds, close to nature and the sea.

## Where to Eat in Nai Harn Beach?

[Where to Eat in Nai Harn Beach?](https://www.phuket101.net/nai-harn-beach-restaurants/)

Your only problem will be to decide which restaurant you’ll pick for lunch. There are a few local shops to choose from, all nicely set in the shade of the huge trees near the beach. They all look the same and serve more or less the same classic Thai dishes at a reasonable price. Just browse the illustrated menu in front of the restaurant and follow your instinct and appetite! It’s fun and very relaxing. There are also a few restaurants along the road between Nai Harn and Ao Sane beach. Rock Salt from The Nai Harn Resort is the most attractive among them. The road to Nai Harn beach has many excellent restaurants, such as Maggie & Mario’s Pizza, De Renata and Rum Jungle Cafe.
## Rock Salt

[Rock Salt](https://www.phuket101.net/the-nai-harn/)

For something more sophisticated, walk to the beautiful Rock Salt restaurant at [The Nai Harn Resort](https://www.phuket101.net/the-nai-harn/), just at the end of the beach. It’s a lovely place for lunch with a view. For dinner, don’t miss their rooftop bar!
## Samut Restaurant

[Samut Restaurant](https://www.phuket101.net/samut-restaurant-phuket/)

Samut is a trendy restaurant in Nai Harn that serves modern Thai cuisine, focusing on local seafood prepared with local ingredients and spices. ‘Samut’, meaning ‘Ocean’ in Thai, is perfect for a special occasion, and while it appears like a high-end restaurant, it brings a new, fresh and unique touch to Phuket Island.
## Reflections Rooftop at The Nai Harn

[Reflections Rooftop](https://www.phuket101.net/reflections-rooftop-bar/) at [The Nai Harn](https://www.phuket101.net/the-nai-harn/)

The rooftop of the Nai Harn is immense and breezy, superbly designed, and offers incredible views of the nearby bay and island. This rooftop is a place for an unforgettable sunset with a cocktail!
## Summore Day Cafe

[Summore Day Cafe](https://www.phuket101.net/summore-day-cafe/)

On the road down from the cape toward Ya Nui Beach, Summore Day is a cafe, restaurant and bar built into the hillside, with terraced gardens, an air-conditioned coffee shop and a rooftop deck full of bean bags facing Koh Man island. The coffee is surprisingly serious, the cocktails pour Phuket-made rum and gin, and the kitchen cooks everything from breakfast sourdoughs to a Black Angus steak. It opens 10 am – 8 pm daily, with live music on weekend afternoons, and makes an easy stop before or after the sunset crowds at the cape.

Nai Harn is a superb beach with soft, smooth white sand tucked between green hills and a couple of tiny islands in the south of Phuket. More laid-back than West Coast tourist spots, Nai Harn has enough restaurants, hotels, and shops near the beach for a great day and has remained relatively untouched. The protected bay offers great anchorage for sailing boats and luxury yachts and has always been very popular among expats and locals. There is no town nearby but a dozen scattered restaurants along the road that goes north.
## Pros and Cons of Nai Harn Beach

Beautiful and peaceful beach: Soft white sand with clear blue water, surrounded by green hills and small islands.

Less commercialized: No large town nearby, offering a more relaxed atmosphere.

Family-friendly: Quiet environment with beach massages, paddle boat rentals, and nearby viewpoints.

Scenic dining options: Several Thai and seafood restaurants near the beach and along the road to Ao Sane.

Nearby viewpoints: Windmill Viewpoint and Promthep Cape provide stunning views and sunset photo opportunities.

Limited nightlife: Very few nightlife options compared to more touristy areas.

Remote location: Fewer activities and shopping options; most attractions require travel.

Busy during peak season: Though generally quiet, it can get busy in December and January.

Minimal amenities: Few hotels and accommodations directly on the beach, though some resorts offer scenic views.
## What to Do in Nai Harn Beach?

Despite its remote location, Nai Harn offers many things to do. In addition to the beach, visitors can explore several viewpoints, visit a beautiful Thai temple by the lake, and enjoy many local Thai and seafood restaurants.
## Beach Massage

A massage on the beach for 300 baht for an hour is the best way to treat yourself when you start tired of doing nothing. If you prefer a peaceful and quiet massage, there are some real shops near the restaurants. Don’t worry about finding one; they will find you, usually greeting you with the now-iconic “Hello, you want massaaaaage?”
## Nearby Beaches

While Nai Harn itself is undoubtedly the nicest beach in the south of Phuket, several lovely coves and beaches are at a short distance.
## Shopping in Nai Harn

Along the road, a few tiny shops sell the usual batiks, beach necessities and stuff, but the best are the many fruit vendors along the street selling beautiful tropical fruits. Have you ever tried [dragon fruits](https://www.phuket101.net/thai-fruits/), star fruits, or freshly peeled ripe mangoes harvested the same day?

Of course, there is a mini-mart (or two) and the unavoidable [tailors](https://www.phuket101.net/best-tailors-in-phuket/).

As you walk under the tree along the beach, you will find a few more shops selling everything you need for the beach, mostly buoys, beach mattresses, swimsuits, lots of kids’ stuff to build castles, and beach games and snorkelling gear.

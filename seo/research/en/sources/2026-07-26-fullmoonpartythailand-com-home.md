## What is the Full Moon Party?

The Full Moon Party stands as Thailand's most iconic beach bash and one of the most famous beach parties worldwide. It happens monthly on Haad Rin Beach on Koh Phangan island, drawing thousands of travelers from all corners of the globe.

### What to Expect

Once the sun goes down, Haad Rin Beach transforms into a pulsating playground of music, lights, and energy. The beach fills with sound systems, each playing different music styles—from commercial hits and EDM to reggae, drum and bass, and techno.

You will find:

Multiple stages with international and local DJs.

Fire shows, performances, and games involving fire that you can participate!

UV body paint stations.

Food stalls and beach bars.

A massive beach-wide dance floor under the moonlight.

And with the recent legalization of cannabis in Thailand, you will find countless number of weed shops lining up the streets of town.

As of the time of this writing, the party is officially permitted to run till sunrise (6:00 am).

### The Experience

The Full Moon Party goes beyond just a night of dancing, it's an unforgettable cultural experience that brings together people from all backgrounds. The vibe is electric, and dancing under the full moon creates a unique sense of community.

While the party has become more commercial over the years, it still keeps elements of its free-spirited roots, making it a must-do experience for travelers looking for both fun and cultural immersion.

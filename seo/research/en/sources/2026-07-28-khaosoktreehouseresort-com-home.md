## Birds in Khao Sok Thailand

Khao Sok is famous for its wildlife. Especially a large variety of birds can be found here. For example: Sunbirds, chesnut- headed Bee eater, comon kingfisher, Olive Rudy kingfisher, Osprey Eagle, Terek Sandpiper, Brahminy Kite, Great Hornbill and much more.

Khao Sok Tree House offers you an experience of a lifetime: going into the deep jungle to spot (rare) birds.

## Important notes

Khao Sok Tree House offers Tree House Accommodation (room, bungalow) in the Jungle.

When you are in the jungle it can be that, sometimes, an unexpected visitor - birds, small lizards, other small animals - can visit your balcony or terrace. Also it is possible that sometimes some ants or other insects will join you. Take note that you are in the jungle and in the Khao Sok Jungle you have wildlife.

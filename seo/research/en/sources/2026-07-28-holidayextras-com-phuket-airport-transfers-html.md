## A Mini Guide to Phuket

One of the most popular holiday destinations for backpackers, travellers, gap year students and holidaymakers alike, Phuket is an island paradise begging to get lost in.

Known predominantly for its paradise beaches, Phuket offers a plethora of sandy shores, each with their own unique charm and appeal. No matter how long your stay, you'll struggle to have the same beach experience twice.

As well as being a place to kick back, relax, and escape the hustle and bustle of life, Phuket is well known for it's embracing of the decadent side of life. Nowhere is this more apparent than the vibrant and hectic city of Patong. Here you'll find a true city that never sleeps, where you can find a bar on every street corner, and you're never more than a few minutes walk from the party.

If you're looking for culture, Phuket doesn't disappoint. A visit to the magnificent Big Buddha is a must do, if only for the views across Phuket and the ocean beyond. If you fancy getting a little closer to that ocean, don a snorkel or scuba tank and explore the surrounding reefs. Keep your eyes open for beautiful marine life such as turtles, manta rays and even the majestic whale shark.

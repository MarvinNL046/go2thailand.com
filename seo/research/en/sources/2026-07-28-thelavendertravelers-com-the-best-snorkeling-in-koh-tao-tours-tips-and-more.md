## Best Snorkeling Spots in Koh Tao

One of the best things about snorkeling in Koh Tao is how close the island’s reefs are to shore.

You don’t always need a boat to find crystal-clear water — some of the best coral gardens are just a few strokes away from the sand.

Here are a few standout spots we loved (and ones your tour will likely stop at, too):
### Shark Bay

Despite the name, our guide on the snorkel tour told us this calm cove is more famous for sea turtles than sharks.

If you’re snorkeling independently, head out early morning when the water’s clearest and before the tour boats arrive. A good time to spot turtles is around 7am to 9am.

TOP TIP: If you’re staying in Krabi before heading to Koh Tao, where you stay (Ao Nang vs Railay) makes a big difference for boat trips and beach access – [here’s how to choose](https://thelavendertravelers.com/krabi-town-ao-nang-or-railay-beach-which-is-better/).
### Ao Leuk

Ao Leuk translates to “Deep Bay,” and it’s known for its electric-blue water and lively reefs. Even just 20 meters from the beach, you’ll see clownfish and big coral heads.

It’s also one of the easiest beaches for beginners: calm entry and a shallow gradient. We loved finishing our day here with a smoothie while drying off in the sun.
### Hin Wong Bay

Tucked on the island’s eastern side, Hin Wong Bay feels like a secret. Fewer tourists make it here, but the diverse coral shouldn’t be missed.

Because the bay faces east, mornings offer the best visibility. Bring reef-safe sunscreen and plenty of water; facilities are limited but the underwater views are worth it.

## What to Bring + Tips for Your Snorkeling Trip

Packing the right gear makes all the difference when snorkeling in Koh Tao — especially if you plan to explore beyond the main beaches.

Here’s what we recommend bringing:

Reef-safe sunscreen: Regular sunscreen can damage coral, so opt for an eco-friendly version.

Rash guard or long-sleeve swim top: The Thai sun is no joke! These keep you from burning and protect against jellyfish stings.

Dry bag: Keep your phone, camera, and cash dry while hopping on and off the boat.

Water shoes or fins: The coral can be sharp, especially near shore entries. Crocs or Tevas always work just fine for us.

GoPro or underwater camera (optional): We don’t actually travel with a GoPro/underwater camera but many travelers do. Personally we just know we wouldn’t look back at the content often enough for it to be a good investment.

### How Much Does Snorkeling Cost in Koh Tao?

Snorkeling in Koh Tao is very affordable, with tours ranging from $24 USD for a budget option to $94 USD for private experiences.

You can also rent or buy snorkel gear at shops and beaches for around $5–10 USD, then explore sites like Ao Leuk or Shark Bay right from shore.
### When Is the Best Time to Visit Koh Tao?

The best time to visit Koh Tao is late December through early May, during the dry season when the seas are calm and visibility is perfect.

We visited in March, which hit that sweet spot between great weather and smaller crowds — sunny mornings, glassy water, and fewer boats at the popular bays.

## How to make chicken pineapple fried rice

Step 1: Heat oil in a wok over medium-high heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until golden and fragrant. Add the chicken and stir-fry until it’s almost cooked through (about 80%).

Step 2: Add the diced carrots and onions to the wok. Stir everything together and cook until the veggies soften slightly.

Step 3: Push everything to one side of the wok and crack the eggs into the empty space. Let them cook briefly, then scramble and mix them into the rest of the ingredients.

Step 4: Season with light soy sauce, white sugar, salt, and yellow curry powder. Stir well so the flavors coat the chicken and veggies evenly.

Step 5: Add the rice, breaking up any clumps as you mix. Keep stirring until the rice is evenly coated with curry and well combined.

Step 6: Toss in the pineapple chunks and gently mix them through. Add cashews and green onions at the end, and give everything a quick stir before serving. Enjoy as a main dish or side!
## How to serve in a pineapple shell

Slice the pineapple: Use a sharp knife to cut the pineapple in half lengthwise.

Hollow it out: Scoop out the flesh from one half to create a bowl. Use a spoon to scrape out any extra juice or bits of fruit.

Chop the fruit: Dice the scooped pineapple into small chunks and add it to your fried rice during the final stir.

Fill and garnish: Spoon the finished Thai pineapple fried rice into the pineapple shell and garnish.

### Ingredients

2 tablespoons oil

3.5 oz or 3/4 cup chicken, breast or thighs, thinly sliced

1/4 cup carrot, diced

1/3 cup onion, diced

3 cloves of garlic, minced

2 eggs

2 tablespoons [light soy sauce](https://amzn.to/4gLu79t)

1 teaspoon white sugar

1/2 teaspoon salt

1/2 tablespoon yellow curry powder

2 1/2 cups cooked jasmine rice, preferably day-old chilled rice

7 oz or 1 cup sweet fresh pineapple, chunked

3 stalks of green onions, finely chopped

1/4 cup cashew nuts, optional but recommended
### Instructions

Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and stir until fragrant and golden.

Add sliced chicken and stir-fry until it's nearly cooked through, about 80%.

Toss in diced carrots and onions. Stir-fry for another minute until slightly softened.

Push everything to one side of the wok and crack in the eggs. Let them set slightly, then scramble and mix through the other ingredients.

Season with light soy sauce, white sugar, salt, and yellow curry powder. Stir well to coat everything evenly.

Add the cooked rice, breaking apart any clumps, and mix until the rice is fully coated in the curry sauce.

Gently mix in the pineapple chunks, cashews (if using), and sliced green onions. Toss to combine, and serve hot—bonus points if you serve it in a pineapple shell!

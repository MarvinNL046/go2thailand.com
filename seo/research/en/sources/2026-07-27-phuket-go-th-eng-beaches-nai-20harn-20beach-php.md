# Nai Harn Beach

Nai Harn Beach is situated north of Promthep Cape. There is a large freshwater lagoon inland near the beach. It is a popular beach for swimming and sun bathing. The beach is a stretch of sand and sea water with excellent quality. Near this beach, there is a little curved bay called Sane Bay. From May to October, it is recommended not to go swimming due to strong current.

Travelling to Nai Harn Beach

Nai Harn Beach is 18 kms. from town. It is about 3 kms from Rawai beach. From Kata Beach, turn right at Kata Beach Resort and past the Boat House Wine& Grill. Take a left turn, go up the hill and follow the signs to Nai Harn Beach.

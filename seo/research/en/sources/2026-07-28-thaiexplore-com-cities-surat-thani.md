## About Surat Thani

Surat Thani, often shortened to just Surat, is the largest province in southern Thailand and serves as the mainland gateway to the Gulf islands. The city itself is not a tourist destination. It is a busy, sprawling commercial centre where travelers pass through on their way to ferry piers at Don Sak.

Most visitors spend at most one night, usually arriving late by bus or train and catching a morning ferry. The town has a lively night market, a bustling Tapi River waterfront, and good Thai food, but nothing that competes with what lies offshore. Combined bus-and-boat tickets handle connections seamlessly.

Surat Thani's value is practical: good transport connections, cheap accommodation, and efficient island ticketing. The province also contains Khao Sok National Park, one of Thailand's most spectacular rainforests with Cheow Lan Lake and floating bungalows, well worth a multi-day visit.

Historical Note

Surat Thani was part of the ancient Srivijaya Empire, evidenced by Wat Phra Boromathat Chaiya, one of Thailand's oldest temples. The province became an important trade and administrative centre for southern Thailand. The modern city grew as a transport hub connecting Bangkok with the Gulf islands.

Best time to visit: December to April (dry season on Gulf coast)

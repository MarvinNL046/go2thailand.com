## Why Go Scuba Diving in Koh Tao?

Koh Tao is a tropical island in the Gulf of Thailand with dive sites suitable for a wide range of certification and experience levels.

The waters surrounding the island include shallow bays, coral reefs, deeper pinnacles and wrecks. This variety allows complete beginners to develop their skills while giving certified divers opportunities to explore more challenging sites.
### Dive Sites for Beginners and Certified Divers

Protected bays and shallower reefs provide suitable environments for introductory experiences and training dives. More experienced divers can visit deeper pinnacles, wrecks and sites exposed to changing currents and conditions.

Explore our [guide to Koh Tao dive sites](https://www.bigbluediving.com/koh-tao-dive-sites) to learn about locations around the island.
### Tropical Marine Life

Koh Tao’s reefs support coral, reef fish, turtles, rays, moray eels, barracuda, groupers and many smaller species. Pelagic visitors may also be encountered, although wildlife sightings can never be guaranteed.

Visit our [Koh Tao marine-life guide](https://www.bigbluediving.com/koh-tao-marine-life) to discover some of the species divers may see.
### A Complete Island Experience

There is plenty to do when you are not underwater. Visitors can explore beaches and viewpoints, enjoy restaurants and cafés, join other activities or simply relax between dives.

Our [guide to things to do in Koh Tao](https://www.bigbluediving.com/koh-tao/things-to-do-on-koh-tao/) can help you plan time away from the dive boat.

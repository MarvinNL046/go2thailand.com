- **WHO Western Pacific — Five keys for safer traditional food markets:** supports practical market observations around cleanliness, contamination, safe food products, animal contact and personal protection. It does not certify any Bueng Kan stall.
- **WHO — Five keys to safer food:** supports cleanliness, raw/cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or ingredients. It does not guarantee that visible turnover makes a meal safe.

- **Thailand Department of National Parks — Naka Cave Green Season 2026:** supports the broader logistics boundary that protected-site access can be seasonal and controlled. It does not establish a current food-carrying rule; travellers must check the live park or operator instruction.

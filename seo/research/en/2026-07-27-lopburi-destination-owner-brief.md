Date: 2026-07-27
Market: English / United Kingdom
Owner: `/city/lopburi/`

## DataForSEO evidence

- Cluster: 40 usable keywords and 50 competitor domains.
- Primary terms: `thailand lopburi` (480 monthly searches) and direct/province variants (210); captured difficulty remains low.
- Decision long tails cover things to do, monkeys, the current monkey problem, festival, Prang Sam Yot, sunflower season and Bangkok transport.
- All three existing production owners returned no ranking or backlink signal in the captured DFS snapshots.
- Twelve fresh SERPs returned 100 organic results and 63 real PAA occurrences. Worth and things-to-do questions each recur seven times; best time and monkey-population questions recur four times.

## Search-intent architecture

- Owner answers: whether Lopburi is worth it, one-night duration, heritage priority, changing macaque conditions and seasonal-field logic.
- Attractions owner: palace, museum, Khmer sites, Ban Wichayen and verified excursions.
- Travel-guide owner: live train/station and countryside planning.
- Stay owner: old-city access around museum day and arrival station.
- Weather owner: exposed ruins, heat, storms and sunflower verification.

Irrelevant UK sunflower and generic Thailand-budget questions were rejected. Macaque guidance is current-state aware and avoids promising access or animal numbers.

## Competitor and source takeaways

Parsed benchmarks include TAT, Experience Travel Group and Travelfish. Bucketlistly and Reuters body parsing was blocked, while Reuters remains a SERP-verified investigative reference. Competitors often lead with monkeys; the owner instead leads with Khmer and Narai heritage, then explains non-interactive wildlife safety and same-week sunflower verification.

## Design direction

The hero pairs historic prangs with restrained wildlife context. The visual narrative moves to Narai’s palace, then to a clearly optional sunflower route. The dotted route banner connects city and countryside without implying scale. Broad-owner affiliate boundary: no Amazon products; current hotel pricing only.

## Information gain

- Colour is not a heat meter: recipe, chilli type, quantity and kitchen determine heat.
- Let travellers choose by aroma, sauce texture and meal context rather than a simplistic colour ranking.
- Give dedicated green-vs-red and Massaman-vs-Panang comparison paths.
- Explain that vegetables or tofu do not automatically make curry vegetarian: shrimp paste, fish sauce and stock require separate checks.
- Explain the existing-pot limitation at rice-and-curry stalls; heat cannot always be removed after cooking.
- Treat allergy and cross-contact questions as a safety decision, backed by Food Standards Agency consumer guidance.

# English Thailand nightlife owner brief

**Route:** `/nightlife/`

**Research date:** 26 July 2026

## Owner decision

- Keep `/nightlife/` as the canonical English owner. It already ranks for `nightlife in thailand` at position 51 and `thailand best nightlife` at position 42.
- Primary owner: `Thailand nightlife` / `nightlife in Thailand` (UK volume 480, KD 0). The route helps travellers choose an evening type and destination; it must not absorb the much larger city-detail intents.
- Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket and Chiang Mai retain dedicated nightlife owners. Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, Krabi and Hua Hin link to their existing destination or island owners.
- Full Moon Party is treated as a decision section within the broad hub, while date-specific or deep event intent can continue to be served by its dedicated blog owner.

Sexual transaction and price PAA queries were deliberately excluded. They do not fit a broad, trustworthy destination owner and would pull the page toward adult transactional intent.

## Information gain

- Six evening moods replace a generic club top ten: night market, sunset/rooftop, live music, club, beach bar and an optional adult zone.
- Eight destinations are compared by fit, rhythm and return friction rather than crowned with an unsupported universal winner.
- A four-decision evening route makes transport home part of the plan before alcohol or phone-battery pressure.
- Alcohol sales and venue closing are kept separate. TAT’s general 11:00–24:00 sales rule from 29 May 2026 is not misrepresented as a universal bar closing time.
- Full Moon Party is presented as a date at Haad Rin on Koh Phangan, not as the identity of the entire island. Travellers are sent to the official date source before booking transport or accommodation.
- Safety guidance converts current FCDO concerns—drink spiking, methanol, late-night assault, transport and road risk—into concrete route decisions without claiming that nightlife is universally unsafe.
- No fixed cover charges, cocktail prices, universal opening times, venue rankings or fabricated crowd numbers.

## Affiliate fit

- One Klook route appears only after destination, rules and safety context. The live listing must confirm the product, location, start, finish, age, transfer and cancellation terms.
- One Amazon OneLink route promotes a compact power bank as reserve energy for navigation and a booked return. It is not framed as a party gadget and does not replace an offline address, group agreement or sober transport.
- The Amazon CTA is `Check the current Amazon offer`; no fixed price is shown. The link uses `nofollow sponsored` and a visible Amazon Associate/OneLink disclosure.
- No food, alcohol or novelty product cross-sells appear because they do not support the core planning task.

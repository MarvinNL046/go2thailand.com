## Ownership boundary

`/food/nam-prik-goong-siap/` owns the Southern Thai dip Nam Prik Goong Siap / Kung Siap: its smoked or dried shrimp identity, chilli-lime-garlic-shrimp-paste/fish-sauce balance, vegetable-platter service, ordering, heat, crustacean/fish, gluten, halal and safe handling checks.

It does not own generic nam prik, Nam Prik Kapi, Nam Prik Pao, Prik Nam Pla, fried dishes merely dressed with the paste, or every regional dried-shrimp dip. A shrimp-free chilli dip is a different dish rather than vegetarian Nam Prik Goong Siap. This is a traveller and ordering guide, not an independently tested recipe; Recipe schema is prohibited.

## Fact, dietary and safety boundaries

- Goong/Kung Siap is the identity signal: dried or smoked shrimp with a concentrated chew or crispness. Fresh prawn alone does not prove the same dip.
- Dried/smoked shrimp and shrimp paste are crustaceans. Fish sauce adds fish. Cross-contact can extend across mortar, spoon, board, grill and serving bowl.
- Chilli and lime commonly make the dip hot and sour; garlic, shallot, palm sugar and salt/fish sauce can round it. Heat and sweetness vary by cook.
- Shrimp paste appears in documented versions but must not be universalised if a vendor can explain another formula. The owner should ask, not guess.
- The normal service is a small, intense dip with fresh, blanched or cooked vegetables and sometimes other sides. It is not a bowl to eat by itself like soup.
- Classic Nam Prik Goong Siap is not vegetarian or vegan. A shrimp-free version is another chilli dip and needs a separate name and ingredient map.
- Seafood can be halal, but fish sauce, shrimp paste, sugar processing, packaged seasoning, alcohol-containing additions and shared preparation can vary. Halal suitability requires vendor or certification-level verification.
- The core ingredients may not require wheat, but commercial fish sauce, shrimp paste, seasoning, side dishes and shared mortars can add gluten or cross-contact. Only labelled products and kitchen checks support a coeliac claim.
- Smoked/dried does not mean sterile or shelf-stable after mixing with lime, water and fresh aromatics. Use clean ingredients and utensils, protect the dip from heat, refrigerate promptly and follow current product/recipe guidance.
- Never publish a fixed price, calorie total, health verdict, automatic halal/gluten-free status, permanent restaurant ranking, universal heat score, exact shelf life or one authentic formula.

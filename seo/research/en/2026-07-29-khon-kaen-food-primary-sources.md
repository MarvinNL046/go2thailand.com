# Khon Kaen food - primary-source boundaries

**Captured:** 2026-07-29

**Locale / market:** English / United Kingdom

## Primary and authoritative evidence used

- **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Khon Kaen:** supports the city and province as central Isaan context. It does not prove a current restaurant, food-market schedule or specific menu.
- **MICHELIN Guide - A Guide to Khon Kaen:** supports Thai-Chinese breakfast, som tam, fermented-fish seasoning, grilled fish and contemporary Isaan cooking. Named listings and opening details remain live commercial information to reconfirm.
- **Creative Economy Agency - Khon Kaen people:** supports Khon Kaen's traditional-to-modern creative identity and explicitly recognises som tam and pla ra as local cultural and economic assets.
- **Travelfish - Food and drink in Khon Kaen:** provides independent context for Vietnamese-influenced breakfast, central markets, lake food and evening circuits. Its venue list, times and prices are historic and are not republished as current.
- **WHO - Five keys to safer food:** supports cleanliness, raw/cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or ingredients. It does not certify any Khon Kaen vendor.
- **UK Food Standards Agency - allergen advice when eating out:** supports naming an allergy, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and declining a meal when communication remains inadequate.

## Editorial boundaries

- Broad food, breakfast, market, vegetarian-navigation and food-zone intent belongs to `/city/khon-kaen/food/`.
- Named current restaurant comparison remains with `/city/khon-kaen/top-10-restaurants/`; city/province routing, accommodation and attractions remain independent.
- Som tam, larb and pla pao link to their complete dish owners. Kai yang, khai krata and sai krok Isaan remain recognition directions until a dedicated owner exists.
- Sri Chan/centre, Bueng Kaen Nakhon, Kangsadan/university and a current evening market are separate food contexts, not one walkable strip.
- Market names, operating days, venue hours, ratings and prices are live details. Archived source values are excluded.
- All recommended meat and fish examples are fully cooked. Raw larb, blood dishes and automatic safety assumptions are excluded.
- Food safety is observable risk reduction, never a guarantee. Vegetarian and allergy status requires confirmation of fish sauce, pla ra, shellfish, stock, egg and shared equipment.
- Amazon products are limited to a cookbook and mortar because both support the Isaan cooking techniques discussed. Every CTA checks the current offer; neither product is required for the trip.

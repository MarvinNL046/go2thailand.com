## Tourism Authority of Thailand: Chiang Rai food

- URL: `https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/https-www-tourismthailand-org-articles-wheretofindthebestrestaurantsinchiangrai`
- The official article names Northern food signals including sai ua, nam prik noom, gaeng hang lay, khao soi and nam ngiao and connects a dessert stop with the Chiang Rai Night Bazaar.
- Used for durable dish and food-zone context only. Restaurant names, recommendations, prices, contact details and opening hours in the 2020 article are not treated as permanent.

## Tourism Authority of Thailand: getting to know Chiang Rai

- URL: `https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/https-www-tourismthailand-org-articles-getting-to-know-chiang-rai`
- The official article independently supports nam ngiao, khao soi, walking-street food and the distinction between central Chiang Rai and mountain communities.
- It identifies Doi Pamee as a high-altitude Arabica-coffee area with Akha community context. This supports a planned highland route, not a generic downtown-café claim.
- Older market days, venue hours and individual recommendations require live verification.

## Mae Fah Luang Foundation: DoiTung coffee

- URL: `https://www.maefahluang.org/en/author/corpcom/page/2/`
- A current 2025 foundation article links DoiTung coffee to Chiang Rai highland communities, forest rehabilitation and long-term livelihood development.
- Used for coffee provenance and community context. It does not support ranking every Chiang Rai coffee, plantation or café by quality.

## World Health Organization: safer food

- URL: `https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/`
- Used for the bounded food-handling framework: cleanliness, raw-to-cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or raw materials.
- The page does not guarantee that an individual stall, market, restaurant or class is safe.

## Food Standards Agency: restaurant allergy communication

- URL: `https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/22196`
- Used for checking ahead, stating an allergy clearly, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and not eating when communication remains unclear.
- The owner provides general travel information rather than individual medical advice.

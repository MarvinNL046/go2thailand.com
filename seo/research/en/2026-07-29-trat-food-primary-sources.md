## Search ownership

## Primary and specialist source findings

- A 2023 Journal of Thai Food Culture study used interviews, participant observation, focus groups and knowledge synthesis in Borai and Khlong Yai. It documents rainforest ingredients including ginger, galangal, elephant banana and bamboo; a Mairood coastal community with a blue-crab bank; elephant-banana chicken red curry with coconut-flavoured sticky rice in Lum-pang leaf among Chong people in Ban Chang Thun; and bánh đúc as a Khlong Yai dessert.
- MICHELIN's Trat cultural-food feature supports the coexistence of Buddhist, Muslim and Chinese influences at Ban Nam Chiao and the caramel snack `Tang Mae Krob` or `Khanom Nam Tan Chak`. Its search capture also supports the Khlong Bang Phra soft rice crepe `Khao Kriap On Nam Chim Kung` with dried-shrimp dip.
- Travelfish describes the municipal day market, night-market layer, provincial fruit and a range of eastern seafood directions. Its named venues, prices and schedules are historical observations, not current guarantees.
- TAT's Trat restaurant article supports grilled cuttlefish, seafood noodles, `khao pad prik kleua`, grilled banana and salacca as useful dish directions. Named restaurant status, freshness guarantees, addresses and opening details are excluded.
- Thailand.go.th's current 2026 fruit-season announcement identifies May as a durian and mangosteen peak and explicitly includes Trat in secondary-province agritourism. Harvest, orchard access and individual fruit availability still require a current check.
- TAT's destination page mostly concerns the island gateway and is used only to keep mainland Trat and Koh Chang structurally separate.
- WHO and the UK Food Standards Agency remain the primary safety and allergy frameworks.

## Page plan and information gain

1. A unique old-town/canal-market hero that visually separates mainland Trat from island imagery.
2. Four formats: municipal breakfast, eastern seafood table, fruit-and-snack market and arranged community meal.
3. Four geographical layers: town market, Khlong Bang Phra, Ban Nam Chiao/Laem Ngop and the wider Borai–Khlong Yai branch.
4. Six evidence-backed dish directions rather than a volatile restaurant list.
5. One realistic day that makes the distant community branch optional instead of pretending every stop is walkable.
6. Clear checks for shellfish, fish sauce, shrimp paste, pork, halal claims, vegetarian requests, raw/cooked separation and current market operation.
7. Two restrained Amazon OneLink products only after an editorial technique bridge: a Thai cookbook and granite mortar. The visible action must be `Check current price at Amazon`; no stored price, rating or availability.
8. Exact-PAA FAQ, natural links to the restaurant, destination and accommodation owners, and transparent sources.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- Sri Trat in Bangkok, Koh Chang restaurant rankings or a claim that the island represents mainland Trat food.
- Fixed opening hours, current stall locations, restaurant rankings, historic prices or guaranteed fresh seafood.
- A universal recipe, guaranteed dish availability or a claim that every Trat kitchen serves the researched community foods.
- Automatic vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free or allergy-safe status.
- Fabricated first-hand visits, nutritional promises, medical advice, `Offer` or `AggregateRating` schema.

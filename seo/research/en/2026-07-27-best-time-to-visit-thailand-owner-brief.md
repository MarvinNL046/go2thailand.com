## Search intent

The reader is choosing a travel window, not requesting a weather forecast. The page must help them choose between competing benefits:

- broad first-trip comfort;
- fewer crowds;
- lower current travel demand or room pressure;
- Andaman or Gulf beach time;
- festivals;
- family or honeymoon rhythm;
- green-season landscape and flexibility;
- northern heat and air-quality risk.

The winning format is a decision guide with a concise overall answer, season trade-offs, traveller profiles and local handoffs. A second twelve-month climate table would cannibalise `/weather/`.

## Cannibalisation contract

- `/weather/`: climate normals, rainy/monsoon season, month comparison, coast mechanics and live-forecast handoff.
- This owner: best/worst/cheapest/high/low/shoulder-season decisions by travel goal, crowd tolerance and route style.
- `/thailand-in/[month]/`: one specific calendar month.
- Destination best-time/weather pages: local month and operational detail.
- `/blog/thailand-island-hopping-guide/`: route construction after choosing the coast and window.

## Content design

1. Direct-answer hero: the best broad window is a starting point, not a universal winner.
2. Interactive “what matters most?” selector for first trip, beaches, fewer crowds, festivals, family and honeymoon.
3. Three season cards with benefit, trade-off and route cue.
4. A visual coast-switch block linking to the weather owner and island-hopping owner.
5. High/low/shoulder-season demand logic without frozen discounts or price promises.
6. Four traveller-route stories with destination handoffs.
7. Festival planning that requires the current official programme rather than recycled dates.
8. Compact twelve-month directory linking to month owners; no duplicate climate table.
9. Contextual live hotel/activity/transport checks only after the travel window is chosen.
10. Exact useful PAA and sources/method.

## Required corrections to the old article

- Remove “perfect weather”, “calm seas on both coasts”, guaranteed sunshine and a single nationwide wettest month.
- Remove fixed 30–50% savings, permanent cheapest-flight claims and unsupported crowd certainty.
- Remove claims that rain is usually exactly one to two hours or rarely lasts all day.
- Remove fixed festival dates except stable legal-calendar dates; lunar and programme-led events require a current check.
- Replace “avoid” language with route/activity trade-offs and official live warnings.
- Keep visa, insurance and packing links secondary; they do not decide the travel window.
- Do not force Amazon products. Physical gear belongs to the weather/packing task, not this timing decision.

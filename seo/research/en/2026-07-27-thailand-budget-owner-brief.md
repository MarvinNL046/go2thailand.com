# English Thailand budget owner brief

**Market:** United Kingdom / English  
**Research date:** 27 July 2026  
**Recommended owner:** `/thailand-index/budget/`  
**Primary keyword:** `thailand travel cost`  
**Secondary intent:** `is thailand expensive`, `how much spending money for thailand`, `thailand trip cost`, `cost of 2 weeks in thailand`, `thailand backpacking budget`, `thailand budget per day`

## Evidence collected

- Six DataForSEO keyword clusters produced **192 raw records and 160 unique keywords**.
- Ten live UK SERPs produced **85 organic results, 54 PAA records and 29 unique PAA questions**.
- Five full competitor pages were parsed through DataForSEO: Never Ending Footsteps, Starling Bank, HolidayHypermarket, Go To Thailand and Southeast Asia Backpacker.
- Exact ranking and backlink checks were run for eight competing Go2Thailand URLs. None returned a ranking keyword or reportable backlink signal at capture time.
- Historic GA4 reports show visits but very weak engagement for the old `how much 2 weeks`, `daily costs` and `Thailand cheap` blog URLs. Traffic alone does not justify preserving three pages with the same answer.

## Search intent

The SERP does not support separate broad owners for “travel cost”, “daily budget”, “is Thailand expensive” and “spending money”. Results repeatedly combine:

1. a fast answer by travel style;
2. accommodation, food, local transport and activity categories;
3. a trip-length calculation, especially seven days and two weeks;
4. UK-specific spending-money framing;
5. cash, cards and exchange-rate guidance;
6. regional and island cost pressure;
7. a method for checking current prices.

The winning owner should therefore be an interactive decision page, not another static annual price list.

## Exact PAA set to answer

Use only on-intent questions from the captured UK SERPs:

- How much does a Thailand trip cost?
- How much money do I need for 2 weeks in Thailand?
- How much money should I bring to Thailand for 7 days?
- Is $100 a day enough for Thailand?
- Is 500 pounds enough for 2 weeks in Thailand?
- Is Thailand cheap for UK tourists?
- Is food and drink cheap in Thailand?
- What is a realistic budget for Thailand?
- How much cash should I take into Thailand?

Exclude duration-only, age, underwear and generic timing leakage. Do not repeat multiple currency variants as separate FAQ items when one calculator answer covers them.

## Content and product requirements

- Interactive days, travellers, travel-style and route-shape controls.
- Clearly distinguish in-country planning from international flights.
- Separate accommodation, food, local transport, experiences and long-distance transfers.
- Display editorial planning bands as assumptions, not live quotations or guarantees.
- Never claim a live GBP/THB exchange rate. Link to the Bank of Thailand daily reference-rate page.
- Replace stale fixed hotel, train, tour and flight prices with “check current price” CTAs.
- Use Trip.com for stays, 12Go for ground/ferry transport and Klook for activities; mark links `nofollow sponsored` and keep disclosure adjacent.
- Do not add Amazon products: the budget-planning intent does not naturally require physical products.
- Include cash/card guidance without promoting unverified bank products or promising fee levels.
- Treat Thai Customs currency declaration rules as a separate high-value-cash edge case, not as ordinary spending-money advice.

## Information gain

- Treat breakfast as a defining city layer: khao kriap pak mor or banh-cuon-style steamed rice rolls, khai kratha, kuay chab yuan or khao piak sen and coffee are more useful than a generic national breakfast checklist.
- Separate the walkable central market and Vietnamese Clock Tower streets from the evening Mekong promenade and from wider That Phanom or village food. They are not one continuous food walk.
- Explain Nakhon Phanom's table as an ongoing local fusion, not as a purity or origin contest: Thai-Vietnamese herb plates, Lao-Mekong noodles and an Isaan shared meal coexist.
- Give freshwater fish, fermented ingredients, broth and raw herbs explicit ordering checks instead of turning the river view into a freshness or safety promise.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- Fixed prices, ratings, current opening hours, permanent market locations, venue rankings, guaranteed freshness or catch origin.
- Claims that one dish is exclusively Vietnamese, Lao, Isaan or invented in Nakhon Phanom; recipes and romanisation vary across families and shops.
- Automatic vegetarian, vegan, halal or gluten-free status; fish sauce, pla ra, shrimp paste, meat stock, pork, egg, peanut, wheat, soy and shared equipment require a batch-level check.
- Raw freshwater fish recommendations, health claims, nutrition promises, Offer/AggregateRating schema and unrelated Phnom Penh, Chiang Mai, northern-Thai or celebrity-vendor intent.

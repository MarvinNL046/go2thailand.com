## Search intent and owner boundary

The page owns the broad visitor decision: how to behave respectfully in everyday situations, homes, temples and at the table, while separating cultural defaults, venue-specific instructions and current law. It does not own Thai-language study, a full legal guide, a generic safety guide, recipes or destination-specific temple information.

The primary phrasing is `Thailand etiquette`; `etiquette in Thailand`, `Thailand dos and don'ts`, `temple etiquette Thailand`, wai, table manners and tipping are supporting intents. The page should answer the visitor's situation first and avoid presenting cultural habits as universal laws.

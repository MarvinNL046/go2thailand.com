# Chumphon food - primary-source boundaries

**Captured:** 2026-07-29

**Locale / market:** English / United Kingdom

## Primary evidence used

- **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Chumphon:** supports the province's mainland coast, islands, town and gateway geography. It does not make one pier, market or coast road interchangeable with another.
- **Tourism Authority of Thailand - 6 Best Restaurants in Chumphon:** supports seafood, southern sour-curry noodles, dim sum, roti, Robusta coffee, pad Thai, oyster omelette and a Chumphon night-market food layer. The article is historic; its venue list, prices and opening hours are not current inventory.
- **Travelfish - Food and drink in Chumphon:** provides independent context for station-area food, a municipal market, southern curry counters and seafood routes toward Pak Nam and Sairee. Its named venues, hours and old prices are deliberately excluded.
- **WHO - Five keys to safer food:** supports cleanliness, raw/cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or ingredients. It does not certify a Chumphon vendor.
- **UK Food Standards Agency - allergen advice when eating out:** supports naming an allergy, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and declining a meal when communication remains inadequate.

## Editorial boundaries

- Town, Pak Nam/Sairee and Thung Wua Laen are separate food zones. No page copy suggests they form one walkable market strip.
- Southern sour curry, pad Thai, oyster omelette, roti, dim sum, seafood and coffee are recognition directions, not a compulsory menu or claim of one uniquely authentic recipe.
- The TAT restaurant article is useful dish evidence but stale live-operation evidence. No archived opening time, price or venue ranking is republished as current fact.
- Seafood examples are fully cooked. Species, weight, unit, origin, preparation, sauces and total remain questions for the current kitchen.
- Current named restaurant comparison remains with `/city/chumphon/top-10-restaurants/`; broad destination, accommodation, attraction and ferry intent remain with their independent owners.
- Food safety is observable risk reduction, never a guarantee. Dietary labels require confirmation of curry paste, broth, fish sauce, shrimp paste, shellfish and shared equipment.
- Amazon products are limited to a cookbook and mortar because both support the curry-paste, seasoning and noodle techniques discussed. Every CTA checks the current offer; neither item is required for the trip.

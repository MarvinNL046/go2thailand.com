## Information gain and verified layer

- Tiger Muay Thai explicitly publishes a beginners programme covering stance, footwork, basic attacks/defence, pads, bags and conditioning.
- Its current schedule can offer two daily two-hour blocks, but the owner treats that as gym capacity rather than personal beginner advice.
- Sumalee states that sparring is not compulsory and can be joined in the participant’s own time.
- Equipment is gym-specific. Tiger currently requires wraps, 16 oz gloves and shin pads for its beginner class; this is not presented as a universal rule.
- Tiger’s official page provided a dated 26 July 2026 benchmark of 500 THB for a drop-in and 1,000 THB for a day pass, excluding 7% VAT and subject to change. No affiliate price or fixed currency conversion is used.
- FCDO guidance supports written confirmation that planned sports/activities, medical treatment, emergency transport and repatriation are covered.
- NHS guidance carries the heat/dehydration warning layer. Commercial universal prescriptions such as 5–6 litres daily or routine supplements were rejected.

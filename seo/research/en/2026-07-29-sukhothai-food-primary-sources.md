## Tourism Authority of Thailand: Thai Foodie Map 2.0

- URL: `https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/explore-thai-taste-thai-foodie-map-2-0-en`
- The current official food map names Sukhothai noodles, a local long-bean Pad Thai, khao poep, khanom wong and local fish preparations.
- Venue names and historic hours are evidence of the food context only; the owner does not present them as permanent rankings, schedules or endorsements.

## DASTA: traditional Sukhothai-Kamphaeng Phet dishes

- URL: `https://www.dasta.or.th/en/article/294`
- The official regional article supports soup and dry Sukhothai-noodle formats, thin rice noodles, long beans, pork, peanut, chilli, lime and sweet-sour-savoury balance.
- It also supports khao poep or Phra Ruang noodles, chicken solay and banana-stem curry as regional context.
- The article is not used to claim that one exact recipe is compulsory or allergen-safe.

## Thailand Government: Sukhothai Historical City

- URL: `https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/001_02_279?hl=en`
- The government page supports the practical separation between park zones, cycling context, Old City orientation and a waterside-market food stop.
- Its recorded market days and hours are historic reference points only. The guide explicitly requires a live local check before travel.

## World Health Organization and Food Standards Agency

- WHO URL: `https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/`
- FSA URL: `https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/22196`
- WHO supports cleanliness, raw-to-cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or raw materials.
- FSA supports checking ahead, stating allergies clearly, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and not ordering when communication remains unclear.
- Neither source guarantees that an individual market, restaurant or dish is safe.

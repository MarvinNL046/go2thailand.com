## Dominant intent and owner boundary

The page owns **Roti Canai in Thailand** as the Malay- and Indian-Muslim-influenced stretched, folded and griddled flatbread served especially with curry or dhal in Southern Thailand. It explains the close Roti Prata relationship without claiming every regional formula is identical.

Keep these independent:

- Sweet Thai street roti with banana, condensed milk, sugar, chocolate or other dessert toppings.
- Roti Mataba / Murtabak: a filled and folded bread with egg, onion, meat or another savoury centre.
- Roti Sai Mai: Ayutthaya's thin wrapper with spun sugar.
- Chapati, naan, paratha, parotta and other Indian-bread owners.

## Evidence-backed information gain

1. **Curry with roti is a Southern Thai breakfast context.** Michelin's Thailand feature ties the pairing to the South's Muslim population and morning service while naming dhal, massaman and other curries as choices.
2. **Roti Canai is a localised descendant, not one frozen Indian formula.** Malaysia's heritage documentation describes decades of adaptation from Indian roti/paratha traditions into a Malaysian identity; Singapore's heritage inventory independently traces Roti Prata through Indian migration to the Malay Peninsula.
3. **Roti Canai and Roti Prata are close regional names.** Tourism Malaysia uses Roti Canai; Singapore's Roots inventory uses Roti Prata and notes regional names, tastes and ingredients. The page can compare them but should not claim every dough and service is interchangeable.
4. **Stretching and folding create the useful texture signal.** Tourism Malaysia describes pounding, stretching, tossing and griddling; Roots documents resting, oiling, coiling, stretching paper-thin, folding and griddling to a crisp exterior and soft centre.
5. **Plain does not prove vegan.** Institutional ingredient records include wheat flour plus variable egg, ghee, margarine, condensed milk or sugar. The bread, griddle fat and curry each need their own check.
6. **The curry is part of the order, not necessarily the bread.** Dhal, fish curry, chicken curry, bean curry and massaman are documented companions. Meat, fish, shellfish, dairy and shared ladles can change dietary status even when the flatbread is plain.

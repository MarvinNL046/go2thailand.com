## Owner decision

Use `/travel-gear/` as the single English packing owner and redirect `/practical-info/packing-list/` after link migration.

Reasons:

1. Neither route has ranking or backlink equity to preserve.
2. `/travel-gear/` already owns the completed Dutch premium counterpart, so it creates a clean reciprocal locale pair.
3. The current sitewide editorial mesh points far more often to `/travel-gear/` as the packing owner.
4. Packing intent is information-first but has a natural, limited physical-product follow-up; one decision-led owner can serve both without a second thin shopping page.
5. The current `/travel-gear/` product catalogue contains frozen prices, ratings, review counts, remote Amazon images and unsupported product claims. It must be replaced, not cosmetically restyled.

## Intent boundary

- `/travel-gear/`: complete route-aware checklist, clothes, shoes, documents, electronics, toiletries, what to buy locally, what not to carry and a small current-product shortlist.
- `/weather/`: climate and regional rain mechanics.
- `/practical-info/etiquette-culture/`: complete temple and cultural behaviour context.
- `/practical-info/health-vaccinations/`: medical preparation and vaccine decisions.
- `/travel-guides/7-eleven-thailand/`: first local basket and replenishment after arrival.
- airline and operator sites: current cabin, checked-baggage, liquid and power-bank rules.

## Winning content and visual structure

1. Direct-answer hero with a photographed flat-lay and a strict “pack for the route, not the country” principle.
2. Interactive route capsule selector: city + beach, islands, north + city, green season, family and long stay.
3. Printable/checkable master list with progress state and categories.
4. “Wear on the plane / cabin bag / checked bag / buy after arrival” visual decision strip.
5. Clothing system led by repeatable outfits, laundry access and a separate temple-ready layer—not gender stereotypes.
6. Shoe decision based on walking, water and real trekking rather than a universal three-shoe rule.
7. Health and medicine boundary that sends personal treatment decisions to a clinician and current official guidance.
8. Current airline-rule warning for batteries, liquids and luggage dimensions; never freeze one universal allowance.
9. Thai Customs “check before packing” block for prohibited and restricted goods.
10. Three contextual Amazon OneLink products at most: a grounded travel adapter, tested waterproof phone pouch/dry bag, and compact organiser or power bank only where current airline rules are clearly delegated.
11. Exact useful PAA, sources/method and natural links to weather, etiquette, health, SIM/7-Eleven and itinerary owners.

## Fact and affiliate controls

- Do not prescribe medicines, doses or a universal first-aid kit. CDC explicitly says relevance depends on the traveller and route.
- Do not imply all medicines are freely available or legal. GOV.UK notes that legal status can differ and some controlled medicines require Thai permission.
- Do not reproduce a permanent customs list. Link Thai Customs and tell readers to confirm current restrictions and permits.
- Do not guarantee that a particular plug fits every room or that every adapter converts voltage.
- Do not publish fixed prices, ratings, review counts, savings, stock or universal “best product” claims.
- Amazon CTAs must say “check current option/price”, use the existing multi-country OneLink route and carry visible disclosure plus `nofollow sponsored`.
- Trip.com, Klook and 12Go are not natural primary exits on a packing decision and should not be forced into the owner.

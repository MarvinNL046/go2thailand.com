1. Direct definition and spelling note without keyword stuffing.
2. Taste compass: savoury, garlic-forward, basil-peppery and heat that varies by chilli request and kitchen.
3. Ingredient signals: holy basil, garlic, chilli, protein or tofu/mushroom alternative, oyster/fish/soy sauce family, rice and optional egg.
4. Holy basil versus Thai/sweet/Italian basil: identify the intended herb while acknowledging substitutions at home; do not claim every restaurant uses the same leaf.
5. Choice cards for pork, chicken, beef/seafood and tofu/mushroom, with vendor-level checks.
6. Ordering flow including protein, heat, rice and `kai dao` rather than a frozen restaurant recommendation list.
7. Dietary and allergen boundary: oyster sauce, fish sauce, soy/wheat, egg, shellfish ingredients and shared wok/utensils mean vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and allergy suitability must be confirmed.
8. Practical high-heat cooking sequence without exact quantities, guaranteed timing or Recipe schema.
9. Natural links to the English food hub, Bangkok street-food context, cooking classes and related dish owners.

- Do not claim every bowl contains one fixed sauce formula, vegetable list, protein, heat level or fried egg.

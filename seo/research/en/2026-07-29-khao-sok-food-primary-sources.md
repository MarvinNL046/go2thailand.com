## Official and primary evidence

1. **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Khao Sok National Park**  
   https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/khao-sok-national-park  
   Supports Khao Sok's rainforest and protected-area setting. It does not prove a permanent restaurant list, lake menu, package or schedule.

2. **Tourism Authority of Thailand - Surat Thani**  
   https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/Surat-Thani/354  
   Supports provincial context for rice noodles, sweets and spicy curry traditions. It is not used to claim that every Khao Sok kitchen serves the same dish.

3. **Department of National Parks, Wildlife and Plant Conservation**  
   https://www.dnp.go.th/  
   Primary authority for national-park access and instructions. Current rules determine whether food can be carried and how waste must be handled on a route.

4. **World Health Organization - Five keys to safer food**  
   https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food/  
   Primary basis for cleanliness, raw-to-cooked separation, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water or raw materials.

5. **Food Standards Agency - Allergen advice when eating at a restaurant**  
   https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/22196  
   Primary basis for stating allergies, asking about ingredients and cross-contact, and not ordering when communication remains inadequate.

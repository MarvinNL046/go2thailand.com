## Current source checks

### Tourism Authority of Thailand: Ko Samui

- URL: https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/Ko-Samui/360
- Supports Koh Samui as an island destination with cooking-course activity and distinct coast geography.
- Does not prove a live market calendar, named-restaurant quality, current price or seafood origin.

### Tourism Authority of Thailand: Surat Thani

- URL: https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/Surat-Thani/349
- Supports the province context around Southern khanom jeen with curry, seafood and strong Southern flavours.
- Province-wide food is not automatically exclusive to Koh Samui.

### MICHELIN Guide: Wai khua

- URL: https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/article/dining-in/what-is-wai-khua-a-samui-dish-recipe
- Supports wai khua as a Samui-native dish direction involving octopus, coconut milk, aromatic herbs and shrimp paste.
- The owner summarises identity and ordering consequences; it does not reproduce the recipe.

### MICHELIN Guide: Ko Samui restaurant directory

- URL: https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/cchanghwad-suraasdrthaanii/koh-samui_7770005/restaurants
- Current evidence that Southern Thai and seafood cooking is represented across multiple Samui zones.
- Named venues, badges, hours and live selections remain in the restaurant owner and must be rechecked at use time.

### World Health Organization: Five keys to safer food

- URL: https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food
- Supports keeping clean, separating raw and cooked food, cooking thoroughly, safe temperatures, and safe water and raw materials.
- These reduce risk but never justify a safety guarantee for a vendor or dish.

### Food Standards Agency: Ordering allergy-safe food

- URL: https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/ordering-allergy-safe-food
- Supports stating the allergy clearly, checking substitutions and cross-contact, and not eating when staff cannot confirm handling.
- UK legal requirements are not presented as Thai law; only consumer communication principles are used.

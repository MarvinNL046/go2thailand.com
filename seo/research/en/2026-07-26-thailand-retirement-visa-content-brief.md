## Topics all three cover — must include

- Age 50-plus requirement.
- Non-O and/or O-A route distinction.
- 65,000 THB monthly income or 800,000 THB balance route.
- Application documents and permitted duration.
- Work restriction / retirement purpose.

## Topics two of three cover — should include

- Medical certificate, criminal-record clearance and insurance for O-A.
- O-X / five-year route and higher financial requirements.
- 90-day reporting, extension of stay and re-entry planning.
- Application fees and non-refundable payment risk.
- Partner and in-country status-change questions.

## Information gain

- Separate four clocks: visa validity, permission to stay, 90-day address report and re-entry protection.
- Separate three calculations: immigration evidence, sustainable living budget and UK pension treatment.
- GOV.UK does not list Thailand among countries receiving annual State Pension increases; the pension can therefore be frozen under the published rules.
- GOV.UK states that Thailand has no reciprocal healthcare agreement with the UK.
- Explain the Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ 31 August 2025 category consolidation without inventing “new retirement visa rules”; the official notice says it does not affect applicants.
- Provide a document-validity calendar instead of applying one generic expiry period to medical, police and residence evidence.
- Honest skip guidance: O-X is not automatically better; a service provider cannot guarantee approval; an immigration threshold is not proof that retirement is affordable.

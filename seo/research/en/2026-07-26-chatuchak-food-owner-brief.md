## Current factual basis

- Chatuchak Market publishes the full-market window as Saturday and Sunday, 09:00–18:00. Plant-only and wholesale periods are not equivalent to a complete weekend visit. Individual stalls can vary.
- The market’s published section overview includes food and beverage in sections 2, 3, 4, 23, 24, 26 and 27. Sections are orientation points, not a guarantee that a named stall remains fixed.
- MRT Kamphaeng Phet is the preferred food-first arrival in this plan; BTS Mo Chit and MRT Chatuchak Park remain alternatives depending on origin and the rest of the route.
- Chatuchak’s own visitor guidance suggests at least three hours for a brief market sweep and about five for a deeper visit. The editorial food-only loop is a separate 90-minute planning estimate.
- CDC travel guidance says fully cooked food served hot is generally the safer choice and warns that street-vendor food carries additional risk. WHO’s Five Keys are cleanliness, separating raw and cooked food, thorough cooking, safe temperatures and safe water/raw materials. The page must present risk reduction, never guarantee that a stall is safe.

## Search intent

The dominant intent is not a timeless country score. Searchers want three answers in order:

1. whether their route is affected by a current official warning;
2. which everyday risks deserve the most attention;
3. what practical checks and emergency contacts reduce avoidable exposure.

Current-event phrasing is prominent: `right now`, `today`, flight cancellations, war and travel warnings. The owner must therefore provide a dated source check and direct visitors to the live government page. It must never present Go2Thailand as a live incident feed.

The strongest editorial opportunity is a decision system that separates:

- **route-level status:** border areas and any official regional warning;
- **high-frequency choices:** road transport, motorbikes, boats, swimming and weather;
- **situational exposure:** theft, scams, nightlife, drink spiking and isolated routes;
- **health preparation:** insurance, mosquito-borne disease, animal bites and access to care;
- **response:** emergency numbers, documents, insurer and official help.

Use only relevant questions captured in the UK SERPs, deduplicated for meaning:

Do not turn flight-cancellation or geopolitical-news leakage into evergreen claims. Those answers require a live airline or government check and belong in the route-status decision rather than separate stale FAQs.

## Content requirements and claim boundaries

- Lead with a qualified answer: many established tourist routes are commonly visited, but no country or itinerary can be guaranteed safe and some areas are under official advice against travel.
- Show the official warning boundary before general tips. At the 27 July source check, FCDO advised against all but essential travel to specified southern districts and areas within 20 km of the Cambodia land border. Always link the live map; do not freeze this as a permanent route list in unqualified copy.
- Treat road transport as a major practical risk without inventing a tourist-death ranking or unsupported superlative.
- Explain licences, helmet, insurance wording, vehicle condition and weather as a single motorbike decision.
- Cover boats, red flags, currents and monsoon conditions as same-day checks, not universal beach labels.
- Include drink spiking, sexual assault and isolated-night-route guidance without victim-blaming or presenting popular nightlife as automatically unsafe.
- Route health detail to the dedicated health owner and TravelHealthPro; never prescribe vaccines, medicine or antimalarials.
- Use official emergency contacts: Tourist Police `1155`, police `191`, medical emergency `1669`, fire/rescue `199`. Tell readers to save insurer and embassy details as well.
- No self-created country score, city ranking, annual tourist-volume proof, fixed crime rate, fixed hospital guarantee, universal `safe for women/families` verdict, or claim that violent crime is categorically rare unless a directly current source supports the exact scope.
- Do not label a provider safe merely because it appears on an affiliate platform.

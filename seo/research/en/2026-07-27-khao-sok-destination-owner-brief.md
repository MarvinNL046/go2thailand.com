# Khao Sok destination owner brief — EN

Date: 2026-07-27
Market: English / United Kingdom
Owner: `/city/khao-sok/`

## DataForSEO evidence

- Cluster: 118 usable keywords and 50 competitor domains.
- Primary demand: `khao sok national park` (8,100 monthly searches in the selected DFS market dataset; low-to-moderate difficulty across variants).
- Strong commercial sub-intent: floating bungalows (2,900), tours (720), village (320) and access from Krabi (320).
- Existing production owners `/city/`, `/guides/travel-guide/` and `/where-to-stay/` returned no ranking or backlink signal in the captured DFS snapshots.
- Twelve fresh SERPs produced 107 organic results and 53 real PAA occurrences. Recurring questions centre on worth, days, independent access, safety, wildlife, swimming and the Krabi/Phuket route.

## Search-intent architecture

- Owner answers: what Khao Sok is, whether it is worth it, village versus lake, how many nights and route order.
- `/city/khao-sok/attractions/`: activities, Cheow Lan programmes, forest/river experiences and access rules.
- Planned dedicated stay owner: village, jungle lodge and floating-bungalow trade-offs. Until it is rebuilt, the owner links to the healthy hotel directory rather than the legacy 404 URL.
- Surat Thani owner: southern transport and onward gateway logic. The legacy Khao Sok travel-guide URL is not linked while it returns 404.
- `/city/khao-sok/best-time-to-visit/`: rain, activity closures and seasonal decisions.

- Is Khao Sok National Park worth visiting?

- Is it safe to swim in Cheow Lan Lake?

Medical and wildlife answers are bounded: no diagnosis, no guaranteed sightings and no static safety promise.

## Competitor and source takeaways

Parsed benchmarks include Much Better Adventures, Seek Sophie, Wanderlust Chloe, Thai National Parks and Indie Traveller. Their recurring strength is excursion detail; the owner’s opportunity is a clearer decision model that separates Khlong Sok village, the lake pier and floating stays. Official controls use Tourism Authority of Thailand, Thai Meteorological Department and live park instructions.

## Design direction

The shared premium template is retained, with a distinct rainforest route: misted karst hero, village/lake split, wildlife ethics highlight, lake logistics banner and a three-night sequence. No Amazon products are placed on this broad destination owner. Hotel/experience actions use current-price language.

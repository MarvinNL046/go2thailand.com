## Information gain

- Separate Rayong City/Yomjinda, Ban Phe, the mainland beach corridor and the orchard/community interior rather than treating the province as one walkable food district.
- Give seafood an ordering protocol: identify species, weight or portion, cooking method, doneness, sauce, allergens and current price before confirming.
- Make the fruit layer live and conditional: current harvest, cultivar, orchard access, tasting format and transport rules must be checked on the day.
- Treat pak krachap and rare khanom ob bab as researched local directions, not dishes promised at every market.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- Fixed prices, ratings, schedules, market superlatives, guaranteed freshness or catch, guaranteed orchard buffets and ripe fruit.
- Named restaurant rankings, automatic vegetarian/vegan/halal/gluten-free status, raw-seafood encouragement, nutrition or medical promises.
- Koh Samet restaurant coverage, Pattaya comparison intent and unrelated Hong Kong, Italy or UK durian-law questions.

## Information gain and design direction

- Start with immediate need rather than social-media hype: first-night, transport-day, island and allergy scenarios.
- Separate fast packaged food from fresh market or food-court intent.
- Show broad price orientation with an explicit branch/pack/promotion warning instead of publishing a fake live price list.
- Separate store opening, alcohol-sale time and service eligibility.
- Route SIM comparison to the dedicated SIM owner.
- Keep Amazon only in the pre-departure equipment decision. Every product card must visibly say `Check current price at Amazon` and use OneLink; no cookware belongs in this store-visit owner.
- Use official CP ALL/7-Eleven material for store count and services, TAT for the 29 May 2026 alcohol update and NBTC for tourist-SIM identity context.

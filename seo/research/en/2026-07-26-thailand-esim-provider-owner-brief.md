## Current primary-source facts

- **Yesim:** the current Thailand page exposes fixed and unlimited plans; its displayed country plan is data-only, validity begins on connection to a compatible network, hotspot depends on the operator, top-up is available, the existing WhatsApp number can remain in use, and the page advertises 24/7 support. Prices are intentionally not copied.
- **Saily:** the current Thailand product includes fixed and unlimited data shapes. Current Saily materials also expose a separate optional US phone number with call/text plans, so the old blanket “data-only” description is stale. This is not described as a Thai local number. Activation and fair-use conditions must be rechecked on the live plan.
- **Airalo:** Thailand is a marketplace of individual products. Some products are data-only while selected products can include calls and texts. Product-specific inclusions, activation, hotspot and top-up terms take precedence over generic brand assumptions.
- **Apple:** supported iPhones can use a travel eSIM alongside a home line; the device must support eSIM and be unlocked when two carriers are involved. The data line must be selected deliberately to avoid accidental home-line roaming.

## Editorial and monetisation rules

- No fixed provider prices, discount percentages or expiring promotional claims in editorial copy.
- Every commercial CTA says `Check current price at [provider]`.
- Every commercial link uses `noopener noreferrer nofollow sponsored` and placement SubID tracking.
- Affiliate disclosure appears directly below the provider cards.
- No provider receives a universal winner badge.
- “Unlimited” always triggers a check of full-speed allowance, throttled speed, reset time and hotspot rules.
- No Amazon block on this owner: three provider CTAs already satisfy commercial intent, while the broad SIM/eSIM guide owns the related travel-accessory cross-sell.

## Information-gain angles implemented

1. Treat the live plan card—not the logo—as the product.
2. Separate installation from activation and make the validity trigger a purchase criterion.
3. Correct the stale assumption that Saily is universally data-only while distinguishing an optional US number from a Thai number.
4. Compare calls/number, WhatsApp, hotspot, activation and fair-use in one criteria-led table without copying volatile prices.
5. Provide a fixed troubleshooting order that explicitly warns against deleting the eSIM first.
6. Send broad connection-choice intent to the dedicated SIM/eSIM owner rather than duplicating it.

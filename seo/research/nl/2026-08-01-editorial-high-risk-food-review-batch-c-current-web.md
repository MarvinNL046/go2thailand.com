# NL high-risk food review — batch C research

Checked: 2026-08-01. Scope: three ready/indexable food owners.

## Pad thai

Primary intent: what differs between a street stall, restaurant and home preparation. [Thailand Foundation’s pad-thai history](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/th/853/) supports the 1930s context, Chinese noodle/Korat influences and the common tamarind, salty and sweet structure. Its [street-food overview](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/street-food-opening/) supports pad thai’s place in urban street-food culture. The owner now distinguishes visible wok preparation, restaurant questioning and home ingredient control without calling one form more authentic.

## Som tam

Primary intent: recognizable regional/ingredient variations and how to order them. [Thailand Foundation’s som-tam page](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/som-tam-2/) establishes the dish context; its [pla-ra article](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/pla-ra-thai-fermented-fish/) supports fermented fish as an Isaan staple, som tam pla ra and spread through migration. The rewrite separates som tam Thai, pu pla ra, tam sua and alternative produce while avoiding a claim that every vendor follows one recipe.

## Southern Thai cuisine

Primary intent: what makes the cuisine regional beyond ‘very spicy’ and which dishes illustrate it. [Southern Thai Cuisine](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/southern-thai-cuisine-opening/) supports maritime-trade influences, sea produce, chili, vegetables and examples including khua kling, gaeng tai pla and Hat Yai chicken. [TAT Foodie Map 2.0](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/explore-thai-taste-thai-foodie-map-2-0-en) provides current official examples of Chumphon home cooking and curry traditions.

## Safety framework

The [WHO Five Keys](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food) provide the general hygiene framework: cleanliness, separating raw/cooked, thorough cooking, appropriate temperatures and safe inputs. These are not used to guarantee a vendor or diagnose risk. Each owner asks about its own likely ingredients and shared equipment. No dish is declared allergen-free, gluten-free, halal or medically suitable based on its name.

## Description

Grand Mercure Krabi Ao Nang

Upscale hotel for business and leisure

Located in the heart of Ao Nang, Grand Mercure Krabi Ao Nang offers a tranquil retreat with stunning limestone cliff and lush garden views, just moments from Ao Nang Beach. Enjoy all-day dining at Chill Kitchen or savour authentic Italian cuisine at Da Vino Trattoria. Relax by two inviting swimming pools, including a kids' pool, recharge at the fitness centre, or unwind at the rooftop bar with panoramic views. With versatile event spaces, it is an ideal base for an unforgettable Krabi escape.

Discover Ao Nang, Krabi's most vibrant beachside district, where limestone cliffs meet turquoise seas. Embrace the vivid sights, tropical aromas and local culture, with island-hopping adventures and a lively coastal energy that inspires lasting memories.

Welcome to Grand Mercure Krabi Ao Nang, where Southern Thai charm meets modern comfort. Just steps from the beach, enjoy limestone vistas, sparkling pools, fun Kids Club and an enchanting stay with attentive, cultured service. - David, General Manager

Patoo P. Couples
- 7/16/2026 Confirmed reviews ALL

We were not terribly impressed with the layout of the hotel, with several buildings, (and several stairs or elevators required) to navigate to our allocated room. Whilst being initially escorted to our room, I did notice that several rooms were undergoing some kind of construction or renovation, which was surprising for a hotel of this standard which (apparently) only opened 7 months ago. The same with the bar & restaurant 'Typsy' which was also full of workmen inside too. I realise that due to space constraints Accor have / had in this location, there is little choice in the design of the hotel layout, but just saying that it wasn't to our taste.
First impressions on opening the door of our allocated room were good. Light, airy and well furnished. But:
The balcony sliding glass door had the upper weather seal literally hanging down from the door rail. At first we thought it was a snake, but closer inspection showed approximately 6-8 inches of seal displaced.
The hanging lights at either side of the bed have been placed at head height and I lost count how many times I hit my head on the light, particularly when trying to use the telephone next to the bed.
The bottle of hair conditioner in the shower was empty when we checked into the room, and we had to call to request it was filled up.
The coffee sachets in the room are 3in1 (only), even though the hotel also supplies packets of sugar too. You can easily add more sugar if you have a sweet tooth, but you cannot remove sugar from 3in1 coffee.
The tea and coffee in the room, was NOT replenished by Housekeeping during daily cleaning the next day.
Also, during daily cleaning the next day, the waste bin next to the refrigerator was NOT emptied and still contained the stuff we had thrown away the day before.
When using the room shower for the first time after checking in, we were pretty surprised and disappointed to see a thin layer of yellow soap scum on the lower walls and floor of the tiles near to the actual shower, particularly in the corners. To prove how easy it is to clean this soap scum off - I used one of the hotel's toothbrushes to clean the tiles and grout and restore it to its natural colour. It only took a few seconds but isn't really something one expects to have to do when checking into a hotel of this standard. I do have a phone video clip if anyone would like to see it. On the first day in the room, I dropped a few coins from my pocket, one of which managed to roll behind the bedside cabinet where the telephone sits on. As I went to retrieve my coins, what I saw behind the cabinet was 'disgusting'. I can't be sure if what I saw amongst the piles of dirt was actually insect droppings but it certainly looked like it, and then my eyes wandered to behind, and under the bed - which was just as bad and even had a plastic bag under the bed mixed in with all the dust and dirt. One of the things in the room that concerned me was the placement of the room's smoke detector about 50cm in front of the air-conditioner vents. It is normal to install a smoke detector in the centre of a room and/or at the highest point of the ceiling, to be able to detect smoke from all areas of the room, and accounting for the fact that smoke rises. To place a smoke detector less than 2 feet away from an air conditioning vent which could affect the smoke detection capabilities is extremely 'surprising'. And whilst looking at this situation I also noticed that the normal flashing LEDs on the detector to show it is working / has power, were not flashing. This could mean that there is either no power to the detector OR that is malfunctioning. I looked at several other smoke detectors in the hotel in various buildings and all of them seemed to be of the same type/design and all of them had working (flashing) green LED's which flashed approximately every minute or so - unlike the one in our room.
I also noticed that something HAD been fitted in the exact centre of our room at some stage (above the bed) but had since been removed and a blanking plate fitted in its place. Could this be where the smoke detector was originally fitted, I wonder?
On the first day of our stay, my partner decided to spend an hour or so at the hotel pool. I'm not much of a pool person so elected to lie on the sun lounger instead whilst she got her swimming exercise in. It is normal to shower before swimming, but we couldn't see any shower at or near the pool, although we were told there IS one.
I noticed that on the surface of the water close to the entry/exit stairs, there was something floating on the surface of the water which looked like a film of sand or perhaps even soil/compost from the trees and shrubs around. Closer investigation revealed this yellow substance to have thinly coated the surface of the tiles and tile edging near the top of the pool water. I concluded (rightly or wrongly) that this was similar to the soap scum that I had discovered in the room shower, and to see if I was correct, I ran my finger over the tiles and it was like drawing a picture with a crayon on the tiles, and where my finger went over the tiles, the white of the tile and tile edging strips was exposed underneath. (See above Re "pre swim shower") I have never seen this in a swimming pool before, but it confirmed to me that I will never be setting foot in that swimming pool for sure, I can't imagine that stuff going into your mouth as you swim, play and have fun in the water.
Finally, and possibly the most serious.
On our first (and only) full day at your hotel, we had planned to have breakfast at the hotel and then go off to one of the islands for a boat day trip.
We went down to the breakfast buffet around 08…. The selection was impressive.
After a delightful breakfast and a couple of cups of coffee, we went back up to our room to plan where we would go that day. Around 45 minutes after getting back to the room, I began to feel some discomfort in my stomach and assumed I merely had some trapped wind or similar. After another 45 minutes or so, I began to take up residence in the toilet and knew something was not right. We decided to put the day trip plan on hold for a while and see how I was feeling. By 11.30am I had really strong stomach pains and even began discussing if I might need medical attention. In the early afternoon I began vomiting and by this point I was completely drained of energy. We ventured out to find a pharmacy and found one near the hotel. I was given medicines to try to stop the vomiting and stop the pain, and told that if it continued, I should seek emergency care at a hospital. After taking the given medicines I slept for several hours and felt disappointed that our short stay in Krabi had been spent mostly in a hotel room. I suggested to my partner that we go to walk or swim or something - just to get out of the room, as we had been in the room all day, so we went down to the pool area where she had a drink at the pool bar whilst I lay on the sun bed and slept (again). We were due to leave the next day back to Bangkok and had to make a decision to carry on with the flight or to stay in Krabi until I felt better. I insisted to get on the plane the next day, back to Bangkok, which is home territory and then, if still feeling bad - to seek further medical treatment then. Our flight was in the afternoon so we asked the front desk if we could have a late check-out "as I was sick and didn't want to be far from a toilet or somewhere to lie down". We were told that a 1pm checkout (1 hr late) was NOT possible as "the hotel was busy and had many check-ins that day", but - they would allow us an extra 30 minutes!
After landing in Bangkok, we went directly to the Hospital. I spent several hours being given treatment and was told that I had "a severe bacterial infection caused by something I had digested". The only thing I had eaten at all in the last 48 hours was the hotel breakfast the previous morning.

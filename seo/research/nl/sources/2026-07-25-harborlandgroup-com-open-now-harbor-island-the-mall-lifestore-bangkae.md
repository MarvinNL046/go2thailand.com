# Open Now Harbor Island The Mall Lifestore Bangkae

Get ready for a splashing summer of non-stop fun! Harbor Island proudly presents its second branch on the 3rd floor of The Mall Lifestore Bangkae, thoughtfully designed under the theme “Aqua World – Theมหานคร of Joy, Wonders Beneath the Sea.” Spanning over 11,000 square meters and featuring the largest variety of water slides in Thailand, the park officially opens on 6 March 2026.

Harbor Island is designed and manufactured in accordance with European EN-1069 and EN-13451 standards. Safety and ride quality are our top priorities, ensuring that everyone enjoys a truly unforgettable experience. Every attraction is meticulously crafted for all genders and all ages, making it the perfect destination for families and groups of friends seeking excitement in Bangkok.

The water park features 9 thrilling zones, including:

Super Island

Experience the massive Aqua Tower featuring up to 18 different slides, each delivering a unique adrenaline rush for kids, teens, and thrill-seekers alike. Highlights include Super Extreme No Limits, an ultra-speed slide racing through twisting tracks at limitless velocity; Super Maelstrom, a swirling storm slide plunging riders into the heart of a giant vortex; Super Vertical Velocity, a near-weightless free-fall “Kamikaze” drop at a 66-degree angle; Super Cliff Diver, a slightly less daring but still thrilling 50-degree plunge; Monsoon Falls, a high-speed slide shooting through a ring of fire before splashing into the pool below; and Turbulence, a spiraling mega-slide enhanced with spectacular lighting effects.

Little Island

The largest Spray Park in Bangkok, specially designed for children and family-friendly fun.

Wave Island

Float freely in the artificial sea and ride the joyful rolling waves.

Lazy Island

Drift along a fun-filled adventure river stretching over 210 meters.

Jungle Island

A dynamic adventure zone where kids conquer exciting challenges with brand-new attractions such as Joy Journey, a large outdoor playground featuring 8 slide designs; Monkey Jumper, an exhilarating bungee trampoline experience; Eagle Glider, a 14-meter outdoor zipline adventure; and Explorer Trail, a Low Rope Course with 7 obstacle stations to complete.

Sky Rider

For true thrill-seekers ready to test their courage—soar 13 meters above the horizon on Thailand’s longest sky rail track, stretching over 130 meters.

Toys Island

A water play zone designed to enhance children’s developmental skills with a strong focus on world-class safety standards.

Sand Island

An interactive sand play zone that promotes fine motor skills, concentration, creativity, and joyful learning through play.

Art Island

Unleash creativity through engaging arts, crafts, and DIY activities that enhance focus and imagination.

## Stay Extension for Thailand Privilege Member

A member wishing to extend their stay permission can apply at the provincial immigration office in their residence area. The immigration officer will extend the stay permission in accordance with the member’s visa. The processing fee is THB 1,900*, and members can apply for an extension up to 30 days in advance.

For Thailand Privilege Members, the stay extension process can be completed at the immigration office in their residential area. Members are required to be physically present at the immigration office for a picture and fingerprint scan, conducted in the presence of the immigration officer.

*The price is subject to change by the Immigration Bureau

Documents needed are as follows

Your Passport: Ensure it is valid and contains the required information.

Photocopies: 2 photocopies of specific pages in your passport with your signature on all copies: Front page showing name, surname, and passport number. / Current Thailand Elite visa. / Latest entry stamp and all extension stamps in the latest visit (if any).

Photos: 2 recent passport-size photos (2 inches or 4x6 cm).

## Overstaying in Thailand: Important Information

Overstaying occurs when a foreigner resides in Thailand beyond the permitted duration specified by their visa and entry stamp.

If you accumulate a significant overstay record and voluntarily report to authorities, you may be required to appear before a judge in court. It's crucial to note that this process also entails overstay fines.

In cases of prolonged overstay, individuals could be detained at the Immigration Detention Center until they arrange a direct flight back to their home country.

A small fee is typically required for immigration officers to escort you to the airport, guide you through Immigration, stamp your passport with a blacklist mark, and accompany you to the gate. Your passport will be handed to cabin crew on the flight and returned upon arrival in your home country.

Members with a Privilege Entry (PE) Visa will be granted a 1-year extension with each application.

Consequences of Overstaying

Repeated violations of overstaying carry serious consequences. If you have multiple overstays, your passport may be stamped, declaring you in violation of Thailand's immigration laws and regulations. This stamp categorizes you as an "undesirable alien," potentially complicating travel and entry to other countries.

According to the Immigration Bureau, the following are the advisable and required practice and regulations to be complied with by foreigners seeking a stay in the Kingdom of Thailand.

Once arrived in the Kingdom of Thailand, a foreigner shall not stay in the Kingdom longer than the period of a stay permit.

A foreigner who overstayed in the Kingdom of Thailand before the order’s date of enforcement of March 20th, B.E. 2559 (2016) can surrender to the authorities at the immigration checkpoints before being allowed to leave the Kingdom and pays a fine of 500/day, but not exceeding 20,000 baht.

In the event that a foreigner overstayed in the Kingdom of Thailand and failed to leave the country before the order’s date of enforcement of March 20th, B.E. 2559 (2016), the foreigner will face a fine of 500 baht/day, but not exceeding 20,000 baht, and will be banned from re-entering the Kingdom of Thailand for the respective period as follows.

The prohibition of entry starts from the day the alien departs from the country.

### Should I check the arrival stamp when I enter to Thailand?

Answer: Please check arrival stamp every time. Immigration will put the entry stamp from the following details

If an incorrect stamp is placed on your passport, please notify either immigration bureau or our EPA (Elite Personal Assistance) immediately before departing the airport. Failing to address this issue may necessitate a return to the airport to rectify the stamp with immigration. Although this process can be time-consuming and inconvenient, it's mandatory to resolve it before the expiration of the stay permission stamped in your passport. Otherwise, it could result in an overstay record being recorded against you.

### What is TM.30 notification (Notification of residence for foreigners)

Answer: Please be advised that the TM.30 is the most commonly required document in order to contact the immigration bureau regarding other issues. The TM.30 form is also called “Notification from Housemaster, Owner or the Possessor of the residence where alien has stayed”.

According to section 38 of the 1979 Immigration Act, “The householder, the house owner or the possessor of a dwelling place or the hotel manager, who takes in, as a resident, an alien with permission to temporarily stay in the Kingdom, shall notify the competent official at the immigration bureau located in the locality in which the house, dwelling place or hotel is located within 24 hours from the time the alien has taken the residence. If there is no immigration bureau located in that locality, the police office at a police station of that locality jurisdiction shall be notified.

In case where the house, dwelling place or hotel where the alien is staying under paragraph one is located in Bangkok Metropolis, such notification shall be made to the competent official at the Immigration Division1.

There are 3 ways of notification as convenient as possible:

- In person or by an authorized person at the respective office

- By registered mail

- Via the internet through https://tm30.immigration.go.th/tm30/#/login

*** Please note that Thailand Privilege’s staff is not responsible for reporting or submitting the TM.30 on the landlord's or the member’s behalf refer to the information above***

### How can I apply residence certificate?

Please be aware that a residence certificate is a letter confirming your address in Thailand that can be obtained from either your home embassy in Thailand or the immigration bureau nearest to your address.

You can actually obtain this document at the immigration residing your residence if you have done a 90-day report from your latest arrival in Thailand.

- The embassy of your country in Thailand (Please check with your embassy to see if they are still able to issue this letter as some embassy cannot do that)

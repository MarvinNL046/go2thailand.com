### What are floating bungalows on Khao Sok Lake?

Floating bungalows — also called raft houses — are traditional Thai-style accommodation built on bamboo or wooden platforms anchored on the surface of Cheow Lan Lake inside Khao Sok National Park. They range from rustic open-air bamboo rooms to modern en-suite bungalows and fully private luxury teak villas. All are accessible only by boat.
### How many floating bungalows are there on Khao Sok Lake?

There are 14 floating bungalow operations on Cheow Lan Lake, spread across the lake’s various inlets — known as klongs. Khao Sok Lake Co., Ltd. works with all 14 and is the only operator physically based on the lake, giving us direct relationships and real-time availability across every option.
### What is the difference between bamboo and luxury floating bungalows?

Bamboo raft houses are the original, authentic experience — simple open-air rooms on bamboo platforms, shared bathrooms, and direct exposure to the jungle sounds and lake breeze. Luxury floating villas such as the Panvaree are fully private, air-conditioned teak structures with en-suite bathrooms, private decks, and premium bedding. Between these two extremes sit modern and superior tiers offering varying levels of comfort. We offer all four tiers — see above to compare.
### Can I book a floating bungalow without a tour?

No. All floating bungalows on Cheow Lan Lake are only accessible by boat, and all guests must travel with a licensed operator. Independent access to the raft houses is not possible — the lake is inside a national park and all activity requires a TAT-licensed guide. Booking through Khao Sok Lake Co., Ltd. includes boat transfers, all meals, and a licensed local guide throughout your stay.
### How much do Khao Sok floating bungalows cost?

Prices vary significantly by tier. Bamboo group tours are the most affordable entry point. Private bamboo (Bamboo Bliss), modern (Modern Comforts), and superior (Lake Superior) options sit in the mid-range. Luxury teak villa experiences (Luxury on the Lake — Panvaree) are at the premium end. Contact us directly for current pricing, or browse our individual tour pages for full details.
### When is the best time to stay in a Khao Sok floating bungalow?

The dry season — December to April — offers the clearest water, the best cave trekking conditions, and the most reliable weather. The wet season (May to November) brings lush green jungle and excellent wildlife sightings, with occasional rain. The floating bungalows operate year-round. Peak season is December to February — book well in advance for those dates.
### How do I get to the floating bungalows on Khao Sok Lake?

All guests travel by longtail boat from Ratchaprapha Pier, approximately 65km east of Khao Sok village. The pier is roughly 2.5 hours from Phuket, 3 hours from Krabi, and 1.5 hours from Khao Sok village. We can arrange transfers from all major southern Thailand hubs — contact us for details.
### Is it safe to swim in Khao Sok Lake?

Yes. The lake water is clean and swimming near the raft houses is common and safe. There are no dangerous currents. Small freshwater fish may occasionally nibble — harmless and very much part of the experience.

Vibrant night in Hua Hin

The darkness is about to fall in Hua Hin, but the night market glows and turns vibrant. The crowd flocks to the place, roaming for delicacies among the local touches.

When visit the resort city of Hua Hun in Prachuap Khiri khan, Hua Hin Night Market is a fascinating place you should not miss. Along the short road, which turns into a pedestrian street, you will find hundreds of stalls offering yummy food which is cooked and served to you right away, from deep fried insects to durian. Moreover, just behind these outlets that line the road are a number of restaurants which show the fresh catches of the day and ready to cook whatever you select. Khaimuk, Rod Fai Restaurant and Ko Seafood are on the popular list.

Complete your dinner with local desserts is a delight. As the market has plenty of sweet treats to lure you. Mango and sticky rice is a famous sweet of Hua Hin. Meanwhile, J Tub Sweet serves Thai style iced dessert with syrup. The “72 Crepe” the first and only crepe vendor in the market place, is also worthwhile your visit.

Located in the heart of Hua Hin bustling district, Hua Hin Night Market is on Dechanuchit Road. It stretches from Phetchakasem Road to the Railway Road. The market is on from 5pm and last until 11pm, daily.

Besides the great variety of food, you will find yourself among countless choices of local crafts, art, souvenirs and fashion items. You cannot claim you have been in Hua Hin until exploring its night market.

How to get there : Hua Hin Night Market is situated right by the Phetchakasem Road, in the downtown. It starts daily from 5pm onwards.

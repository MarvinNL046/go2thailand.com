# Sections at Chatuchak Market

With Chatuchak Market spanning across 25 acres and 15,000 stalls, organisers have separated the weekend market into sections for the convenience of tourists and store holders.

The market itself is divided up into 26 sections where you can find everything from the latest fashion to Thai antiques. The sections are numbered and often contain similar shops inside them. Many store holders will use sections and ‘sois’ meaning roads to distinguish where their shop is on a map.

The sections are as follows:

Clothing and accessories (sections 2–6, 10–26)

Handicrafts (sections 8–11)

Ceramics (sections 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 25)

Furniture and home decor (sections 1,3,4,7,8)

Food and beverage (sections 2, 3, 4, 23, 24, 26, 27)

Plants and gardening (sections 3, 4)

Art and galleries (section 7)

Pets and accessories (sections 8, 9, 11, 13)

Books (sections 1, 27)

Antiques and collectables (sections 1, 26)

Miscellaneous and used clothing (sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 22, 25, 26)

This map also contains where you can find toilets, information centres, first aid and banks. We advise everyone travelling to Bangkok Market to download this before entering the market as many tourists get lost in the vicinity.

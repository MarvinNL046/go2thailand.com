(aseanrecords.com) Harbor Island is a massive rooftop water park located on the 3rd floor of The Mall Lifestore Bangkapi in Thailand, covering over 10,000 square meters. Often cited as Thailand's largest and sometimes referenced as the largest in ASEAN, it features 7–9 fun zones with numerous slides, a lazy river, and a spray park.

First opened in February 2025, Harbor Island is one of Thailand’s newest – and biggest – rooftop water parks. Located on the 3rd floor of The Mall Lifestore, Bangkapi, the space is divided between Harbor Island and Harborland. Separate tickets can be purchased physically at the park for both attractions.

Harbor Island features 7 zones of varying thrill intensities that adhere to European safety standards, ensuring a safe experience for anyone aged 2 and above. Although the park does feature some kiddy zones, it has a good range of thrill rides like free falls and vortexes, so it’s not just exclusive to kids.

7 Action-Packed Zones for Endless Fun!

Super Island: Experience the thrill of a massive Aqua Tower featuring 18 unique slides that range from kid-friendly to extreme adventures. Must-try attractions include:

Super Crazy Kraken – Twist and turn through the tentacles of a giant octopus slide!

• Super Twister – Dive into a swirling rainbow vortex for an electrifying ride.

• Super Free Fall – Feel weightless on Thailand’s steepest enclosed Kamikaze Slide at 66° incline!

• Super Plunge – Brave the open Kamikaze Slide with a 51° drop.

• Mako Rush – Speed through fiery rings before plunging into the water.

• Vortex – Spin through dazzling light and sound effects on this spiraling slide.

Little Island: Bangkok’s largest Spray Park, designed for kids and families to splash and play together.

Lazy Island: Drift through 200 meters of interactive water effects and scenic adventure. Jungle Island: An outdoor playground with exciting obstacle challenges for all ages.

Sky Rider: Soar through the air on Thailand’s longest sky track – an 8-meter-high, 100-meter-long aerial glider!

Toys Island: A water play zone fostering child development, built to the highest global safety standards.

Art Island: Get creative with crafts, DIY projects, and artistic activities that enhance focus and imagination.

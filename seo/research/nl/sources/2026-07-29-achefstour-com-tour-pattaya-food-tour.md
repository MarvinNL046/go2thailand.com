# Pattaya food tour

If you want to know what Pattaya really tastes like, you have to look where the locals are fuelling up. Beyond the Pattaya of postcards; come to Naklua, a 100-year-old traditional fishing community where you can escape the neon nightlife and discover a different side of the city.

Begin at the century-old market, where our foodie guide will navigate the bustling stalls to see the fresh catch brought in from the boats bobbing in the harbour. Tuck into a bowl of kuay jub rolled noodles, swimming in a dark, aromatic broth that carries a bold black pepper kick.

Welcome to 'Old Pattaya' - a place of wooden shopfronts and narrow alleys where family lineages stretch back further than the first tourist resort. Watch clouds of steam rise from backstreets marked by steamers with space-age lids, hiding the fluffy moo daeng pork buns.

The pace quickens as we hunt down the street-level essentials. From fish cake skewers charred over coals to pan-fried gui chai dumplings from the legendary Grandpa Ta-Pae, a local rite of passage. We’ll snag thumb-sized parcels of rice dumplings and khanom buang crispy crêpes filled with airy coconut cream.

Look out for juicy oysters flash-fried into a golden batter, and melt-in-your-mouth braised pork leg slow-cooked for hours in a master sauce of cinnamon and star anise. Try mok pla - a fish curry mousse steamed inside a banana leaf, to lock in the herbal aromatics of wild ginger and kaffir lime. Among many more surprises, there’s 15+ tastings in all, a deep dive into the bona fide food scene of the Chonburi coastline.

Seek out a hidden gem run by former fishermen who still use walkie-talkies to call their next catch. We’ll tear into seafood specialities: fresh squid sautéed in midnight-black buttery ink, a soufflé-like egg volcano pot bubbling over flames, and freshly seasoned fish, fried to a crisp.

This isn't just a food tour; it's a window into the traditional heart of Naklua, a far cry from the laminated menus of Walking Street. Come hungry, and visit the family-owned eateries that unveil the real Pattaya. This is the side of the city's cuisine that you never knew existed.

JJ Night Market Kanchanaburi is a vibrant spot near the Kanchanaburi Railway Station. Visitors can enjoy a wide variety of delicious local Thai food, like mango sticky rice, grilled meats, and seafood. There are also many stalls selling clothes, accessories, and souvenirs.

The market is popular with locals as it provides an amazing experience of Thai culture. It’s a great place to spend an evening shopping, eating, and exploring. JJ Green night market is a must-visit for anyone in Kanchanaburi.
## JJ Night Market Opening Hours

The JJ Night Market in Kanchanaburi is open every day from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM. During this time, you can try local food, and shop for souvenirs and unique items.
## How to reach JJ Night Market Kanchanaburi

By Car/Taxi

The most convenient way is by taxi or private car. The market is located near the Kanchanaburi Railway Station, so it’s easy to navigate.

By Scooter

Renting a motorcycle or scooter is another option. The roads are easy to navigate and give you flexibility to explore the city.

By Songthaew

Songthaews are an affordable way to get around Kanchanaburi. You can take one to the market from various points.
## Best time to visit JJ Night Market

The best time to visit JJ Night Market Kanchanaburi is in the evening, around 5 PM to 8 PM. During this time, the market is lively, and you can try the delicious food, and shop for various items. The temperature is cooler and is comfortable for exploring. It’s also less crowded earlier in the evening. So you can enjoy the market at a relaxed pace. If you are looking for a more lively atmosphere, you can visit around 8 PM.
## Highlights

The market features a variety of local Thai dishes, including barbecued cuttlefish, mango sticky rice, and various grilled items.

Situated near the Kanchanaburi Railway Station, the market is easily accessible for both locals and tourists.

Visitors can explore stalls offering affordable clothing, accessories, electronics, and souvenirs.

JJ Night Market offers a great experience into the daily life and culture of Kanchanaburi.

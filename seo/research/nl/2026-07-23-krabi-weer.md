## Intent and recommended format

The Dutch SERP is dominated by live-weather portals. Their core modules are
current conditions, hourly/48-hour forecast, rain radar, a 5- or 14-day forecast
and a small climate link. The PAA block is much more travel oriented: users also
want the best travel period, heat, rainy season and trip length.

Recommended page format: a static, evergreen climate and travel-planning guide
that clearly separates long-term averages from the current forecast and links to
the official Thai Meteorological Department forecast. Target 1,200–1,600 useful
Dutch words plus a scan-friendly monthly table. The top-three result pages are
widget-heavy and their stable editorial copy is limited; a conventional article
word count is therefore not a useful target.

## Competitor structure

1. Weeronline: current hour, today, precipitation radar, precipitation graph,
   sun/UV, five-day forecast, Thailand climate, monthly climate and activity
   weather.
2. Weerplaza: current conditions, 48 hours, today/tomorrow, 14 days and a short
   monthly climate entry point.
3. Buienradar: upcoming hours, upcoming days, 14-day forecast, today’s conditions
   and navigation to climate averages.

Shared topics: current temperature, precipitation, wind, short-term forecast and
some access to climate averages. Shared gap: little practical guidance about
choosing months for island trips, interpreting a “rain day”, keeping boat plans
flexible or distinguishing the Andaman coast from the Gulf coast.

## Verified climate evidence

Primary source: Thai Meteorological Department, station Krabi 48563,
climatological period 1991–2020. Annual mean temperature is 27.6 °C. Monthly mean
maximum ranges from 31.3 °C (September) to 34.7 °C (March). February is the driest
month at 35.9 mm average rainfall; October is the wettest at 292.9 mm. These are
climate averages, not a promise for an individual trip.

The Tourism Authority of Thailand describes the Andaman west-coast travel season
as broadly November–April. The page uses the more conservative December–March
window as the strongest average chance of drier conditions, while keeping
November and April as nuanced transition months.

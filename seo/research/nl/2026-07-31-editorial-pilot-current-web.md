## Method

This is a current public-web fallback refresh while DataForSEO credits are constrained. It records observable intent and primary-source facts only. It does **not** claim DataForSEO volumes, rankings or People Also Ask ownership.

## Phuket Airport

- Search intent: arrival sequence, terminals, onward transport and airport-to-hotel decision support.
- Primary source: [Phuket International Airport](https://phuket.airportthai.co.th/?lang=en), checked 2026-07-31.
- Primary-source facts used: separate passenger guidance; airport bus, express bus, Smart Bus, public van and rental-car options; travelers should confirm current schedules.
- Editorial decision: remove hard promises about waiting times, taxes and prices from the decision layer. Present transport as a comparison with a check-current-price CTA.

## Khao Yai National Park

- Search intent: feasibility of a Bangkok day trip, transport inside the park, highlights, opening hours and wildlife safety.
- Primary source: [Khao Yai National Park — Plan Your Visit](https://khaoyainationalpark.com/en/plan-your-visit), checked 2026-07-31.
- Primary-source facts used: daily 06:00–18:00 opening hours; two entrances; no regular transport between park attractions; official elephant-distance and conduct advice; temporary closures must be checked.
- Editorial decision: lead with “day trip versus overnight” and transport choice rather than an unqualified “ultimate day trip” claim.

## Wellness retreats

- Search intent: compare retreat types, destinations, inclusions, professional guidance, prices and booking conditions.
- Primary sources: [Tourism Authority of Thailand — Spa & Wellness](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Search-result/spa) and [TAT wellness retreat example](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Promotions/115/wellness-retreats-near-bangkok-all-inclusive-detox-weight-loss-longevity), checked 2026-07-31.
- Primary-source facts used: programs can combine accommodation, meals and a structured schedule; inclusions, transfers, health assessments, refund terms and availability vary by provider.
- Editorial decision: avoid “best” rankings without defensible review methodology. Build a choice framework and explicitly separate accommodation booking from the retreat program.

## Owner boundaries

- `phuket airport` owns airport arrival and onward-transport intent; it does not own Phuket hotels or general destination planning.
- `khao yai national park` owns visit planning; detailed Nakhon Ratchasima destination and accommodation intent remains with those route owners.
- `wellness retreat thailand` owns program comparison; Thai massage, yoga destinations and individual hotels remain separate owners.

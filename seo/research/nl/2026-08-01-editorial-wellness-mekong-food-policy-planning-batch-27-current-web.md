### Longevity and medical-boundary context

- [TAT: Going for the Long Game](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/05/going-for-the-long-game-how-thailand-is-redefining-longevity-tourism/) — confirms tourism positioning; treated as marketing context, not clinical evidence.
- [TAT tourism update at TTM+ 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/06/thailand-tourism-update-at-ttm-2026-reinforces-quality-led-growth-direction/) — confirms longevity, preventive care and lifestyle positioning.
- [WHO healthy-ageing research, evidence and data](https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/ageing-and-health/research-evidence-and-data) — supports evidence-informed framing. The page does not validate individual retreat claims.

Editorial consequence: separate non-medical lifestyle support from diagnostics and intervention; require credential, risk, intake and aftercare questions; remove detox, rejuvenation, outcome, accreditation-count and price claims.

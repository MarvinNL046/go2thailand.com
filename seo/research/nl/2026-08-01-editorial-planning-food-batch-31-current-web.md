ACM explains that package status depends on combined travel services and booking structure, and that package organizers have additional duties and responsibility. ACM/ConsuWijzer also distinguishes package changes and cancellation rights. Decision: ready/index as the package-versus-separate decision owner; no live prices or affiliate block.

### Travel insurance

NederlandWereldwijd advises good insurance for Thailand that covers additional hospital and repatriation costs, and notes that healthcare costs can be high and may require advance payment. Coverage remains policy-specific. Decision: ready/index as the Thailand-specific coverage-check owner; no insurer recommendation or quote affiliate.

Sources: [NederlandWereldwijd Thailand advice](https://www.nederlandwereldwijd.nl/reisadvies/thailand), [Rijksoverheid foreign healthcare](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/zorgverzekering/vraag-en-antwoord/ben-ik-voor-zorg-verzekerd-als-ik-op-vakantie-ben-in-het-buitenland).

### What makes Thai food spicy

An accepted sibling profile explicitly delegates ingredient-level spiciness to this route. Scientific literature supports capsaicin activation of TRPV1; TAT describes regional use of spicy, sour, sweet and salty flavours without making every dish uniformly hot. Decision: ready/index as the specialized chili, paste and ordering owner.

Sources: [TAT regional cuisine](https://www.tatnews.org/2018/01/discover-thai-cuisine-famous-four-regions/), [TRPV1/capsaicin review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5326624/), [chili preference review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2022.1039207/full).

- LISA campaign: What role did TAT confirm? When was it launched? Which places were featured? Is this a practical itinerary? Did the campaign prove more visitors or spending? Are reward activities still active?
- Songkran flights: Which dates and capacity were covered? Was the percentage against a maximum fare? Is the offer still bookable? Where should future festival planning live?

## Primary-source findings and editorial consequences

### Food and health

- [WHO: Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet), updated 26 January 2026, uses adequacy, balance, moderation and diversity as core principles. It discusses vegetables, fruit, pulses, whole grains, free sugars, sodium and fat at dietary-pattern level.
- [TAT: Thai cuisine through four regions](https://www.tatnews.org/2018/01/discover-thai-cuisine-famous-four-regions/) documents regional variety and common flavour components. It is tourism context, not a clinical nutrition source.

Consequence: retain the health route, but remove cure, metabolism, anti-inflammatory, “healthiest dish,” fixed-calorie and cuisine-versus-cuisine claims. Never infer a dish’s composition from its name alone. Add an explicit medical boundary.

### Food and spice

- The same TAT regional source supports meaningful regional variation, not a universal heat scale.
- Existing owner inventory assigns broad “is Thai food spicy?” to `/nl/food/`; an existing specialist route owns the chilli/curry-paste mechanics.

Consequence: archive the duplicate. Keep only a short transition explaining that recipe, cook and individual tolerance vary; “not spicy” is not an allergen guarantee.

### LISA and Amazing Thailand

- [TAT official unveiling, 29 January 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/01/tat-unveils-lalisa-lisa-manobal-as-amazing-thailand-ambassador-inviting-travellers-to-feel-all-the-feelings/) confirms Lalisa “LISA” Manobal as Amazing Thailand Ambassador for “Feel All the Feelings.”
- [TAT official campaign-location article](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/02/tat-invites-visitors-to-follow-footsteps-of-lisa-the-amazing-thailand-ambassador-in-a-journey-exploring-landmarks-presented-in-tvc-feel-all-the-feelings/) identifies dispersed cultural and natural locations.
- [TAT craft and attire context](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/04/tat-unveils-the-inspiration-behind-feel-all-the-feelings-showcasing-lisas-attire-and-local-artisanal-products-intertwining-thai-heritage-and-culture-within-every-scene/) explains campaign styling and local craft framing.
- [TAT reward campaign](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/04/tat-inspires-travel-across-thailand-with-lisa-through-feel-all-the-feelings-seeking-thailands-wonders-inviting-travellers-to-discover-thailand-and-win-five-styles-of-exclu/) ran through 31 July 2026; it is not presented as still active.

Consequence: retain as a branded source-backed campaign decoder. Do not claim LISA caused visitor growth, spending, virality, safety, sustainability or quality. Generated hero deliberately avoids celebrity likeness and logos.

Consequence: elapsed/noindex. No current booking CTA, fare or availability claim.

### Aviation costs and Middle East disruption

- [CAAT annual report and 2026 risk context](https://www.caat.or.th/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CAAT-%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%88%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%B5-2568_50M_U.pdf) identifies geopolitical disruption and fuel cost as aviation risks. It does not establish the price of an individual ticket.
- `/nl/blog/middle-east-conflict-thailand-flights-airfare-2026/` already owns the current traveler task: flight, operating carrier, route and transfer verification.

Consequence: archive the generic March price story. Remove fixed percentage, fuel-queue and hotel-pass-through claims.

### Visitor numbers

- [Ministry of Tourism and Sports](https://secretary.mots.go.th/news/6953) reported 7,489,983 international arrivals for 1 January–11 March 2026.
- [TAT, 8 April 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/04/tat-refocuses-on-value-over-volume-as-2026-tourism-outlook-is-recalibrated/) reported 9.31 million for the full first quarter and a forecast range, explicitly separating result and outlook.
- [TAT, 10 June 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/06/thailand-tourism-update-at-ttm-2026-reinforces-quality-led-growth-direction/) reported more than 14 million through 2 June and a later full-year projection.

Consequence: archive the 11 March counter and route it to the dated tourism timeline. National arrivals do not prove local crowding, prices, safety, deals or infrastructure quality.

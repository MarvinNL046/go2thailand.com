# NL editorial batch 28 — current-web research

Checked: 2026-08-01
Scope: seven owned routes; Trat and Koh Kood excluded.

## Method

Primary-source research was used for claims that can change: programme status, event dates, policy status, opening status, official holidays and venue rules. Search-result phrasing was used only to observe question families. No PAA, search-volume or difficulty figures were invented.

Observed question families:

- what STAR means, whether it is a hotel rating, and how to verify a participant;
- whether the March crisis centre is still active and who helps with a cancelled flight;
- what to wear in a Thai temple, whether a sarong is enough, and photo/monk rules;
- whether the Tourism Festival is still open and what Zero Waste to Landfills meant;
- whether announced visa changes already apply, to which passport, and where to check;
- whether THAINOSAUR is open, where it is and whether it suits families;
- when Visakha Bucha occurred, what closed and how visitors behave respectfully.

## Route findings

### STAR accreditation

- TAT says STAR was introduced in 2023, rates from three to five stars and covers governance, socioeconomic, culture and environment dimensions.
- TAT reported more than 2,300 participants as of August 2025 and still named STAR in its June 2026 standards framework.
- No primary support found for the previous claim that 70% of all tourism businesses would be accredited by end-2026. Removed.
- Sources: [TAT sustainability overview](https://www.tatnews.org/2025/10/thailand-drives-journey-to-sustainable-tourism-leadership/), [TAT TTM+ update](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/06/thailand-tourism-update-at-ttm-2026-reinforces-quality-led-growth-direction/), [STAR register](https://www.tatstar.org/companies).

### TAT Crisis Monitoring Centre

- TAT explicitly activated the centre on 1 March 2026 in response to Middle East tensions and flight disruption.
- The release reported 59 cancellations at that moment and support at airports. These are historical figures, not current conditions.
- No later primary source was found that makes the same activation a standing current traveller alert.
- Source: [TAT activation release](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/03/tat-activates-tourism-crisis-monitoring-centre-in-response-to-middle-east-tensions/).

### Temple etiquette

- The Grand Palace publishes a venue-specific prohibited-clothing list. It must not be flattened into a universal rule for every Thai temple.
- Shoes, photography, ceremonial access and clothing can differ by building and venue; local instructions are authoritative.
- Sources: [Grand Palace practical information](https://www.royalgrandpalace.th/en/visit/practical-information), [Grand Palace visitor regulation PDF](https://cdn.royalgrandpalace.th/images/visit/%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%AF%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%82%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%872567.pdf).

### Thailand Tourism Festival 2026

- Event elapsed: 25–29 March 2026 at QSNCC.
- TAT described the fourth consecutive Zero Waste to Landfills edition, Zone 7 Road to Sustainability, vendor separation rules and 14 bilingual sorting stations.
- The announcement contains goals and measures, not an independent post-event diversion result.
- Source: [TAT festival release](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/03/thailand-tourism-festival-2026-spotlights-5-must-do-in-thailand-and-honours-her-majesty-queen-sirikit-the-queen-mother/).

### Visa policy

- Cabinet announcements on 19 May and 15 July 2026 described revised visa-exemption/VoA designs.
- Both state that relevant drafts take effect 15 days after Royal Gazette publication. Cabinet approval was therefore not presented as immediate effectiveness.
- Because the topic is volatile and the route's old Asian-country framing was misleading, no fixed country list is used for travel advice.
- Sources: [PRD 19 May](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/48/iid/504639), [PRD 15 July](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/2078/iid/522327), [Thai e-Visa](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/).

### THAINOSAUR Museum

- AWC confirms opening on 23 December 2025 at Asiatique, not “early 2026”.
- The route duplicates accepted owner `/nl/blog/bangkok-spectacular-2026-awc-thainosaur-museum-chao-phraya/`; archive/noindex and redirect advice are required.
- Source: [AWC opening release](https://www.assetworldcorp-th.com/en/newsroom/news-activities/1034/awc-launches-bangkok-spectacular-2026-with-the-auspicious-opening-of-thainosaur-museum-bringing-new-experience-of-art-culture-and-education-to-bangkok).

### Visakha Bucha 2026

- The religious day fell Sunday 31 May 2026; Bank of Thailand lists Monday 1 June as the substitute financial-institution holiday.
- General alcohol rules say restrictions may apply on major religious days; travellers need the date-specific announcement for exact application.
- Wildlife-release advice was removed and replaced with a do-not-buy-captive-animals safeguard.
- Sources: [Royal Thai Embassy](https://wellington.thaiembassy.org/th/content/%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B9%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%B2-visakha-bucha-day-2?cate=5d75e39215e39c06b8005478), [Bank of Thailand holidays](https://www.bot.or.th/en/financial-institutions-holiday.html), [Thailand.go.th alcohol rules](https://thailand.go.th/public/event-detail/alcohol-sales-and-consumption-rules-updated-in-thailand).

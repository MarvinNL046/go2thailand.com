# Contentbrief — mooiste plekken Thailand

**Route:** `/nl/city/`  
**Primary:** `mooiste plekken thailand` — volume 480, KD 0, informational  
**Secondary:** `mooiste plekken in thailand`, `thailand mooiste plekken`, `hoogtepunten thailand`, `bestemmingen thailand`  
**Evidence:** live DataForSEO NL/Netherlands, captured 23 July 2026

## Search intent and format

The dominant intent is visual inspiration followed by selection help. The result page favours editorial lists or destination hubs with large photography. The page must answer “where should I go?” before exposing the full directory.

Top-three evidence:

- 27Vakantiedagen: list of 11 highlights, 2,585 captured words; destination-by-destination format.
- Reisjunk: visual Thailand content hub, 680 captured words; routes users to destination articles.
- ANWB: list of 12 sights/places, 2,289 captured words; longer narrative sections.

Target: roughly 2,000–2,700 useful words including destination summaries, comparison help, FAQs and the browsable directory. Do not pad card copy to hit a word count.

## Shared topics

Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Krabi or the Andaman coast, Khao Sok, Ayutthaya, Kanchanaburi, Pai and southern islands recur across the strongest results. Competitors rely heavily on a numbered list and scenic photography.

## Information gain

- Separate “beautiful” from “right for your trip”: include fit and trade-off per featured place.
- Let users choose by region and by travel style.
- Keep an accessible search/filter directory for the complete city inventory.
- Use an accurate Thailand silhouette to explain north, central Thailand, Isaan and the south.
- Route users to weather, transport, accommodation and itinerary owners instead of duplicating those topics.

## PAA — verbatim from the live NL SERP

- Wat is het mooiste gedeelte van Thailand?
- Welk deel van Thailand is het mooist?
- Waar moet je echt zijn geweest in Thailand?
- Wat zijn de beste plekken om te bezoeken in Thailand?
- Wat is een budget voor 4 weken Thailand?
- Wat moet je vermijden in Thailand?

The first four can be answered on this hub. Budget belongs primarily to the budget guide and “avoid” belongs to the safety/culture guides; give a short answer and a prominent internal link rather than duplicating those pages.

## Editorial stance

“Mooiste” is subjective. Lead with a concise shortlist, explain the type of trip each destination suits and name its practical trade-off. Avoid unverifiable superlatives, current prices and universal claims.

## SEO ownership

This page owns country-level destination discovery. It does not own individual city attractions, hotel choice, weather, route planning or “Thailand bezienswaardigheden” as a full attraction inventory. Those intents are summarized and internally linked.

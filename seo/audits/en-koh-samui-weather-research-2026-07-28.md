# Koh Samui weather — English research brief

**Proposed canonical owner:** `/city/koh-samui/weather/`
**Legacy owner to consolidate:** `/city/koh-samui/best-time-to-visit/`
**Research date:** 28 July 2026
**Market:** United Kingdom / English

## Evidence captured

- Exact-route DataForSEO checks returned zero ranking keywords and no backlink or referring-domain summary signal for both the proposed `/weather/` URL and the existing `/best-time-to-visit/` URL.
- The `koh samui weather` cluster returned 234 keyword records and 50 competitor domains. The separate `best time to visit koh samui` cluster returned 134 keyword records and 50 competitor domains.
- `koh samui weather` and close variants carry 6,600 UK volume with KD 0. `best time to visit koh samui` and close variants carry 1,600 UK volume with KD 0. Monthly demand is material: August reaches 1,000; July, November and December reach 880; April and June reach 720; February, May, September and October reach 590.
- Ten current UK-English SERPs returned 85 organic results and 59 genuine People Also Ask appearances across 39 case-normalised distinct questions.
- Kuoni appeared in all ten selected SERPs, TUI in nine, The Koh Samui Guide in eight and Trailfinders in six. Forecast domains dominate the broad head SERP, while travel-planning pages dominate best-time and month-specific intent.
- Six usable full parses cover Kuoni, two detailed The Koh Samui Guide pages, Climates to Travel, Silavadee and Tourism Authority of Thailand. Trailfinders, TUI and Garrya returned zero DFS body and cannot carry detailed claims.
- The official TMD 1991–2020 climate-normal PDF and current Ko Samui forecast remain the numerical and same-week control sources. Competitors inform intent and information gaps, not the authoritative monthly table.

## Canonical decision

The two English URLs target the same weather, month and travel-timing decision. Neither has ranking or backlink equity. Publish one stronger owner at `/city/koh-samui/weather/`, permanently redirect `/city/koh-samui/best-time-to-visit/`, remove the legacy URL from the English sitemap and normalise English internal links to the weather owner.

This decision:

1. aligns the higher-volume weather head term with the canonical slug;
2. creates a true EN/NL hreflang pair;
3. removes a site-owned duplicate instead of adding another indexable page;
4. keeps monthly weather intent inside one evidence-backed table and decision guide.

## Required information gain

- Explain that Koh Samui follows a later Gulf rainfall pattern than Phuket and Krabi; do not repeat the generic Thailand cool/hot/rainy model used by the legacy page.
- Lead with a traveller decision: February–April for the driest statistical starting point, June–August as a workable UK-summer option, and October–December as the period requiring most flexibility.
- Publish the complete TMD station table with mean high, mean low, rainfall and rain days for all twelve months.
- Separate climate normals from a forecast and make sea state, wind, thunderstorms and marine warnings decisive for Ang Thong and other boat days.
- Explain why a measurable rain day need not mean an all-day washout, while avoiding the opposite promise that tropical showers always pass quickly.
- Give three useful plan swaps: exposed boat day, island land day and genuinely wet day.
- Use current-price and current-availability wording for Trip.com and Klook. Do not publish fixed room prices, tour prices or weather guarantees.
- Add contextual Amazon OneLink products only in the packing decision: rain poncho, insect repellent, drybag and quick-dry towel. Do not force unrelated products into climate or FAQ copy.

## Genuine PAA selected

- What are the best months to go to Koh Samui?
- What is the rainy season in Koh Samui?
- What is the rainiest month in Koh Samui?
- When not to go to Koh Samui?
- Is August a good time for Koh Samui?
- Is July or August better in Koh Samui?
- Is Koh Samui good to visit in December?
- Does Koh Samui get a lot of rain?
- Should I go to Phuket or Koh Samui?
- Are there a lot of mosquitoes in Koh Samui?

The broad “which island is nicer” questions belong to the destination-comparison owner. Mosquito advice must remain practical and must not become medical guidance.

## Content boundaries

- `/city/koh-samui/` owns the complete island decision, coast choice and itinerary.
- `/best-hotels/koh-samui/` owns accommodation zones and hotel selection.
- `/city/koh-samui/attractions/` owns the island-wide activity shortlist.
- `/compare/phuket-vs-krabi/` owns the future destination comparison; this weather owner may explain the Gulf-versus-Andaman timing difference without becoming a complete island comparison.
- Month-specific legacy child pages may answer narrower month intent but must not contradict the official table or create a second broad canonical.

## Publication decision

Internationalise the established premium `WeatherGuideTemplate`, create an independent English `WeatherGuideData` owner and register it on the existing weather route. Publish only after the legacy redirect, canonical, EN/NL/x-default hreflang, Dataset/FAQ/Breadcrumb/WebPage schema, internal links, Klook, Trip.com, four contextual Amazon OneLink exits, responsive design, assets and layered sitewide QA pass.

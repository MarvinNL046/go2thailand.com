# English destination season-subpillar owner family

**Implemented:** 29 July 2026  
**Research:** `seo/audits/en-destination-season-subpillar-research-2026-07-29.md`  
**Final sitemap:** 1,564 EN + 703 NL = 2,267 routes

## Owner decision

The 32 indexable English `/city/<place>/best-time-to-visit/` candidates did not justify 32 independent owners. Fresh exact-URL ranking and backlink checks returned no signal for all candidates, while the independent DFS and live UK SERP evidence supported only two standalone season decisions:

- `/city/hua-hin/best-time-to-visit/` retains the Hua Hin travel-window intent;
- `/city/khao-sok/best-time-to-visit/` retains the materially different rainforest, Cheow Lan Lake and activity-access decision.

Nine overlapping best-time routes now permanently redirect to their stronger `/weather/` owner. Twenty-one low-demand routes permanently redirect to the independently researched destination owner, where their useful season guidance already belongs. All 30 retired routes are absent from the English sitemap and from rendered internal links.

## Premium reusable template

`SeasonDecisionGuideTemplate` is a reusable English season-owner template with route-specific data. Both owners include:

- an editorial hero, glass decision card and responsive section navigation;
- one direct answer, three honest travel windows and six two-month rhythm cards;
- no invented monthly climate normals or false day-level precision;
- a route-specific visual banner with a dotted planning route and waypoint;
- trip-style matching, three flexible Plan A/B/C outcomes and practical packing;
- genuine search-question FAQs, Article, Breadcrumb, FAQ and ItemList schema;
- current primary-source handoffs, related canonical owners and disclosed affiliate decisions.

Hua Hin uses its city, coast, market and Sam Roi Yot visual system. Khao Sok uses rainforest, village, Cheow Lan Lake and lodge visuals. Existing rights-safe redesign assets were inspected and reused because they fit the exact editorial scenes; no filler image was introduced merely to claim a new asset.

## Affiliate parity

Affiliate fit is decided by reader task, not locale or quota.

- **Hua Hin:** current hotel and transport comparison through Trip.com and 12Go are the useful handoffs. Amazon is explicitly omitted because buying a physical product does not improve the timing decision.
- **Khao Sok:** Klook and 12Go support current activity and transport checks. A drybag, water shoes and quick-dry towel solve a visible rain-and-lake packing task, so three central OneLink routes are shown with `Check current ... at Amazon` wording.
- Every Amazon link uses `target="_blank"`, `noopener noreferrer nofollow sponsored`, full country/seller/price/delivery variation disclosure and the central `/go/` registry.
- Runtime checks returned HTTP 307 to the canonical Amazon product with the approved `go2thailand-20` tag for all three products.

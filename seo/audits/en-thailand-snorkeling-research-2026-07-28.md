## Owner and template plan

Keep `/best-diving-snorkeling-in-thailand/`, but make the English page a snorkelling-first decision owner instead of an encyclopaedic list of dive-site claims:

1. Quick answer: choose the coast and month before choosing the island.
2. Same-day condition check: weather, swell/current, local warning, operator go/no-go and swimmer confidence.
3. Coast-season switch: Andaman, northern Gulf and eastern Gulf with ranges as planning context, never a guarantee.
4. Destination matrix: Koh Tao, Koh Lipe/Tarutao, Surin/Similan, Phi Phi/Krabi, Racha/Phuket and Koh Chang/Koh Rang, each with access, fit and trade-off.
5. Beach-versus-boat decision and beginner/family boundary.
6. Responsible wildlife and reef behaviour without unsupported “reef-safe” product promises.
7. Natural internal links to the researched Koh Tao snorkelling/diving owners, island-hopping, weather and transport.
8. Ten current English PAA answers, source/update policy and Article/WebPage, FAQ, Breadcrumb and ItemList schema.

Build a reusable premium `ThailandSnorkelingGuideTemplate` with a unique wide hero and coast-switch visual. The existing NL rendering remains unchanged during this English implementation cycle.

## Affiliate policy

- Klook: current snorkelling/island-tour options after the coast and departure point are chosen.
- 12Go: current island transfer and ferry options.
- Amazon OneLink: only contextual kit that helps the day without replacing operator safety equipment—dry bag, water shoes and quick-dry towel. Use current-price CTAs, country-aware OneLink routing and nearby disclosure.
- Do not present a generic mask or full-face snorkel as automatically safe or well fitting; rental/included equipment must be verified with the operator.
- No fixed tour, ferry, park, rental or product prices; no copied ratings or fake availability.

## Explicit exclusions

- Fixed visibility, water clarity, wildlife sightings or calm-sea guarantees.
- Universal “best island” ranking without travel month and access context.
- Stale park closure, fee, timetable or route claims.
- Unsupported personal visits, precise swim distances, coral-health superlatives or safety certification claims.
- Medical advice, beginner ability guarantees, “chemical-free” or universal reef-safe sunscreen claims.
- Dive-course pricing and certification detail beyond a clear link/boundary to the dedicated diving owner.

## Dominant intent

The owner must solve one commercial decision: where and in what format should a traveller stay in the wider Khao Sok journey?

1. Khlong Sok Village is the practical west-side land base for park access, food and land activities.
2. A riverside or treehouse lodge increases atmosphere but can reduce walkability and everyday flexibility.
3. The Ban Ta Khun/Rajjaprabha side is a logistical staging choice for an early lake departure, not the same destination centre.
4. A Cheow Lan floating bungalow is normally part of a boat, meal and activity package; it is not a conventional room-only hotel comparison.
5. A deliberate split stay can add real value because land and lake open different landscapes, but only when the transfer and final drop-off are explicit.

The high-volume floating-bungalow cluster belongs on this owner because it is inseparable from the Khao Sok accommodation choice. A future property detail may own one named resort; it must not duplicate the land-versus-lake decision.

## Required information gain

- Lead with the Khlong Sok versus Rajjaprabha/Cheow Lan split rather than an unverifiable universal ranking.
- Give six accommodation formats visible fit, advantage, trade-off and transport consequence.
- Use six independently verified named examples: Our Jungle Camp, Our Jungle House, Khao Sok Tree House Resort, Anurak Community Lodge, Panvaree The Greenery and 500 Rai Floating Resort.
- Make a floating-stay package checklist explicit: pier and boat, meals, activities, park fees, guide, bathroom, power/air-conditioning windows, luggage, weather change and final road drop-off.
- Compare one land base, a purposeful split and a land lodge plus lake day trip without implying one itinerary suits every traveller.
- Use current-price and availability language for Trip.com exits. Do not publish a fixed room rate, package price, park fee or availability claim.
- Do not add Amazon products: a physical product does not help a reader choose a Khao Sok accommodation format. The commercial owner remains Trip.com-led.

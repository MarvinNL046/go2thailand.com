# Phuket weather English owner research

**Candidate canonical:** `/city/phuket/weather/`  
**Legacy candidate:** `/city/phuket/best-time-to-visit/`  
**Captured:** 28 July 2026

## Search demand and owner decision

- Independent UK-English DFS research returned 251 records for `phuket weather` and 102 records for `best time to visit phuket`, with 50 competitor domains in each cluster.
- The captured head terms report `phuket weather` at volume 12,100 and KD 0, `best time to visit phuket` at volume 1,900 and KD 0, and `phuket weather by month` at volume 720 and KD 1.
- Ten current UK-English SERPs produced 79 organic results, 53 genuine People Also Ask appearances and 39 distinct case-normalised questions.
- Neither the weather URL nor the legacy best-time URL has a ranking-keyword or backlink-summary signal in the current DFS checks.
- One canonical owner should therefore cover weather by month, best time, rainy season, shoulder months and the planning consequences. The legacy best-time URL can be permanently consolidated after implementation and redirect QA.

## Current SERP pattern

- Forecast publishers dominate the short head query, while holiday operators and local editorial guides dominate best-time and month-planning searches.
- Recurring decision questions include the best month, wettest and driest month, rainy season, October and November conditions, whether rain lasts all day, the worst period, mosquitoes and whether green-season travel is still worthwhile.
- Competitors commonly flatten rainfall into a simple good/bad calendar. The owner should instead separate climate normals from a forecast and separate rain from wind, waves, thunderstorms and beach flags.

## Sources reviewed

- Thai Meteorological Department current Phuket forecast: primary live-weather authority.
- Thai Meteorological Department 1991–2020 station normals already verified for the paired Dutch weather family: primary source for the monthly table.
- Tourism Authority of Thailand Phuket destination page: official destination and beach-safety context.
- Kuoni, Phuket.net, Phuket101 and Climates to Travel: current UK/local competitor structures and month interpretations parsed through DFS.
- TUI and Trailfinders blocked useful parsing and are retained only as SERP evidence, not as factual support.

## Content and design requirements

- Use the existing premium bilingual weather template as infrastructure, but write independent English copy and labels.
- Lead with a direct answer: December through March offers the driest statistical starting point; February is driest in the official station normals; September has the highest rainfall total and October the most measured rain days.
- Preserve all twelve monthly values, three season decisions, four travel-style decisions and a genuine weather-aware Plan A/B/C route.
- Include at least ten genuine PAA answers without promising that every shower is brief or that any month guarantees calm seas.
- Link naturally to the Phuket destination, attractions, hotel and national weather owners.

## Affiliate fit and exclusions

- Klook belongs only at the current-condition activity decision; Trip.com belongs only at the flexible-stay decision.
- Amazon OneLink has strong contextual fit in the packing section. Use current-price wording for a compact poncho, insect repellent, drybag and quick-dry towel; do not force sunscreen or a hat product merely to increase density.
- Every commercial exit must use `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow sponsored"`, with visible provider-specific disclosure.
- Exclude fixed hotel, tour or product prices, copied ratings, guaranteed weather, guaranteed sea state, medical mosquito claims and a universal “worst month” prohibition.

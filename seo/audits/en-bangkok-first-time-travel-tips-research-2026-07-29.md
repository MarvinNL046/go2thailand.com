# English research brief — Bangkok travel tips for first-time visitors

**Proposed owner:** `/blog/bangkok-travel-tips-reddit/`
**Research completed:** 2026-07-29
**Market:** United Kingdom / English

## Owner decision

Retain the exact bilingual path proposed by the existing Dutch route, but target the natural English head term **Bangkok travel tips** rather than repeating “Reddit” as a keyword device. The Reddit modifier is a small secondary query and a useful source of recurring traveller questions, not an authority for factual answers.

Keep these owners independent:

- `/city/bangkok/` owns the broad destination and “is Bangkok worth visiting” decision.
- `/blog/bangkok-beyond-temples-local-guide/` owns alternative sights beyond the standard temple circuit.
- `/blog/thailand-first-time-visitors-essential-guide-2026/` owns first-time Thailand planning across the country.
- `/where-to-stay/bangkok/` and `/best-hotels/bangkok/` own accommodation and neighbourhood selection.
- `/blog/bangkok-public-transport-bts-mrt-tourist-guide-2026/` owns detailed network and ticket-system intent.

The new owner must answer the narrower job: what a first-time visitor should decide before arrival, during the first 90 minutes, and across the first three or four days to avoid needless city-crossing, payment, clothing, connectivity and safety friction.

## Information-gain format

Build a decision-led owner rather than another attraction list:

1. A first-90-minutes arrival route: connect, move to the chosen corridor, check in, reset.
2. Four corridor consequences: Old Town/riverside, Siam, lower Sukhumvit and Silom/Sathorn; link to the accommodation owner for property choice.
3. A mode-switch map: rail for long corridors, river for the old-city edge, car for gaps or late arrival, walking only inside compact zones.
4. Three bounded day rhythms that group nearby places instead of zig-zagging across Bangkok.
5. Six community pressure points answered with verified guidance: cash/payment, clothing, heat/rain, connectivity, scams/safety and realistic walking.
6. A mistake-to-better-swap board and a same-day check list.
7. Ten genuine PAA with direct, non-alarmist answers and clear delegation to specialist owners.

Target roughly 2,300–3,000 useful on-page words. Essential copy must remain HTML, not embedded in images.

## Owner decision

Retain the established travel-guide URL as the English owner. It already has the strongest internal-link network and an exact Dutch counterpart. Keep these intents independent:

- `/is-thailand-safe/` owns broad countrywide tourist-safety intent and already ranks for ten generic safety queries, including `is thailand safe` (volume 2,400).
- `/practical-info/scams-safety/` owns scam recognition and response.

- Destination, accommodation, nightlife, itinerary, insurance and transport owners retain their detailed commercial or local decisions.

The solo-female owner must answer a narrower job: how to preserve choice, connection and a clear return route across accommodation, arrivals, intercity moves, meeting people, evenings and incident response without promising that a place or activity is universally safe.

- GOV.UK getting-help guidance lists police 191, ambulance 1669, fire 199 and Tourist Police 1155, and directs travellers to providers, insurers and consular support after serious incidents.
- The current FCDO Thailand sexual-assault support guide explains options for medical attention, reporting and consular support without prescribing one mandatory response.

## Information-gain format

Build a choice-preserving decision owner rather than another “safest places” list:

1. A six-moment safety chain: book, arrive, check in, move, meet, return.
2. Four destination formats with fit and trade-off rather than a universal safest-city ranking: connected Bangkok, compact-social Chiang Mai, activity-led coast, and island/ferry travel.
3. An accommodation check based on address, arrival route, reception, access, recent reviews, locker/privacy options and late-return reality.
4. A transport switchboard: rail, identifiable car, shared activity transfer, intercity operator and the scooter boundary.
5. A connection-without-dependence board for hostels, classes, day tours and activity groups.
6. A nightlife return plan covering drink control, methanol uncertainty, a pre-decided exit and no obligation to stay polite.
7. A practical incident route: reach a staffed place, obtain urgent help, preserve choices, contact provider/insurer/consular support and document only when safe.
8. Ten genuine PAA with direct answers and current-source boundaries.

Target roughly 2,500–3,200 useful on-page words. Essential safety copy must remain HTML and should not use fear-based conversion language.

- Create a unique, rights-safe solo-arrival hero with an alert but calm traveller in a real transport context, plus a second editorial visual showing three independent travel anchors rather than a defensive “victim” image.
- Use the shared premium primitives with a dotted six-moment chain, asymmetric destination-format cards, a dark-jade return-plan section and an emergency decision strip.
- Relevant affiliates: Trip.com for current accommodation choices, Klook for optional social/group activities, 12Go for current intercity operators and Saily for connectivity.
- Amazon is contextually justified only for one optional power bank that keeps maps, contact details and booking references available. Do not market a physical product as protection or imply that buying gear creates safety.
- Use current-option and current-price CTA language; do not publish fixed room, fare, tour, product or insurance prices.

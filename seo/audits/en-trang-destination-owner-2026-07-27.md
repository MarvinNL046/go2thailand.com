# English Trang destination owner audit — 2026-07-27

## Outcome

The English Trang owner is rebuilt as a premium breakfast-city, rail and island-decision guide on the reusable destination template. It starts in Trang city, gives Kantang a clear heritage function and makes the traveller choose one coast or island layer instead of treating the province as one interchangeable pier. The Dutch locale remains on its existing independently written owner.

## Research and ownership

- DFS cluster: 104 records and 50 competitor domains; `trang thailand` has UK volume 590 and KD 5.
- Ten current English SERP/PAA sets and three full competitor or primary-source parses were captured.
- The exact owner returned zero ranking terms and no backlink summary signal at capture time.
- Nha Trang-polluted airport PAA and overly broad island questions were explicitly excluded.
- Hotel, attraction, food, transport and weather depth remains naturally linked or separately owned rather than duplicated.

- The open FAQ answer renders in high-contrast charcoal text and remains visibly readable against the cream background.

## SEO, schema and affiliate verification

- Metadata title: `Trang Thailand Guide: Breakfast, Kantang & Islands` (50 characters).
- Metadata description: 145 characters.
- Canonical: `https://go2-thailand.com/city/trang/`.
- Hreflang: English, Dutch and x-default point to their correct owners.
- Schema types: `Organization`, `TouristDestination`, `ItemList`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList` and `WebPage`.
- Ten verbatim researched PAA questions are visible and included in FAQ schema.
- Five affiliate exits use disclosed sponsored-link handling; all price-, schedule- and availability-sensitive choices use current-check wording.
- No fixed fare, price or rating is stored.
- Amazon is deliberately absent because the broad destination owner contains no physical-product task. Contextual cross-sells are reserved for genuine food, recipe and cooking-class owners, limited to two or three relevant OneLink products with `Check current price at Amazon` wording.

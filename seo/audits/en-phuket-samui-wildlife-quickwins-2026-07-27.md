## Why these owners were selected

The fresh DataForSEO page-two scan returned two owner opportunities whose live pages were still on legacy visual templates:

- `koh samui vs phuket` — position 17, UK volume 720.
- `poisonous animals thailand` — position 15, UK volume 480.
- `thailand dangerous animals` — position 13, UK volume 480.

The existing Phuket–Samui owner retained two ranking keywords. The wildlife owner retained six ranking keywords and a backlink footprint of three backlinks from one referring domain. URLs and intent were therefore preserved.

## Research evidence

- Phuket–Samui: 38 DFS keyword records, 50 competitor domains, eight live SERP/PAA captures, owner ranking and backlink snapshots.
- Wildlife safety: 22 DFS keyword records, 50 competitor domains, eight live SERP/PAA captures, owner ranking and backlink snapshots.
- FAQ questions are exact relevant PAA questions captured on 27 July 2026. Irrelevant mortality and fertility questions returned by the SERP were excluded rather than rewritten.
- Health and first-aid guidance is constrained to primary CDC and WHO sources. No incident rate, medical diagnosis or animal-identification claim was invented.

## Design and conversion decisions

### Phuket vs Koh Samui

- New premium comparison template with an original split-coast hero asset.
- Fast verdict, side-by-side trade-off table, weather decision bands, traveller profiles and dotted cross-coast route panel.
- Static flight and hotel prices from the legacy article are removed from the visible owner.
- Trip.com, Klook and 12Go buttons use “check current” language and sponsored attributes.

### Wildlife safety

- New premium safety template with an original calm documentary hero; no attack, gore or fear-based framing.
- Risk is organised by realistic traveller exposure: mosquitoes, bites and scratches, snakes and marine conditions.
- Emergency guidance separates animal bites, suspected snakebite, marine stings and fever.
- Three functional Amazon OneLink products are limited to ordinary prevention-supporting travel gear. The disclosure explicitly says they do not prevent or treat disease.

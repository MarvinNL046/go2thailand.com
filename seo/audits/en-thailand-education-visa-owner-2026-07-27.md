## Search intent and information gain

The SERPs mix generic application guides, school sales pages and questions about cost, duration, extension and work. The redesigned owner does not repeat unverified school packages. Its information-gain layer is a route selector that separates:

1. primary or secondary school;
2. university or college, including the ED Plus boundary;
3. Thai or English short-course study;
4. a curricular internship.

Muay Thai is treated separately because the current Royal Thai Embassy London pages explicitly place it under DTV2 soft-power activity, while the ED short-course section names Thai and English language study. The page tells readers to verify the current category and provider evidence before paying.

## Claims corrected from the legacy owner

- Removed fixed school/course package prices.
- Removed named-school recommendations that had no current independent verification.
- Removed absolute lesson-hour and attendance claims.
- Removed any implication that an extension or one-year stay is guaranteed.
- Separated the 90-day visa-use window from the stay of up to 90 days after entry.
- Explained that standard ED is single-entry and that re-entry and extension require separate treatment.
- Explained that ED status is a study route, not general permission to work.

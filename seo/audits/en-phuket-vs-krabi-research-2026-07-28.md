# Phuket vs Krabi — English research brief

**Candidate owner:** `/compare/phuket-vs-krabi/`  
**Market:** United Kingdom / English  
**Captured:** 28 July 2026

## Search demand and route evidence

- The independent DFS cluster returned 41 keyword records and 50 competitor domains.
- `krabi vs phuket` and `krabi thailand vs phuket` each carry 720 UK volume with KD 0; `phuket vs krabi` carries 140 with KD 0. Family, honeymoon, nightlife, cost, first-time and three-destination variants form the decision long tail.
- Ten current UK-English SERPs returned 71 organic results and 57 People Also Ask appearances across 46 distinct questions.
- Exact-route ranking research returned zero keywords. The backlink summary returned no backlink or referring-domain signal. The current generic/noindex English output has no measurable equity that should block a purpose-built owner.

## Competitor and source findings

- Eight usable full parses cover Thailand Starts Here, On The Go Tours, Little Passport Crew, Postcards by Hannah, The Lavender Travelers, Helena Bradbury and official Tourism Authority of Thailand province pages for Phuket and Krabi.
- DFS also returned Reddit and Tripadvisor discussions. They help expose traveller vocabulary and decision friction, but do not carry mutable price, schedule, safety or operator claims.
- Competitors repeatedly compare beaches, scenery, nightlife, ease, family fit, cost and day trips. Many flatten entire provinces into one score and ignore the fact that Patong is not all of Phuket, while Railay, Ao Nang and Krabi Town create very different Krabi stays.
- The strongest information gain is a consequence-led matrix: each advantage must show its trade-off, and profiles must choose a base and daily rhythm rather than award an abstract universal winner.
- The combine intent is material. The owner should explain when eight to ten nights make a two-base Phuket-and-Krabi route more honest than forcing one winner, without inventing a fixed ferry duration or price.

## Owner intent and boundaries

The page owns one decision: should an English-speaking traveller choose Phuket, choose Krabi, or combine them for this trip?

- Keep destination depth on `/city/phuket/` and `/city/krabi/`.
- Keep neighbourhood selection on the where-to-stay and hotel owners.
- Keep monthly climate specifics on each weather owner; both destinations are on the Andaman side, but no month guarantees a calm sea day.
- Keep excursion detail on the attraction owners and live Klook inventory.
- Keep current hotel availability on Trip.com and transfer/operator details on 12Go.
- Amazon was assessed but is not forced into the comparison. A physical product cannot improve the Phuket-versus-Krabi destination decision; relevant OneLink products belong on packing, weather, island-activity and practical owners.

## Required page model

- Reuse the premium visual comparison system while making all content, schema, disclosures and sub-IDs locale-aware.
- A one-minute verdict for scenery/pace, choice/ease, nightlife and limestone/island feel.
- A six-row consequence matrix rather than a fabricated weighted score or crowd-sourced rating.
- Four traveller profiles, two realistic day rhythms and a clear combine route.
- At least ten genuine English PAA answers covering beauty, beaches, cost uncertainty, families, couples, first visit, trip length, Phi Phi departure logic, combining and transfer planning.
- Official TAT source context plus transparent competitor methodology.
- Current-check CTAs only: Trip.com for accommodation, Klook for excursions and 12Go for transport. No fixed hotel, tour or transfer price.
- `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage` and `WebPage` schema with exact EN/NL/x-default ownership; no fake `AggregateRating`, `Review`, `Offer` or universal “best” claim.
- Desktop and mobile browser QA, internal-link audit, canonical/hreflang proof and full layered sitewide verification before sitemap promotion.

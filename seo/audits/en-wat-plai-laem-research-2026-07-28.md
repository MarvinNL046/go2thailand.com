- Six usable full parses cover Wikipedia's referenced site summary, Thailand Magazine, Thailandee, Chris and Wren's World, KohPlanner and Lub d Koh Samui.

- Published opening-hour ranges conflict or may change. The page must avoid presenting a scraped timetable, donation amount, fish-food price or transport duration as permanent fact.

- Keep the Big Buddha entity as a related stop rather than absorbing its full history or search cluster.
- Hand broad island prioritisation to `/city/koh-samui/attractions/` and destination planning to `/city/koh-samui/`.
- Hand weather and sea-day choices to `/city/koh-samui/weather/`.

- Amazon was assessed but is not forced onto this cultural attraction owner. A generic product does not improve the core temple-visit decision; natural OneLink placements remain on English packing, weather, connectivity, food and equipment owners.
